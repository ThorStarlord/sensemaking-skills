# Project 004: Freelance Marketplace for Specialized Services

## Raw Fog (Problem Description)

Specialized service providers (designers, copywriters, growth marketers) struggle with:
- Competition from generalist freelance platforms (Upwork) focusing on race-to-bottom pricing
- No way to build sustainable client relationships or recurring work
- Time spent finding clients eats into service delivery
- Hard to demonstrate specialization and get premium rates
- Clients can't find providers matching their specific niche

They need a platform that:
- Connects them with clients who value specialization (not lowest price)
- Enables retainer and project-based work equally
- Provides proposal templates and contract management
- Offers growth tools (portfolio showcase, case studies, testimonials)
- Takes smaller commission (10% vs Upwork's 20%)
- Focuses on long-term relationships, not transactional gigs

## Target Market

- Freelancers with 3+ years experience in their niche
- Those earning $75K+/year and wanting $150K+
- Consultants transitioning to independent work
- Agencies needing overflow capacity

## Success Metrics (Initial Hypotheses)

- Average contract value $5K+ (vs $500 on generalist platforms)
- 40% of work is recurring/retainer vs. one-off projects
- Provider earn $50/hour or more (vs $30/hour average)
- 90% of client-provider matches result in completed work
