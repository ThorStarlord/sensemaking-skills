# Project 005: AI Fitness Coach Mobile App

## Raw Fog (Problem Description)

Fitness app users face:
- Generic workout plans that don't adapt to progress
- No real accountability or motivation mechanism
- Hard to stay consistent (app fatigue after 2-3 weeks)
- Limited access to expert guidance without paying for personal trainers
- Progress tracking is fragmented (various apps and devices)

They need an app that:
- Adapts workouts daily based on performance, recovery, and mood
- Provides AI coaching that responds to form feedback and encourages without being annoying
- Integrates with wearables to understand actual recovery/sleep
- Gamifies consistency with streaks and weekly challenges
- Allows opt-in peer groups for accountability
- Costs $15-20/month (vs $200/month for personal trainers)

## Target Market

- Fitness enthusiasts (not casual - those with 3+ month commitment history)
- Ages 25-45, tech-comfortable
- Those wanting personal training results at 1/10th the cost
- People with home gyms or access to gyms but no coach

## Success Metrics (Initial Hypotheses)

- 70% of users maintain app usage after 12 weeks (vs 30% industry average)
- Users improve workout performance metrics (reps, weight, endurance) by 20%+
- 60% of users recommend to friends (NPS > 50)
- Retention rate Year 1 to Year 2 is >50%
- Churn rate <5% per month after 3-month mark
