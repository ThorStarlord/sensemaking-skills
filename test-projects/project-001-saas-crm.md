# Project 001: AI-Powered CRM for Service Businesses

## Raw Fog (Problem Description)

Service businesses (plumbers, electricians, HVAC companies) struggle with:
- Manual scheduling and job tracking spread across email, SMS, phone calls
- Customer communication fragmentation (no unified view of all interactions)
- Poor visibility into technician availability and location
- Lost opportunities due to slow quote turnaround (24-48 hours typical)
- No proactive maintenance scheduling for repeat customers

They need a simple, mobile-first CRM that:
- Consolidates customer communications (email, SMS, form submissions)
- Automates job scheduling with technician assignment
- Uses AI to auto-draft quotes and service recommendations
- Provides real-time job status updates to customers
- Reduces quote time to under 2 hours

## Target Market

Service businesses with:
- 3-15 technicians
- $500K-$5M annual revenue
- Heavy mobile workforce
- Currently using spreadsheets or paper for job tracking

## Success Metrics (Initial Hypotheses)

- Reduce quote turnaround from 24h to 2h
- Increase customer satisfaction scores by 25%
- Enable 20% more jobs per technician per day
- Reduce time spent on admin by 30%
