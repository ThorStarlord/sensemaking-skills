# Developmental Trajectory Reasoning (DTR) v1

**Role:** first-class intellectual capability within the existing using-sensemaking controller and Long-Horizon Adaptive Strategic Development. DTR discovers, understands, evaluates, cultivates and adapts possible developmental trajectories through which present actions transform the conditions for future value creation. It is not a planner, roadmap, separate ontology runtime, fixed workflow or permission grant. Its [explicit conceptual ontology](../../../docs/semantic-architecture/developmental-trajectory-reasoning-ontology.md) defines the semantic roles and relationship boundaries within the existing layered ontology; this portable method remains usable without the framework docs.

## When it changes a decision

Use DTR when future capability states, the route toward them, a shared enabler, path dependence, an opportunity cost, changed evidence, or the fate of an unselected direction could change a consequential present choice. Skip for narrow, adequately grounded direct ACT or STOP. AMS owns when to widen or narrow the horizon, deepen, challenge, construct, or stop. The [integrated lifecycle](long-horizon-strategic-development-v1.md) remains the entry/re-entry contract.

A developmental landscape is the decision-relevant view of possible and actual states, paths, enabling conditions and their changing relationships. It is not a separate persistent entity. The selected trajectory is only one part of it. Prior possibilities remain defeasible, not instructions.

## Reason from both ends and across paths

Use the smallest useful combination of the following intellectual moves (not phases):

| Move | Question that could change a choice |
| --- | --- |
| Forward | What new valuable states become plausible from current capabilities and a proposed transition? What direct and systemic effects could follow? |
| Backward | From a desired beneficiary outcome, what must be true? Which conditions are actually necessary, merely supporting, substitutable, or unknown? |
| Alternative routes | Could a cheaper, reversible, independent or differently sequenced route reach the same outcome? Compare its real constraints. |
| Branch and recombine | Which commitments create incompatible branches, complementary capabilities or shared enabling conditions? Could a previously deferred opportunity become viable? |
| Path dependence and optionality | What will the commitment make easier, harder, unavailable, or expensive to undo? What is the cost of keeping an option open? |
| Temporal tradeoff | What present value, information or future value would each viable action create, and what would it delay? Avoid invented certainty or universal scalar scores. |
| Adapt and learn | What actually changed after construction? Which selected and unselected futures, assumptions and methods merit reconsideration? |

A state is a capability/outcome condition, not a date or ticket. A transition is a plausible mechanism for changing that state, not a promised implementation. Distinguish established capabilities from hypothesized enabling mechanisms and actual commitments. Forward reasoning is not forecasting; backcasting is not evidence that the target or assumed prerequisites are achievable. Avoid unsupported long chains: expose load-bearing premises, contrary branches, and the nearest useful discriminator. Do not regard common prerequisites as proof of complementarity.

Apply [Construction Path Synthesis](../../strategic-repository-analysis/references/construction-path-synthesis-v1.md), [Connected Prospective Inquiry](../../strategic-repository-analysis/references/connected-prospective-inquiry-v1.md), [Possibility Assessment](../../strategic-repository-analysis/references/possibility-assessment-v1.md), [Convergent Direction Selection](../../strategic-repository-analysis/references/convergent-direction-selection-v1.md) and [Premise-Sensitive Reconsideration](../../strategic-repository-analysis/references/premise-sensitive-reconsideration-v1.md) where their distinct responsibilities are warranted. Divergence and architectural reconciliation inform each other. Preserve genuine nonselection, deferral and the no-new-mechanism alternative.

## Retain a useful landscape with existing surfaces

| Existing surface | DTR producer and later consumer |
| --- | --- |
| Governing intent and reviewed capabilities | Recover actual goal and source-grounded current state; verify original evidence, capabilities and missing coverage. |
| Existing strategic_repository_analysis | Represent a bounded set of coherent competing paths, current capability map, future_state, why_plausible, builds_on/required capability IDs, dependencies, unlocks, risks, assumptions, reassessment_triggers and optional path_transitions. For historical v1 use its actual fields. |
| Reviewed ProductPossibility, ArchitectureOption, ConsequenceHypothesis and their sources | Retain only enduring meaningful futures, structural alternatives and conditional effects with existing kinds/predicates and epistemic status. Use authored disposition history and revisit_when for consequential deferred possibilities. |
| Existing strategic_reconciliation or dated decision/analysis records | Explain changed/unchanged premises and judgments, effects on selected and unselected paths, new alternatives and continuity; preserve the original analysis and local path identifiers. |
| Session/query receipts and SCL | Point to actual source/decision records and separately retain attributable method experience only when actual experience warrants it. A receipt is not a landscape database. |

For a consequential comparison, record in the appropriate existing artifact: the source revision and intent; outcome and current capability state; hypothesized transition and required/supporting conditions; competing or complementary routes and shared enablers; load-bearing assumptions and what would reverse the preference; chosen/deferred disposition and rationale, authority, and conditions for revisiting. Name only relationships supported by their sources and separate hypothesized consequences. A bare reference is not a causal edge. An unselected path can remain in the dated analysis; a reused product future may justify a reviewed opportunity claim. Use existing Steward RECALL and RECONCILE with explicit knowledge-edit authority; do not retain every temporary scenario. A path ID is scoped to its original analysis, not automatically a global identity.

## Recover and inspect

Start with the actual question, current revision, earlier analysis and latest valid reconciliation. Use source-only reading if the CLI is absent. If available, existing commands provide candidate context:
- `sensemaking-skills semantic workspace-query --repo TARGET --ref SHA --operation product-outline --include-candidates --json` for authored goals and candidate futures.
- `sensemaking-skills semantic workspace-query --repo TARGET --ref SHA --operation possibility-ecology --subject GOAL_OR_POSS_ID --json` for bounded candidate relations; inspect original records.
- `sensemaking-skills strategy paths --artifact ANALYSIS --json --include-trajectory-context` for declared path context, prerequisites, transitions, unlocks, and shared typed capability/assumption references within one analysis; `strategy assumptions` for declared assumptions. Shared references are candidates for investigation, not proven shared enablers. `strategy graph` keeps source-local path/assumption/transition identities separate and discloses unresolved prior-analysis references.
- `sensemaking-skills strategy compare --before OLD --after NEW --json --include-trajectory-context` exposes literal context changes for matching local IDs. Check continuity and actual semantic correspondence independently; the tool does not match renamed paths or infer causality.
- `sensemaking-skills semantic workspace-changes --repo TARGET --base-ref OLD --ref NEW --json` and the existing opt-in possibility reconsideration surface can locate evidence changes. Inspect command help for the supported option in the available version. Structured reference changes are prompts, not truth of the natural-language condition.

These commands project authored facts and candidates only. Source gaps, missing fields, omitted results, untracked work and old IDs limit coverage. Do not treat an empty result as absence. Re-read original artifacts and sources when material.

## Cumulative developmental reasoning across actual episodes

The developmental landscape is the object of continuity, not a queue of
tasks. Recover prior selected and unselected futures and their original
conditions before a consequential development choice. Distinguish five
complementary intellectual contributions, using only those that can change
the decision:

- **Continuity:** reconstruct earlier state, route and rationale; inspect
  original source before carrying a claim forward.
- **Causality:** compare a proposed enabling mechanism, actual precondition,
  supporting or substitutable condition, and counterexample. A changed
  source, observed association or declared prerequisite is not causal proof.
- **Emergence:** compare the previous and current capability landscape.
  Ask what new beneficiary outcomes become plausible, whether earlier
  opportunities can recombine, and what became less useful or unavailable.
  A newly authored opportunity is not evidence of newly feasible value.
- **Optionality:** compare immediate retained value, future options created
  or foreclosed, cost of preserving them and reversible alternatives.
  Do not treat every shared enabler as strategically superior.
- **Reciprocal development:** compare what the product gained with what
  the agent learned about its method. Recover a relevant prior SCL lesson,
  check conditions and counterevidence, apply/adapt/reject if useful, and
  retain only actual attributable experience.

For a consequential return, the optional
`semantic workspace-changes --base-ref OLD --ref NEW --json --include-possibility-evolution`
consumer compares authored opportunities, direct relationships and linked
claims across exact snapshots before display truncation. Use its before/after
records and existing fingerprint classification to focus source reading.
Without the CLI, inspect the original Git-authored packs at both revisions.
Combine this with actual changes, existing revisit conditions, source-scoped
strategy history, and conditional consequence reasoning. Mechanically added
or changed records are not new opportunities or proven effects. A later
agent can reaffirm, revise, defer, reject or discover a candidate; preserve
the rationale and consider the lightest existing persistence surface.

These contributions are not stages, required artifacts, scores, a new
controller or a separate cognitive subsystem. Select valuable work anywhere
within the beneficiary's authorized product landscape, including work with
no DTR-specific code change. A single source-grounded continuation is not
independent longitudinal improvement or cross-domain transfer.

## Construct and return consequential evidence

After authorized real work, distinguish the actual outcome from expected transition and possible downstream effects. Ask:
- Which prerequisite, constraint, or assumption is now supported, weakened, disproven or unassessed?
- What did the change unlock, constrain or make more expensive, including for a previously unselected direction? Which alternative remains valid? Which newly plausible opportunity warrants investigation or retention?
- Is there a better present responsibility now? Does an earlier selection remain warranted, need revision, or require reopening? Does the change only affect a local path?

Use the existing learning/reconciliation and strategic reconciliation contracts. Preserve unaffected judgments, identify changed evidence with source revision, retain a material future reconsideration trigger, and record a justified NO_MODEL_CHANGE when nothing reusable changed. Do not automatically reopen Level 3, rank paths, pursue deferred ideas or assert an observed effect from a hypothetical model. Reopen only when the earlier frame is materially invalid or direction genuinely becomes open. An unselected possibility is not authorized work.

AMS can select a forward pass, backcast, challenge a necessary-condition claim, run a useful reversible build, or stop. SCL can recover prior method experience, check contextual applicability/counterevidence, then adopt, adapt or reject before a decision; use actual feedback rather than declaring a lesson from hypothetical examples. Domain and agent-level experience remain separate. Continuous Construction is the Probe, not permission to turn every decision into an experiment.

## Adequate stop

Stop trajectory analysis when the current consequential choice is sufficiently warranted, the key premise needs actual construction/evidence, marginal exploration is not worth its cost, or a protected authority boundary applies. Continue the authorized construction if warranted; no indefinite speculative expansion. An adequate result may be direct construction, defer, nonselection, reaffirmation, or a conditional inquiry. A real later beneficiary episode must separately establish retrieval, application, decision contribution, and eventual comparative benefit. A new reference, projection, or illustrative example does not do so.
