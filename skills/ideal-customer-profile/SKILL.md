# Ideal customer profile

Satisfy a `customer_understanding` responsibility by producing `ideal_customer_profile`.

## Inputs

Require product/service, market/segment scope, and an inventory of available customer evidence. Valuable evidence includes retention/cohort, usage, churn, win-loss, revenue/LTV, CAC, time-to-value, PMF survey, interviews, NPS/CSAT, support, and buying-process evidence.

## Procedure

1. Read [references/contract.md](references/contract.md).
2. Inventory available evidence and define the assessment window/segment.
3. Identify high-value patterns only from supplied evidence; otherwise keep them as hypotheses.
4. Describe company/person/decision profile, behaviors, Jobs to Be Done, needs/pains, ideal-of-ideal indicators, and disqualifiers with claim-level evidence status.
5. Distinguish buyer, champion, and user roles when evidence warrants them; do not invent titles or committees.
6. Treat LTV, churn, CAC, time-to-value, NPS, revenue, deal size, WTP, purchase cycle, and product-usage statistics as observed only with durable evidence.
7. Derive GTM implications as recommendations/hypotheses, not externally validated channel or messaging truth.
8. Return `ideal_customer_profile` and control.

## Boundary

`ICP hypothesis != validated segment`, `high-value pattern != causal explanation`, `disqualifier recommendation != sales policy`, and `GTM implication != external execution authority`.
