# Divergent Exploration: Questions, Frames and Expressions v1

**Use:** a bounded divergent inquiry where question/frame changes, meaningful
candidate differences or durable continuation matter. Load this reference
directly under the active `using-sensemaking` controller. It does not invoke
the parent Skill's full Level-3 strategic analysis.

## Contents

1. Preserve the meaning that could change the inquiry
2. Explore and distinguish
3. Retain a readable result
4. Continue or stop
5. Selective knowledge retention

## 1. Preserve the meaning that could change the inquiry

| Meaning | Use it to distinguish |
| --- | --- |
| **ExplorationInquiry** | The bounded activity from its sessions and resulting documents |
| **InquiryQuestion** | What is being asked, including a new question that is not yet a proposed future |
| **ExplorationFrame** | Beneficiary, desired end, context, horizon, assumptions and constraints; unknown dimensions can stay unknown |
| **ExplorationLens** | A perspective actually used, rather than a required pass |
| **CandidateExpression** | What was expressed at this point: question, tension, fragment, outcome or mechanism; a durable possibility identity is optional |
| **TransformationAccount** | An attributed explanation connecting inputs, a conceptual change and an expression; it does not reveal an internal cognitive trace |
| **PossibilityLandscape** | This bounded view of expressions and asserted relationships under an identified inquiry/frame |
| **ExploratoryJudgment** | A sourced, attributed assessment of difference, novelty, overlap, grouping or an inquiry limit |

An activity, its artifact, an expression and an enduring possibility have
different identities. Keep the original expression when a later interpretation
or known `poss:`/`arch:` referent is recorded. A question can remain useful
before its beneficiary, outcome or mechanism is known.

## 2. Explore and distinguish

Begin with the actual question, target, authority and useful source boundary.
Recover governing intent and decisive source facts. Treat observed
implementation choices as revisable where authority permits; distinguish them
from governing constraints. If a beneficiary or end is unsettled, make that
uncertainty part of the inquiry.

**Lightweight inquiry orientation.** Before substantial exploration, distinguish
(1) invoked reasoning method from the actual target, (2) owner's governing
product intent from this inquiry's tentative end, (3) the open intellectual
question from an implementation task, and (4) governing constraints from
revisable assumptions or observed implementation choices. Identify the
relevant *source relationships* as well as individual facts. Do not invent a
known beneficiary or user demand when it is the object of discovery.

**Global-local coherence.** A source-accurate local improvement is not
necessarily a meaningful answer to the broader inquiry. When expressions
repeat one subsystem's mechanism, ask whether a different beneficiary,
desired experience, premise, time horizon or cross-system interaction would
change the *question or outcome* rather than merely refine the solution.
Move back to a local mechanism when the wider end is settled or this is the
user's intended scope. Global perspective does not demand a repository-wide
inventory or permit silent product-thesis change.

Use the model when it changes what you notice:

- Repeated wording changes with the same end suggest restatement; ask which
  meaningful dimension remains unexplored.
- A different beneficiary, desired end, premise or observation condition can
  change the question itself. Retain that change and its predecessor.
- Similar mechanisms can support different experiences. Explain the
  consequential difference before grouping expressions.
- A missing field in a fragment is an open question. Develop it when useful;
  preserve it as incomplete when supplying the answer would invent intent.
- An apparent duplicate needs operands and a comparison basis. Preserve both
  source expressions even when a later view groups them.

Choose a lens or [creative operation](creative-prospective-discovery-v1.md)
only when it may uncover a meaningful difference. The concepts are not a
sequence of mandatory passes. In an intentionally independent first pass,
honor the exposure boundary before consulting earlier answers; record what
prior material was actually available.

For a novelty or overlap judgment, identify the compared expressions,
dimension, inspected corpus/revision, assessor and uncertainty. Separate new
wording, a changed realization, a different end and a changed question.
An unexamined dimension is NOT_ASSESSED. Retrieval alone establishes neither
originality nor absence of an idea.

**From knowledge to expression:** where the change is material, make legible
which source-grounded decision, capability, tension, changed premise or
relationship informed the expression, what conceptual difference is proposed,
and how it differs from the nearest known outcome. This is an *attributed
TransformationAccount*, not an internal thought trace or evidence of causal
creativity. An expression can remain a question or incomplete fragment.

**Reflective regulation:** repeated local variants, one unexamined premise or
premature selection may justify another lens, an assumption challenge, a
restart from a different frame, or a return to the product-level question.
Consult the existing Metareasoning/Exploration policies when allocating the
next reasoning move is genuinely consequential; do not invoke them after
each candidate. A strong local focus may be correct when the inquiry is
intentionally narrow. A justified no-new-expression result is valid.

## 3. Retain a readable result

Make recoverable: the question and target; source scope/revision; substantive
result, including a justified no-new-expression result; material uncertainty
and authority limits; and why the inquiry stops or recommends continuation.
Use compact prose when it carries that meaning.

For a substantial retained inquiry, the optional
[record template](divergent-exploration-record-template.md) organizes inquiry,
sources, frames/lenses, expressions, judgments, open territory and completion.
Add detail only where a later reader would otherwise lose a distinction.
Shared context can be declared once; expression-specific differences are local.

For cross-referenced entries, use stable headings such as `### C1` with a
separate descriptive label. Qualify C/Q/F/T/J identifiers by their document.
Resolve original table labels by source path plus label; create no fictitious
anchor. Keep the source-file revision separate from the older target revision
reported inside a source. A new expression/version records a changed meaning;
it does not overwrite the earlier expression or its disposition.

A transformation account identifies its inputs, claimed change, outputs and
author/source. Keep it attributed and proportionate. Retain the explanation a
reader needs, not private scratch reasoning or a claim of verified cognitive
causation.

Use the target's authorized artifact location. If it has no convention,
`docs/explorations/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md` is a reasonable retained location.
A read-only inquiry can finish in the response. Existing session receipts may
point to saved records through their existing repository-relative
`artifact_refs`; saving a record creates no additional authority.

## 4. Continue or stop

End standalone divergence with a useful bounded landscape, material unknowns
and a justified stop or recommendation. Consider another lens when it could
change a question or material candidate; repeated cosmetic variants can
justify stopping. State what was inspected and what remains unexamined.

**Calibrate the work, not the label.** Distinguish (a) orientation fidelity:
did the inquiry use the requested target, scope, sources, and divergent
operation? from (b) generative effectiveness: do the resulting expressions
show materially different, source-grounded conceptual work? Correctly
reciting the mode proves neither. On authentic use, inspect relevant
source use, substantive transformation, global-local coherence, appropriate
change/stop, and epistemic limits. One successful artifact does not prove
independent repeatability or benefit caused by this guidance.

Leave coverage assessment, consolidation, comparative ranking, depth,
strategic adoption and implementation to separately warranted responsibilities.
Supply usable source/identity pointers for such a handoff without running it
implicitly. A landscape is not an exhaustive possibility universe or a
construction backlog.

## 5. Selective knowledge retention

The narrative is the record of origin. When durable knowledge is useful and
authorized, follow the existing
[Steward knowledge contract](../../repository-semantic-steward/references/knowledge-contract.md).
A source record can make an exploration discoverable; definitions can be
domain concepts; selected hypotheses and coherent prospective entities can use
their supported kinds. A fragment need not become an opportunity.

Keep source facts, hypotheses, interpretations, preferences and authority
distinct. Existing `references` plus an attributed conceptual-relation
attribute can preserve meanings with no exact predicate; those attribute
semantics remain unchecked. A checked file establishes structural eligibility,
not truth, value or creative benefit.

This guide works without a framework checkout, index or CLI. Each target
supplies its own sources and authority; the framework's example claims do not
transfer as that target's knowledge. Optional canonical provenance:
[Divergent Exploration Ontology](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/sensemaking-skills/blob/main/docs/semantic-architecture/divergent-exploration-ontology.md).
