# Creative Prospective Discovery with Retained Knowledge v1

**Role:** optional agent-facing discovery method, not a new Skill, graph schema, runtime stage, deterministic planner or scoring system.
**Owner:** active `using-sensemaking` agent; Strategic Exploration Funnel owns product breadth; Steward architecture exploration owns structural options and conditional consequences.

## Activation and grounding

Use when the user explicitly requests new product/architecture possibilities or when the known possibility space is materially too narrow. Ordinary bounded edits do not activate it. An exploration-only request produces a landscape, never automatic strategy, task selection or implementation authority.

State the **inquiry question and known desired outcome** before consulting the graph. If the beneficiary or end is unsettled, retain that uncertainty as part of the question. Distinguish **capability novelty** (a new valuable product behavior) from **architecture novelty** (a new mechanism for a known outcome). Recover source-verified goals, constraints, capabilities, decisions, declined alternatives, `poss:`, `arch:` and `conseq:` records as relevant. Selective existing queries: `search --term poss:` / `search --term arch:`; `explain`, `why`, `context-for`, and `contradictions`; original sources and revision-bound status remain decisive. With no index, inspect reviewed JSON and originals. Missing links, `NOT_ASSESSED` and truncation never prove absence.

**Anti-anchoring:** the graph provides *seeds*, not the full allowable option set. Seek important unrepresented outcomes; a past rejection is a reason to inspect changing premises, not an eternal veto.

For meaningful question/frame changes, uncertain candidate identity or durable
continuation, use the [divergent exploration profile](divergent-exploration-v1.md).
Retain emerging questions and fragments before applying the material-candidate
comparison below. Distinguish the source expression, a later interpretation
and any enduring possibility identity; the profile supplies a flexible record,
not another required creative pass.

## Creative operations (optional, not eight mandatory passes)

| Operation | Productive question | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recombination | What worthwhile outcome emerges from interacting existing capabilities? | A conceptual link is not an observed implementation dependency. |
| Assumption inversion | What materially different system could exist if a revisable premise changed? | Preserve governing non-goals/owner authority and label counterfactuals. |
| Capability extrapolation | What adjacent user benefit could existing leverage enable? | Identify the new user outcome, not just a task to implement. |
| Contradiction synthesis | Can an alternative combine complementary strengths of competing approaches? | Avoid an unbounded union inheriting both failure modes. |
| Temporal revival | What rejected possibility becomes worth reconsidering after a concrete premise changes? | Preserve rejection history and evidence. |
| Consequence transformation | If a hypothesized effect occurred, what further ability might become possible? | A may produce B, B may enable C, does not prove A produces C. |
| Negative-space search | What important outcome is missing from both current system and recorded possibilities? | Incomplete knowledge is `NOT_ASSESSED`, not proof of nonexistence. |
| Analogical transfer | Could a mechanism from another explicitly selected domain work here? | Revalidate target sources and scope; never import foreign claims as facts. |

## Creative candidate discrimination

Once an expression supports a material candidate, compare it with the closest sourced `poss:` or `arch:` record and historical rationale when available: **RESTATED** (no distinct outcome/mechanism; cite the ID), **REVISED** (same future but its premise, scope or relation changed; keep its history), **NEW** (materially different outcome or structure, with a specific differentiator), or **NOT_ASSESSED** (novelty uncertain from bounded sources). Classification is semantic judgment, not a deterministic graph score. A fragment can remain unclassified, and a graph miss does not establish novelty.

For each material candidate, state the known desired user/product outcome; source/revision-verified present mechanisms; any supported transformation account; a proposed structural relationship when relevant; assumptions; expected value; credible counterevidence; whether a governing decision would need revision; and what future evidence could change the assessment. Leave missing meaning or provenance explicit. Preserve conditional consequences as hypothetical, and stop when additional variants are merely cosmetic.

## Entity and memory boundary

A product future uses `poss:`, a structural realization uses `arch:`, a conditional effect uses `conseq:`, and a construction path belongs to strategic analysis; these are not interchangeable. Retain only consequential new/revised records via Steward RECONCILE and existing knowledge contract, with provenance, predecessor and `revisit_when` when meaningful. Use an existing `architectural_synthesis` for durable architecture comparisons; exploration-only without write authority stays in the report. A provisional preference is not selection or authorization.

## Evidence ceiling

Separate **retrieved**, **source-checked**, **materially used in generating/revising an option**, **genuinely new compared to the inspected landscape**, and **reused in an independent later session**. A same-context source review or mechanical CI query cannot establish independent-agent creative benefit. Use normal authentic work when available; no mandatory synthetic experiment or second controller.

**Exit:** a useful, source-scoped possibility landscape with meaningful alternatives, novelty distinctions, material unknowns and a warranted stop; no idea quotas, automatic ranking or forced construction.
