# Possibility Assessment: Coverage, Consolidation, Comparison and Portfolio v1

**Use:** when the active agent has a separately warranted request to assess coverage/marginal returns, reconcile distinct possibilities, compare alternatives or retain a qualified inquiry portfolio. This portable reference is reachable from existing `using-sensemaking` / strategic references. It does **not** start a full Level-3 strategy process, advance to implementation, or introduce a mandatory funnel.

## Contents

1. Establish the purpose and units of assessment
2. A shared source-bound judgment
3. Coverage and diminishing returns
4. Consolidation and original identities
5. Comparative evaluation and the three priority frames
6. Portfolio and historical revision
7. Record, stop and selectively retain

## 1. Establish purpose and units

Read the user's actual question, target repository's governing intent/authority, current sources and relevant prior assessments. Select **one warranted assessment responsibility** before selecting a document form. A standalone coverage or portfolio request may stop after answering it; completing one stage never grants authority to run the next.

Keep the **activity**, its **report**, the **source expression**, the **enduring possibility** and the **judgment** distinct. A proposed investigation is a *question plus the means of resolving it*, not automatically a product goal. Use a partial frame rather than invent beneficiary, expected value, evidence or permission.

When prior work exists, identify the **report-file revision** separately from the **source revision that report inspected**. Check original evidence when a distinction would change the answer. Source-linked history informs but does not dictate present preference.

**When explanations are the targets:** a source-qualified explanatory `Hypothesis` can be the original typed operand of a *diagnostic evidence-adequacy* assessment. Keep its common source-observed explanandum, proposed mechanism, source-specific support/counterevidence, assumptions and unresolved cause separate from an expression or future it may inspire. This is an authored `Claim`/`AssessmentJudgment` about an explanation, **not** an executable change to `ComparativeJudgment evaluates` endpoints. Do not mix its evidential fit with the novelty/desirability of a `CandidateExpression` or `ProductPossibility`; a hypothesis count does not measure breadth of product ends. A proposed discriminating observation answers *what would tell explanations apart*; a `ProposedInquiryApproach` additionally answers *how and at what evidence/access cost to observe it*. Neither means the check was executed. Preserve independent historical explanation and assessment revisions if either changes. Use this only when the current question warrants it; pure possibility assessment needs no abductive prepass.

## 2. Shared judgment envelope

Preserve these meanings where consequential: assessment question and purpose; source corpus and revisions; typed target identity; beneficiary/end/time/authority frame; evaluated criterion; assessor/date; stated grounds and counterevidence; uncertainty and unexamined areas; conditional conclusion; reconsideration conditions; and reason to stop.

Reuse common context once; attach local differences only where needed. Unknown/contested information stays unknown/contested. Do not write private scratch or claim an attributed explanation proves a hidden cognitive cause. Read the existing [optional record template](possibility-assessment-record-template.md) when a substantial report needs stable local references; compact prose is legitimate.

An **AssessmentJudgment** is an attributed documentary claim, not an executable predicate or an authorization. The target keeps its original `DE-`/`NX-`/`RP-`/`GD-`/`poss:`/`arch:` identity where available. Similarity and preference are criteria-specific.

## 3. Coverage and diminishing returns

Assess, separately:

- **Declared corpus coverage:** which sources/expressions/systems were actually inspected.
- **Conceptual breadth:** materially different ends, beneficiaries, premises, mechanisms and frames.
- **Consequence depth:** important direct/indirect/systemic and adverse branches actually reasoned through.
- **Empirical support:** observations, hypotheses, counterevidence and absent sources.
- **Perspective coverage:** independent viewpoints, vocabulary differences and source-access boundaries; multiple named lenses do not prove independent framing.

A coverage result may be sufficient **for a named purpose** (e.g., a provisional convergence comparison) while leaving significant unexplored territory. `NOT_ASSESSED` means the dimension or comparison was not examined, not that it is globally absent. Counts of candidate names are inventories, not independent novelty, coverage quality or creative yield.

Keep *three diminishing-return claims* separate:

1. **Observed past repetition:** a justified comparison of prior results and their differences.
2. **Expected additional learning:** what a *specified new inquiry method*, source or perspective might contribute under stated conditions.
3. **Attention allocation:** why the proposed inquiry might deserve less/more effort than a **named alternative** now.

An extra generic essay may have low marginal value while a genuinely independent comparison of the same question has high prospective value if the required conditions exist. The latter is neither automatically feasible nor authorized. Stop when another pass is unlikely to change a consequential premise, interpretation or choice for the present responsibility; do not announce global saturation.

## 4. Possibility consolidation

Start with original source-qualified identities. Separate:

- **Near duplicate/restatement** for one comparison criterion.
- **Variant** that shares a goal/method but differs materially.
- **Navigational grouping** used for a stated explanatory purpose.
- **Complement** that could jointly serve an end (not an established causal dependency).
- **Contextual alternatives** competing under stated assumptions.
- **New synthesis** where a distinct proposed identity and retention value might warrant separate curation.

A group is a **view**, not an assertion that its members are identical. Preserve original expressions, attribution, source revisions, overlapping membership and disagreement. State what the view **abstracts away**. The equivalence criterion may differ across pairs; do not infer A=C merely because A resembles B in goal and B resembles C in method.

The source-based GD-D/DE-F2 case demonstrates near-duplicate *experiential method* without retiring either origin. Plural interpretations, conditional truth and different observation conditions remain distinguishable even though all involve multiple perspectives.

## 5. Comparative evaluation and priority

Name comparison targets and purpose; use qualitative consequences, premises, beneficiary value, feasible learning, uncertainty and alternative outcomes. Permissible results:

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Preferred** | Assessed and favored under the stated frame, with reasons/reversal conditions |
| **Tied** | Actually assessed as equally preferable on decisive dimensions |
| **Conditional** | Relative order depends on an identified unresolved premise |
| **Incomparable** | Assessment considered the shared frame and found no justified common basis |
| **Unresolved** | Shared basis exists but evidence or interpretation cannot support an order |
| **NOT_ASSESSED** | This comparison was not performed |
| **Outside frame** | Retained for broader navigation but outside the current comparable set |

Do not force all entries into one ordinal list. A pairwise preference is not automatically global; a missing rating is not a low rank or tie.

### Three distinct priority questions

| Priority | Compare this | Do not infer this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exploration** | Specific question **and proposed investigation approach**, considering expected consequential learning, repeated material, source/evidence access, dependencies, portfolio diversity and attention cost | That the subject's product future is best |
| **Strategic attractiveness** | Product future/direction under sufficient depth: beneficiary value, second-order/systemic consequences, assumptions, tradeoffs, owner fit and counterevidence | Accepted thesis, owner decision or implementation permission |
| **Implementation** | **Already authorized** concrete change/engineering responsibility under current repository dependencies, useful reversible construction, verification and resources | Authorization from a ranked suggestion |

A product direction can be strategically central while its particular next *conceptual inquiry* is evidence-dependent and presently low-value. Another method, when genuinely available, can change the *inquiry* priority without changing the product direction's identity or historical ranking.

The user may delegate repository-answerable qualitative ranking and bounded decisions; no implicit escalation of action authority follows. A comparison does not rewrite accepted ADRs, history or owner intent.

## 6. Portfolio and revision

A **PortfolioSnapshot** states its date, sources and assessed purpose, contains typed member identities, draws on cited judgments and displays only the ordering actually warranted. It may include ties, partial order, conditional relationships, unassessed items and parallel/outside-frame members.

Consider cross-entry **complementarity, shared uncertainty, evidence reuse, dependencies and substitution** only where those relationships could change the answer. A shared assumption alone does not establish benefit or causal dependence. A number is a navigation label, not a score.

On later review, create an **AssessmentRevisionAccount**: name the older snapshot, changed question/method/frame, new sources or alternatives, revised comparison and remaining uncertainty. Preserve the older assessment as historically scoped. An earlier pairwise result should not be rewritten as a global preference.

The source portfolio retains Exit and Public outside a common six-entry inquiry order; that does not put them last. Historical H/B/N rankings have different comparison units and source states; later changes do not erase earlier reasoning.

## 7. Record, stop and retain selectively

A substantial record can use [the optional Markdown profile](possibility-assessment-record-template.md); include only material sections. For target repos without a convention, `docs/explorations/YYYY-MM-DD-<subject>.md` is a reasonable durable location when authorized. A read-only analysis may stop in the reply. Existing session receipts can refer to retained reports but do not replace them.

After a bounded assessment, say **what was found**, **for what purpose**, **what was excluded/uncertain**, **why this activity stops**, and **what could justify a different inquiry or later revision**. Recommending another inquiry does not execute it. Do not silently continue into Depth Exploration, strategy adoption or implementation.

Where selectively reviewed semantic memory is useful, follow the target repository's **existing** knowledge contract. Stable concept definitions may be `domain_concept`; a published report may be a `source`; supported `references` can make them discoverable. Do not bulk-convert expression labels or rankings into `opportunity`/`decision` claims. `references` and `attributes.conceptual_relation` do not mechanically verify the relation's semantic truth.

This reference is self-contained: the target supplies its own governing sources, authority and history, and **does not need** the Sensemaking framework checkout, CLI, SQLite or reference-case repo. The [framework's conceptual ontology](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/sensemaking-skills/blob/main/docs/semantic-architecture/possibility-assessment-ontology.md) and [worked cases](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/sensemaking-skills/blob/main/docs/semantic-architecture/possibility-assessment-reference-cases-v1.md) are optional explanations, not target authority.
