## Independence from manifest/ranking content

These seeds are independent of manifest and ranking content by design —
they are cryptographic random bytes, not derived from any candidate or
manifest hash. Only their *use* is deferred: each seeds a Fisher-Yates
shuffle over the `(task, regime)` pairs at actual dispatch time, per
`AUTONOMOUS-TASK-V2-PROTOCOL-DRAFT.md:338-341`. That shuffle procedure is
not run by this construction session — it runs at a future dispatch
session, out of scope here.

## Freeze

These three values are final for this experiment. Do not regenerate on
later activation of Tranche 2 — activation controls *use* of the
Tranche-2 seed, not its *generation*, per the design package.
