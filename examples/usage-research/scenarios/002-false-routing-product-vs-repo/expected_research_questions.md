# Expected Research Questions (Scenario 002)

To pass this scenario, the `problem-framer` must identify that the user's "product" desire is actually a "workflow selection" problem.

1. **Object Under Pressure**: Should target `workflow-registry.yaml` or the workflow selection logic, NOT a product roadmap or PRD.
2. **Failure Mode**: Should identify "Premature Domain Skill Handoff" (e.g., trying to use PM skills before the workflow is defined).
3. **Routing**: Should route to `full-local-sensemaking` or a specific repository audit, not to product development workflows.
