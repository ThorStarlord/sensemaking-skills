## 1. The gap being addressed

Retained repository knowledge, an agent-facing reasoning method, and the act of selecting that method are distinct. A graph may accurately describe a repository, an agent may retrieve that graph, and yet the resulting reasoning may remain generic or narrowly optimized. The crosswalk identifies which *existing* meanings should inform task orientation, source-grounded transformation and reflective regulation; it does not promise to inspect or control a model's private cognitive process.

The user wants an **integrated reasoning system that can discover valuable possible product outcomes**, not only retrieve facts or optimize an already supplied engineering solution. During Divergent Exploration, a product end or beneficiary may itself be open; requiring it to be predetermined would beg the question.

| Responsibility | Questions it answers | Current semantic and methodological sources | Visible evidence of performance |

These are **interpretive responsibilities**, not new entity kinds, sequential phases, runtime states or checks to repeat after every idea.

## 3. Global–local coherence: return to the product question when it matters

Local findings are often valid. The failure occurs when improving a local mechanism substitutes for exploring the governing *purpose* of an inquiry in which alternative purposes or benefits remain open.

Use four conceptual levels:

1. **Product purpose and beneficiaries.** Recover accepted intent, but preserve unsettled ends as questions; a newly hypothesized benefit is not ratified owner preference.
2. **Cross-system relationships.** Examine relevant capabilities, constraints, decisions, historical revisions, dependencies, tensions and omissions. A graph miss is not evidence of product absence; a link alone is not causal proof.
3. **Local inquiry focus.** Investigate the particular mechanism, premise, experience or question that the current bounded activity actually asks about.
4. **Return and reframe.** When local optimization appears to be narrowing discovery, ask whether the local finding changes the beneficiary, desired end, central question or how the wider product is understood. Return to a local focus if the broader question is already settled or the task is bounded.

This is a **bidirectional movement**, not an instruction to inspect every repository subsystem. A local finding can change a product-level question; a product-level concern can re-interpret a local fact. Neither move self-authorizes Level-3 strategy or Level-4 thesis revision.

### Example — source-grounded and hypothetical

The [Atlas DE-A account](../normal-use/2026-11-04-de-a-atlas-divergent-exploration.md) starts with authored Guardian/Sigrid/Eira identity, supported/partly implemented Hearth capabilities and future buildcraft designs. A merely local optimization could propose another combat modifier. A global–local reconsideration asks whether those relations support *different kinds of player experience*: whose work makes heroism possible, what the hero learns to notice, or how ordinary-life competence remains recognizable across crisis and peace. Those are **source-grounded proposed experiences**, not implemented gameplay or independent evidence of creative improvement.

### Counterexample — when not to broaden

A user requests a narrow, source-established validator repair, with no materially open product question. Read the relevant code/contract, implement the bounded correction, verify, and stop. A repository-wide possibility landscape would be unnecessary ceremony. A reviewer should treat this as correct use of scope, not a failure of global reasoning.

## 4. Source-grounded abductive and divergent work

Abduction here means proposing an explicitly uncertain explanatory or prospective hypothesis that makes sense of observations. Divergence means opening meaningfully different questions, frames, ends and expressions rather than selecting the most familiar solution. Neither is proof of demand or a required list of cognitive steps.

A useful *attributed account* may contain:

- **Starting ground:** exact source/revision and observed Claim, Decision, Assumption or capability, with authority and currentness.
- **Tension or observation:** what conflicts, became newly feasible, remains ambiguous, or appears absent *from the inspected corpus*.
- **Conceptual change:** beneficiary shift, changed purpose, assumption inversion, changed horizon, relationship composition, or alternative mechanism.
- **Expression:** the resulting InquiryQuestion, incomplete CandidateExpression or source-bounded ProductPossibility/ArchitectureOption hypothesis.
- **Difference and limitation:** why this is not merely a restatement; what is hypothetical, unknown, source-unchecked or owner-reserved.

The [TransformationAccount](divergent-exploration-ontology.md#transformationaccount) is an **authored explanation**, not an externalized private chain of thought or a causal reconstruction of how an LLM arrived at an idea. Incomplete questions can remain incomplete; a candidate is not automatically a durable possibility identity.

## 5. Operation-sensitive orientation without persona inflation

The same source knowledge supports different intellectual work:

| Active responsibility | Orientation | Important drift to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Priority Framing | Recover *accepted* intent and present repository-specific priorities separately from the temporary question. | Inventing owner values or implicitly changing product thesis. |
| Divergent Exploration | Widen materially distinct frames, questions, experiences and ends; preserve sources and provisional status. | Treating a current implementation as a permanent limit; optimizing one known mechanism; prematurely ranking or building. |
| Coverage Assessment | Inspect the represented and unexamined dimensions and likely value of another specified lens. | Generating additional candidates instead of assessing coverage; claiming saturation from counts. |
| Convergent Evaluation | Compare purpose-relative promise, unresolved depth, evidence and tradeoffs. | Choosing the easiest implementation or equating citations with merit. |
| Focused Depth Exploration | Trace conditional mechanisms, consequences and assumption-sensitive forks for a bounded premise. | Broad ideation instead of conditional deepening; promoting hypotheses to causality. |
| Implementation | Complete an already selected and authorized bounded responsibility. | Reopening settled discovery merely because other ideas are imaginable. |

The [dated inquiry-method reconciliation](../explorations/2026-11-03-inquiry-process-reconciliation.md) remains historical source evidence. The current Skill and conditional references own actual portable use. A mode name is not an automatic route or phase transition.

## 6. Lightweight orientation / regulation questions

For a **substantive** divergent inquiry, establish only what matters to the current question:

- What are we actually asking and what remains undecided?
- Which relevant sources/owner decisions govern, and which premises are legitimately revisable?
- What materially different outcome, experience, beneficiary or question could emerge?
- Are we developing the source relationships, or adding cosmetic variants to a familiar mechanism?
- Is a broader frame likely to change the result, or is the local scope intentionally sufficient?
- Should we continue, choose another lens, challenge, restart or stop under existing policies?

Keep this mostly implicit for short, clear work. Do not require a printed checklist, fixed number of frames, novelty score or universal inquiry report.

## 7. Calibration and claim limits

**Orientation fidelity** asks whether the agent did the *right kind* of task under correct scope, sources and authority. **Generative effectiveness** asks whether the *actual output* shows meaningful, source-grounded conceptual work. They are not interchangeable.

For authentic use, inspect six qualitative dimensions: (1) operation recognition, (2) relevant source use, (3) conceptual transformation, (4) global–local product coherence, (5) warranted reflective adjustment/stop, and (6) epistemic/authority integrity. Distinguish **OBSERVED**, **PARTIAL**, **UNRESOLVED** and **NOT_ASSESSED** in natural language, not new enum or machine-scored state. Identify the exact output/source evidence for any assessment.

Proof distinctions remain separate: reference documented != installed != loaded by host != correctly interpreted != materially applied != independently repeated != better than an alternative. A strong artifact may show useful application without proving that the guidance caused it.

Only require stronger independent/comparative qualification when a consequential adoption decision would depend on that claim. Authentic negative results can justify narrowing, repairing or stopping.

## 8. Representation and authority boundary

This crosswalk is **prose** over existing ontology identities, agent-facing guides and semantic policies. It introduces **no** parsed entity kind, graph predicate, migration, machine artifact, fixed workflow or mandatory audit log. A future representation extension must identify an authentic lost competency question and follow the [Steward schema-evolution process](../../skills/repository-semantic-steward/references/schema-evolution.md).

Only current authoritative product sources decide what the owner selected. An agent may generate new hypotheses about desirable outcomes, but those remain **proposals** until legitimately adopted. The active agent, not a graph or guide, owns judgment; implementation, publication and merge remain separately authority-scoped.
