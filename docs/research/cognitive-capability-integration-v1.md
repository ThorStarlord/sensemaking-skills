## 1. Research objective and evidence

Develop the cooperation of intellectual capabilities, not merely their inventory.
Reciprocal domain and cognitive development requires useful interaction within a
decision, across decisions, and across real development episodes. A useful
working definition: cognitive integration is the decision-relevant exchange,
interpretation, transformation and reconsideration of intellectual results
toward a common objective at acceptable total cost. Emergent competence is a
hypothesis about the benefit of cooperation, not an entity or proven effect.

## 2. Divergent exploration (adaptive, combined lenses)

Top-down: imagine a developmental agent that notices a new enabling
relationship, revises its method when warranted, and maintains ambition and
calibration without a supervisor. Bottom-up: inspect current consumer surfaces,
not just names of capabilities. The following branches are genuinely different
mechanisms; they may coexist. No branch is a requirement merely by appearing here.

| Frame / branch | Mechanism and possible higher-order competence | Limiting premise / risk / discriminating evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semantic continuity | Keep goal, scope, assumptions, alternatives, provenance and changed relationships available across operations. Enables grounded imagination. | More context may distract; incompatible source revisions can corrupt a comparison. Inspect exact queries and compare source identities. |
| Decision influence | Returned evidence changes a live premise, option, warrant target, execution shape or justified reaffirmation. Enables strategic synthesis. | A citation or a post-work note can be decorative. Observe a predecision choice and actual response, not invocation counts. |
| Adaptive intellectual organization | Discover and adapt conditional compositions of existing methods. May change the method itself, not only a domain hypothesis. | Retaining recipes without later applicability creates context debt. Inspect method producer, trial, recall and reuse. |
| Generative recombination | Forward/backcast reasoning plus semantic relations discover a new future or a cheaper route. | An interesting possibility is not necessarily feasible, useful or authorized. Compare resulting action and opportunity landscape. |
| Conflict integration | Distinguish factual disagreement, different objective, horizon, assumptions, and different commitment targets. Combine concerns rather than vote among specialists. | Genuine incompatible requirements still need adjudication; synthesis must not hide contradiction. |
| Independent collaboration | Separate explorer/critic challenges a live decision or alternative coverage, returning original source evidence. | Same-model anchoring, cost and unavailable independence; no automatic veto. Inspect actual host events. |
| Environment/model capability | Better source interfaces, context limits, native Skill access, or stronger reasoning can be the bottleneck instead of methods. | Host substitution cannot manufacture actor independence. Compare equivalent authorized execution and real task outcome. |
| Simpler direct agency | A strong direct agent with fewer layers can outperform a complex composition, particularly on clear tasks. | Lost continuity/foregone opportunity across longer work; measure total costs and actual outcomes, not machinery. |
| Explicit composition runtime / graph of cognitive operators | Might preserve relationships mechanically or automate known execution steps. | Premature semantic routing, schema burden, false implied authority; no present evidence that a new controller is needed. Retain as hypothesis, not a build direction. |

Recombination: decision-time context can couple the current possibility
landscape with prior method experience; selective synthesis can convert a
critic's risk and an explorer's opportunity into an affordable reversible
route. The purpose is neither merely to avoid mistakes nor to maximize
specialist utilization. It is to discover and realize stronger warranted
possibilities and learn from their pursuit.

## 3. Focused depth: the intellectual object is the interaction

### Influence and preservation

A result can have enabling (supplies missing knowledge), corrective
(challenges a claim), transformative (changes framing or feasible action), or
generative (supports a new combination) influence. These are descriptive
relations in reasoning, not proposed graph predicates. A useful interaction
has a specific consumer decision and a supported account of what changes or
why reaffirmation remains warranted.

At a consequential boundary preserve only what another operation needs:
governing intent/authority; question and scope; source identity and direct
evidence; competing alternatives and live assumptions; supported result with
counterevidence and limitations; implications for the actual next choice;
reconsideration conditions and relevant rejected/deferred possibilities.
Retain through the existing artifact, receipt, reviewed knowledge or method
episode as appropriate. Do not preserve all intermediate text or private
reasoning. A common vocabulary is insufficient without interpretation.

### Conflict, synthesis and commitment

A critic may identify an architectural risk while a trajectory analysis
identifies a valuable future. These findings can both be true. Distinguish
contradictory source claims (verify), premise/horizon differences (reframe),
tradeoffs (compare value and costs), different levels of commitment (consider
a reversible build or spike), and incompatible commitments (resolve or
escalate within authority). Do not manufacture a compromise that violates an
invariant. New evidence can change a previously rejected option.

Adaptive Commitment is a useful provisional lens, not a new mechanism:
admitting a plausible future, spending inquiry resources, prototyping,
building a removable capability, retaining architecture and claiming
demonstrated outcomes are different warrant targets. Evidence sufficient for
one is not sufficient for the next; demanding final qualification before a
cheap useful build can also be a category error. Existing Warrant / Choice,
Experiment Economy and strategic hypothesis admission govern these distinctions.

### Ambition and opportunity cost

Use **strategic underreach** provisionally for inadequate comparative
justification of a materially weaker contribution to the governing goal than
a credible, sufficiently warranted and authorized alternative. This is a
comparison to investigate, not a label to infer from a small diff. If the
alternative is only plausible, distinguish inadequate option search from
established selection underreach. Preserve historical defensive minimalism
as a hypothesized contributor, not a motive or synonym for defensive coding.
Omission-cost neglect is one potential comparative deficiency. Premature
convergence and premature local closure are different observations. A two-line
repair may be the best action; a massive implementation may be underreach or
overreach. Consider unbuilt futures, path dependence and the cost of delaying
learning as well as commission risks. Never choose hard work for difficulty's
sake or compare only a convenient patch and a giant speculative engine.
Strategic ambition and epistemic discipline can reinforce each other.

### Development of intellectual organization

An actual episode may reveal that the failure lies before evaluation (poor
option set), between methods (a relevant result unused), or after action
(no consequential reconciliation). One plausible conditional composition:
when broad selection is materially uncertain, recover the goal and future
state, reason backward to enabling conditions, explore materially different
routes, compare commitments including inexpensive reversible action, then
reconsider as evidence returns. This is an example, not a universal sequence.
SCL retains an attributable procedure only after genuine experience warrants
it; a method trial can preserve a predecision expectation and later
observation. A changed technical hypothesis or successful build alone is not
method adaptation. Do not create a lesson from this hypothetical example.

### Economic regulation

The marginal benefit of interaction must justify reading, reasoning,
generation, verification, maintenance, integration, future rediscovery,
delay and omission costs. Use the lightest combination that preserves the
decision, not the lightest patch regardless of its value. Clear local work
needs no integration ritual. A broad long-horizon decision may warrant
substantial exploration and a separate critic. Prefer retained reversible
builds when useful if successful and informative at acceptable cost;
disposable exploration is useful when retention is not yet warranted.
Cheap code does not imply cheap commitment or cheap evaluation.

## 4. Coverage and rival explanations

| Research question | Current support | Open frontier / appropriate evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which operation and when? | AMS, coordinator, Adaptive Skill Composition | Actual selection and return; not just a call |
| What survives between operations? | Semantic stewardship, typed artifacts, #997 closure and handoff | Source identity and decision relevance at a combined consumer |
| How are results combined? | Composition feedback moves, reconciliation, Warrant / Choice | Explicit result-to-decision influence and reconciliation of apparent conflicts |
| How can a method composition develop? | SCL, Method Adaptation, anchored Method Trial, detached draft preflight | Recovery beyond linked lessons; attributable composition change, later applicability |
| How do new possibilities emerge? | Strategic exploration, hypothesis admission, possibility ecology, DTR, possibility synthesis | Compare actual alternatives and enabling transitions; preserve uncertainty |
| What is the economic optimum? | Adaptive Guidance, Experiment Economy, Value-Producing Action | Real beneficiary cost/use and a credible direct-agent comparator |
| What proves integrated competence? | Decision Observability, receipt and trial contracts, separate qualification issues | Observed encounter, interpretation, choice influence, behavioral benefit and later transfer; none follows from a new field |

A diagnosis can distinguish activation (not selected/invoked), representation
(incompatible meaning), delivery (missing or truncated context), integration
(returned result not used), reasoning quality (unsupported inference), temporal
loss (no relevant reuse), or excess process. Avoid diagnosing absent private
deliberation; where trace is missing report NOT_ASSESSED. Competing explanations
remain open. Existing architecture supplies most intended operations. The
source-selected episodes justify improving their interaction, not concluding
that a universal runtime detector or another controller is necessary.

## 5. Convergent evaluation and preconstruction decision

Current ADR 0038 and Level-3 integrated development direction are reaffirmed.
The user delegates research, construction and ordinary repository-answerable
decisions. No thesis reopening, external qualification or protected release.

Credible responsibility alternatives:
- Improve only descriptive vocabulary: low cost, but misses actual integration
  consumers and the owner's positive capability-development objective.
- Strengthen portable composition guidance and Warrant/Choice cues: useful
  for decision relevance, conflict synthesis, calibrated ambition and learning;
  does not alone establish behavior.
- Compose current prospective/goal context and attributable method trials at
  the actual selection consumer: increases the available evidence for adapting
  intellectual organization. Existing combined product-outline method query
  resolves the requested ref again instead of using the resolved first ref.
  Standalone method context enumerates only lesson claims; an unlinked authored
  trial (the actual discovery-surface episode) can be recovered by exact ID
  but cannot be returned as a complete trial pair through question-based method
  recall. Generic search and manual exact-ID show are viable alternatives.
  New sustained re-entry goal differs from #965's bounded creator's workflow;
  we do not assert that its historical deferral was wrong or that an observed
  independent agent failed to discover it. Retain the no-list decision: use
  optional question-directed retrieval and existing trial projection, rather
  than create a global history inventory.
- New structured cognitive operator ontology / runtime or automatic method
  optimization: potentially powerful but missing decision semantics, causal
  evidence, calibrated evaluation, and a concrete necessity. Do not build now.
- Independent behavioral study: valuable to distinguish integration effects
  from better context or model; authentic executor is unavailable here.
  Qualification is separate from worthwhile construction.

**Selected:** develop the intellectual model in this research record, deepen
the existing portable composition and ambition/commitment guidance, and make
one source-pinned decision-time consumer integration: explicitly requested
historical trial precedents in existing method context, including unlinked
trials, using existing exact-ID show projection, and enable the same composed
method context with goal/candidate and possibility-ecology views. Preserve
separate evidence status and selection authority. Fix exact-revision join,
avoid new stores/schemas/controllers, disclose truncation and invalid or
unassessed pairs. A later agent can inspect actual experience without already
knowing its ID, while an ordinary lightweight query stays unchanged.

Expected observable product result: the existing historical discovery-surface
trial is discoverable from its actual question without a lesson link, the
anchored trial retains its existing evidence limits, and a composed
possibility/goal query and method evidence share one resolved SHA. Existing
direct method query and exact-ID show retain their meaning. Hosted checks can
verify this mechanically, not cognitive improvement. The decision note is
authored before the code change; it is not an anchored method trial or causal
trace. After construction, reconsider whether more product value is warranted.

## 6. Research continuation and evidence standard

The seven questions above remain a program of normal-use cognitive
development. In authentic tasks, distinguish availability, connectivity,
composition, decision influence, adaptive composition and longitudinal
benefit. These are provisional evidence distinctions, not a scored maturity
ladder. A useful unchanged decision can be success. Use Decision Observability
where consequential or requested; no mandatory audit. Compare a credible
direct approach and other mechanisms when claiming improvement. A later agent
should recover relevant prior evidence without planted IDs, state a specific
composition or warranted restraint, compare real opportunities, act, and
reconcile actual consequences. Learn only when experience supports change.
No synthetic historical receipt or unsupported method claim. Reconcile
#872/#874 separately; a local blocker does not freeze independent useful work.
