# ADR 0037: Domain-General Cognitive Development as Sensemaking's Product Purpose

**Status:** ACCEPTED — owner-ratified after the 2026-11-13 product-definition grilling and explicit authorization to implement the reconciliation
**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Level:** 4 — Product Thesis / Strategy Revision
**Disposition:** REVISE the fundamental purpose, primary users, value proposition and success standard; REINTERPRET compatible earlier architecture without rewriting historical decisions
**Current product thesis:** [Product strategy](../product-strategy.md)
**Current implemented external boundary:** [ADR 0029](0029-current-product-boundary.md)

## Context and owner decision

The owner explicitly answered 45 product-definition questions, chose the domain-general cognitive-development thesis, and authorized implementation of the final reconciliation without further task-by-task consultation. This is a normative Level-4 product selection, **not** a claim of realized general intelligence or proven comparative benefit.

The older repository-centered product remains useful, but no longer states the ultimate product purpose. Persistent semantic knowledge (ADR 0031), ontology-first prospective reasoning (ADR 0034), continuous learning (ADR 0035), and adaptive metareasoning (ADR 0036) are foundations for a wider intended outcome.

## Decision

**Sensemaking is a domain-general cognitive substrate for the continuous development of intelligent agents.** Its intended product outcome is persistent, reflective, increasingly capable and independent cognitive agency across domains. Coding, scientific, creative, educational and other domain-specific agents have equal standing as **primary operational users of core cognitive capabilities**, though availability and qualification are currently unequal.

**Integrated cognitive development**, rather than a knowledge graph or Skill catalogue alone, is the intended distinctive value: retained experience can inform present metareasoning and method adaptation so that later consequential judgment improves. This is a testable product hypothesis. Protect the objective of integration, not a particular schema, database, Skill arrangement or runtime; contrary evidence may warrant substantial redesign.

**Host control:** the active agent remains the semantic actor. Sensemaking is not an independent competing agent, model provider, execution runtime or semantic truth oracle. An agent may critically reconsider values and objectives, but its independent judgment does not confer permissions over third-party assets or override legitimate delegated/owner authority.

### Cognitive and extension boundaries

- **Portability:** distinguish attributable agent-level cognitive state, learned methods and evaluative commitments from domain-specific evidence and local authority. Compatible cross-host continuity is an intended capability, not an observed guarantee of agent identity.
- **Methods and value formation:** the agent may explicitly or adaptively combine Architect, Free-Thinker, Critic, Tutor and other intellectual approaches. These are not independent controllers or fixed mandatory phases. Value judgments should consider consequences, alternatives, affected parties, uncertainty and counterevidence; no universal scalar objective is required.
- **Learning:** agent-owned cognitive adaptation may be attributable and reversible, with independent evaluation. Promotion into **shared** methods, Skills or policies remains separately qualified, reviewed and authorized.
- **Knowledge:** a minimal shared cognitive ontology admits domain-specific extensions, preserving source, authority and context rather than homogenizing all domains.
- **Packages:** future cognitive domain packages extend knowledge and methods. External executable packages, including potential game-genre libraries, remain independent interoperable software capabilities. Current Domain Packs are descriptive Skill manifests, not a plugin runtime.
- **Adequacy:** understanding and relevant possibility discovery are mandatory for consequential strategic decisions; fixed upfront procedural exploration is not required for narrow, already-grounded work.

## Continuity with previous decisions

- **ADR 0029:** still governs the *currently implemented external product boundary* and authorization. This intended purpose does not authorize a generic runtime, policy self-modification, deployment, merge, publication or third-party operations.
- **ADR 0031:** persistent semantic stewardship remains an important knowledge-plane specialization, not the ultimate product end.
- **ADR 0034:** grounded repository understanding, adaptive composition and possibility coverage remain valid software-domain methods; repository-specific procedures are not automatically universal.
- **ADR 0035:** selective retention and higher-order learning are selected capabilities; independent cognitive improvement has not been qualified. Agent-local changes do not automatically rewrite shared policy.
- **ADR 0036:** adaptive cognitive regulation remains a foundational candidate through the active agent; no additional deterministic controller is authorized.
- **General Agency Model v0.1:** a relevant research reference, not automatically an accepted complete universal ontology.
- **ADR 0028 Domain Packs:** current engineering and product-management package manifests remain descriptive, not proof of domain-general cognition.

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

1. **Reaffirm repository stewardship as the ultimate product:** more bounded, but too narrow for the owner-selected developmental purpose.
2. **A composable agent toolkit without an integrated developmental end:** a simpler, strong practical competitor, but does not by itself account for evolving persistent cognition.
3. **Chosen — domain-general cognitive substrate:** coherently unifies continuity, exploration, metareasoning and learning, but is substantially harder to evaluate and may introduce unnecessary complexity unless it improves actual agents.

The owner chose to preserve the integrated-development **objective** under inconclusive early results. Specific mechanisms remain defeasible: sustained negative evidence should lead to revision, stopping or replacement rather than protective rationalization.

## Evidence ceiling and downstream control

The qualification ladder is conceptual coherence -> operational availability -> authentic consequential behavior -> matched comparative advantage -> longitudinal transfer across unfamiliar tasks/domains. Early bounded research should compare experience-informed method adaptation with fixed methods across independent episodes and eventually isolate the contribution of different components.

**Not established:** causal benefit of cognitive integration, transferable improvement, cross-host agent identity continuity, independently beneficial self-improvement, general product utility, or superiority to capable direct agents with ordinary memory and Skills. Acceptance of the strategy changes none of these empirical claims.
