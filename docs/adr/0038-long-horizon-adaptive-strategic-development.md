## Context and ratification

[ADR 0037](0037-domain-general-cognitive-development.md) selected a domain-general cognitive substrate whose intended end was integrated cognitive development. The owner subsequently identified a gap between that broad thesis and the practical experience of sustained product development. Two beneficiary construction reports showed useful agent-facing reasoning guidance but mainly manual reconstruction of repository understanding; one target had no reviewed semantic pack, another retained unchanged reviewed knowledge despite consequential development. Both receipts withheld knowledge-edit authority. Skill currency and receipt validation were inconsistent. These are bounded reported/source-inspected observations, not proof of causation, cost reduction, or cross-domain cognitive superiority.

The owner agreed to progressive semantic stewardship in sustained engagements, reciprocal product and cognitive development, and the proposed definition of Long-Horizon Adaptive Strategic Development. In the final discussion the recommendation was Option B (integrated strategic and cognitive identity), with software product development first as the practical construction and positioning priority and domain-general applicability preserved as the architectural ambition. The owner's explicit agreement and request to implement all recommendations ratifies that selection. This record attributes the selection to that agreement; specific document organization and implementation choices are delegated agent decisions, not additional owner votes.

## Decision

1. **Defining purpose.** Long-Horizon Adaptive Strategic Development is the sustained development of a product or other worthwhile endeavor through persistent understanding, prospective discovery, reflective strategic judgment, adaptive intellectual activity and learning from consequential experience. Sensemaking is the cognitive-development substrate that enables agents to exercise this capability autonomously within delegated authority.
2. **Reciprocal development.** The object of activity (the product/domain) and the subject of activity (the agent's attributable understanding, judgment and intellectual methods) are both intended to develop through consequential work. Neither is merely an excuse for the other. Prefer independently valuable work and selectively retain experience that can improve later work. Continuous Construction is the Probe.
3. **Practical priority.** Software product development is the flagship construction and positioning priority: delegate the development of a product, not merely its tasks. Scientific, creative, educational and other agents retain equal conceptual standing in the cognitive core; equal current availability or qualification is not claimed. A repository is an important environment, not the universal unit of cognition.
4. **Meaning of long horizon.** Distinguish duration from consideration of distant consequences, and distinguish strategy (valuable direction) from execution. Preserve directional, semantic, prospective, cognitive and developmental continuity across fresh contexts. A development trajectory evolves with realized capabilities and changing evidence; a fixed roadmap or continuously executing backlog is insufficient. Support both strategy-guided and strategy-generating development within authority. No arbitrary elapsed-time or episode-count threshold.
5. **Integrated operating experience.** Recover governing intent; establish or reuse adequate semantic understanding; discover and compare meaningful future possibilities when the direction is open; use AMS to select the right intellectual work; pursue authorized, valuable outcomes; reconcile material evidence; retain domain knowledge and conditional intellectual experience through SCL. These are complementary responsibilities, not mandatory stages. Direct local ACT or STOP remains appropriate. Analysis-only requests remain read-only unless separately authorized.
6. **Sustained stewardship expectation.** For sustained repository development, maintaining adequate reviewed semantic knowledge and considering attributable method learning are ongoing intended responsibilities, not independent optional extras repeatedly skipped for immediate economy. Bootstrap when the model is materially inadequate for sustained engagement, recall when knowledge could affect a decision, and reconcile consequential new understanding. Use existing authoritative surfaces, record NO_MODEL_CHANGE for routine or non-reusable outcomes, and scale effort to total development cost including future rediscovery. No exhaustive model, per-task artifact quota, or mandatory strategy reopen. Explicitly establish knowledge-edit authority; without it, make provisional knowledge and the unresolved stewardship boundary visible rather than silently claiming retention or widening authority.
7. **Artifact lifecycle and economy.** A new agent should know which existing artifacts to create, read, verify and update, what each establishes, and when it can omit them. The existing [portable reference](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/long-horizon-strategic-development-v1.md) defines the integrated producer/consumer path. Session receipts are history/pointers, reviewed knowledge is source-linked meaning, strategic artifacts are direction/rationale, and indexes are derived observations. None substitutes for another or confers authority.
8. **Cognitive ownership.** Preserve distinct domain-owned knowledge, attributable agent-level methods/experience, and reviewed shared guidance. Transfer only appropriately qualified and permitted methodological experience, not domain facts or authority by default. AMS regulates present thinking; SCL supports retention, later source-checked recall, feedback and method adaptation. No autonomous shared-policy modification or model-weight training is implied.
9. **Control and boundary.** `using-sensemaking` remains the single public agent-native controller; `strategic-sensemaking-loop` is the internal continuity capability. No new master Skill, state machine, ontology, planner or execution runtime is selected by this ADR. ADR 0029 remains current external product boundary; ADRs 0031/0034/0035/0036 remain compatible foundations. Explicit user authority and target policy govern writes, commits, merge, publication, release and deployment. The product definition is not an authority grant.
10. **Success.** Demonstrate sustained valuable development and decision-time reuse/reconciliation of relevant understanding; independently evaluate whether attributable method adaptations improve later consequential reasoning at acceptable total cost. Separate product outcome, availability, actual use, decision influence, comparative benefit and longitudinal transfer. The integrated reciprocal-development benefit remains NOT QUALIFIED.

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

- A — positioning only: keep ADR 0037 unchanged and advertise strategic development as a use case. Preserves breadth but could leave feature selection and fragmented operational experience unchanged.
- B — chosen integrated identity: make sustained strategic development and reciprocal cognitive development defining commitments. Provides a concrete selection criterion, while adding a responsibility to make continuity economical and operationally accessible.
- C — product-centered only: treat cognitive development merely as an instrument of better products. Sharp practical positioning but abandons an important owner-selected developmental purpose.

A capable direct agent with ordinary memory and specialist Skills is a serious comparator. Documentation and additional procedures can cost more than they return. Prefer integration and simplification of existing capabilities when sufficient; revise mechanisms under contrary evidence. The definition does not establish market uniqueness.
