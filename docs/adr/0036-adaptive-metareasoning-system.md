# ADR 0036: Adaptive Metareasoning System as a First-Class Cognitive Capability

**Status:** ACCEPTED — owner-selected product direction, 2026-11-12; behavioral effectiveness and release qualification remain separate.
**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Level:** 4 — Product Thesis / Strategy Revision
**Disposition:** REINTERPRET/EXTEND ADR 0034's adaptive agent-native reasoning objective; preserve ADR 0031's semantic knowledge destination, ADR 0035's separate SCL learning capability, and ADR 0029's external authority boundary.
**Authority:** The owner explicitly agreed to the name Adaptive Metareasoning System (AMS), requested a detailed construction plan, and subsequently delegated implementation in the 2026-11-12 conversation. The present record captures that direction, not an invented claim of demonstrated improvement.
**Current source:** [Product strategy](../product-strategy.md) and [Level-4 revision contract](../product-thesis-revision.md).
**Design / implementation program:** [AMS design](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-adaptive-metareasoning-system-design.md) and [AMS plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-adaptive-metareasoning-system.md).

## Context and product decision

ADR 0034 already establishes one public, agent-owned semantic controller, **using-sensemaking**, and source-grounded prospective reasoning. The [Metareasoning Policy](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/metareasoning-policy-v0.md) chooses whether the next useful move is ACT, INQUIRE, CHALLENGE, EXPLORE, VERIFY, ESCALATE, or STOP. The [Adaptive Policy Coordinator](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/adaptive-policy-coordinator-v0.md) and [Cognitive Orientation Crosswalk](../semantic-architecture/cognitive-orientation-crosswalk-v1.md) already express conditional composition, purpose-relative question fitness and reasoning regulation.

Earlier [Stage 1-4 adaptive-metareasoning exploration](../explorations/2026-11-07-adaptive-metareasoning-convergent-evaluation.md) identified consequential intellectual challenges: (A) apparently productive reasoning that misses governing purpose; (B) discriminating bad evidence, execution, method, question and host/delivery causes; (C) minimum useful method correction or composition; (D) differentiating correction of a bad question from a good answer opening a distinct question; (E) preserving consequential continuity without overgeneralizing. These concepts are largely represented; reliable ordinary-use behavior is not independently qualified.

The owner distinguishes current **metareasoning** from longer-term **continuous learning**. The prior metareasoning behavioral-gap assessment ended NO_CHANGE because no concrete defect justified a new engine at that time. That bounded diagnosis did not close a separately owner-selected capability-development objective.

## Decision

1. **First-class identity:** Adaptive Metareasoning System (AMS) names Sensemaking's integrated cognitive-regulation capability: orient to governing purpose, examine question/method fitness, choose proportional intellectual work and maintain appropriate stopping and authority boundaries.
2. **Existing controller:** AMS is exercised through **using-sensemaking**, not a second controller, scheduler, agent, Skill package, planner or mandatory state machine. Existing policy guidance remains canonical.
3. **Question fitness is central:** Recognize both incorrect-but-productive-looking questions and valid answered questions that reveal separate consequential inquiries; do not automatically reframe or promote interesting tangents.
4. **Diagnosis precedes correction:** A poor outcome may reflect bad evidence, a flawed inference, wrong question, unsuitable method, flawed composition, unavailable tool, host condition or an authority constraint. Prefer the smallest warranted correction rather than mandatory method switching.
5. **Proportionality:** Explicit AMS reasoning is conditional. Direct ACT or STOP can be the correct output for narrow, well-evidenced tasks. No quotas, numeric metacognitive score or periodic mandatory reflection.
6. **Distinct SCL boundary:** AMS owns present method/question judgment. Sensemaking Continuous Learning (ADR 0035) owns evidence-grounded retention, later recall and learning feedback. Historical lessons may inform, but do not dictate, current choices or transfer source-repository authority.
7. **Observable maturity:** Distinguish conceptual coverage, accessible guidance/tools, authentic demonstrated agent behavior and independently qualified benefit. Documentation, record retrieval or a successful PR does not prove metareasoning improvement.
8. **Conditional implementation:** Prioritize discoverable agent-native use and an authentic, worthwhile engineering vertical; add executable support only when an exact consumer gap and bounded source-backed benefit are established.

## Alternatives assessed

- **Keep AMS entirely implicit:** preserves low ceremony but fails the owner's explicit objective to define and improve cognitive regulation as a recognizable product capability.
- **Build an automatic MetareasoningEngine or central cognitive router now:** would duplicate the accepted semantic controller, assume a deterministic choice contract not evidenced in real use, and risk expanding authority. Rejected now, not forbidden forever.
- **Chosen:** first-class product identity over existing agent-native semantics, an implementation program oriented toward authentic use, and narrowly evidenced supporting changes.

## Evidence, authority and Level-3 return

Current policies and executable knowledge infrastructure exist. Independent, reliable decision-time question/method correction and its comparative product benefit are **NOT QUALIFIED**. A source-backed task may show an appropriate cognitive move without establishing causality or repeatability.

The current Level-3 PATH-1 goal (source-grounded stewardship through valuable engineering responsibilities) remains relevant. AMS strengthens the *method of choosing and conducting* those responsibilities; it does not authorize a new strategy, unrelated target repository, automatic learning engine, policy self-modification, release, deployment, protected merge or publication. Ordinary integration follows existing repository authorization and build policy.

The owner's selection is a Level-4 direction; each bounded engineering change still requires a consumer, source-grounded warrant, appropriate verification and reconciliation. When evidence later shows a method is unhelpful, revise or retire that application instead of defending AMS through more reflection artifacts.

## Owner ratification

The owner explicitly selected the AMS name and first-class direction and requested autonomous construction in the 2026-11-12 conversation. This ADR records the selection while preserving the difference between an intended product end and verified autonomous cognitive behavior.
