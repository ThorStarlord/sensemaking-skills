- Constraint inversion overlaps F5 proxy capture; gate preemption and local-blocker freeze overlap F8/F16. The [existing strategic taxonomy](../strategic-normal-use-failure-taxonomy-v1.md) also recognizes symmetric over-search and over-reconciliation.

## 2. Divergent and abductive inquiry

Combined problem/repository-led adaptive exploration, then decision-focused comparison for the immediate construction choice. The question is how to make appropriate cognitive review affect the *actual next action*, while preserving cheap direct work and active-agent authority. Consider these competing explanations and approaches independently.

| Branch | Mechanism / value | Decisive uncertainty and risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose-fit and continuation | Surface the original intent, scope, selected work and unaddressed difference at consequential decisions. Could prevent mission contraction. | Guidance already exists; visibility may not translate into application. Inspect authored decisions and stop rationale. |
| Alternative coverage and choice | Use divergent, abductive, coverage and comparative methods; compare omission costs and actual feasible responsibilities. | Premature convergence may not be the cause. Broader search can be wasteful. Check whether an omitted alternative was credible at decision time. |
| Critic / independent review | A separate agent can challenge assumptions and stopping judgments with original evidence. | Availability, cost, independence and same-context anchoring; critique is not authority or automatic veto. Use selectively. |
| Method continuity | Use source-linked prior lessons, method context, method trial and feedback when the intellectual method choice is material. | A method may be irrelevant, host unavailable, or retrieval not applied. Preserve authentic provenance; no invented trial. |
| Decision-time evidence delivery | Make already-authored goal, stop rationale, limits and next responsibility jointly accessible at re-entry; avoid forcing inference from a stop enum or a bare next task. | Actual reader may already expose enough. Raw receipt reads are a viable alternative; additional tooling must reduce real friction, not create a detector. |
| Environment and delegation | Recover exact tool/host identity, separate local from mission-wide blockers, preserve authority/gate equivalence; choose an available lawful execution substrate where appropriate. | Host-specific behavior or independent actor identity cannot be substituted. #995 provides a real, bounded precedent. |
| No new capability / operational guidance only | Apply existing methods and observe ordinary work; no architecture change. | Recurrence may continue if the decisive evidence is not available at point of use. |
| Dedicated automatic failure detector or universal cognitive runtime | Could give consistent machine checks. | No established detection semantics or calibrated causal model; semantic authority conflict, false positives, costs, and ritual review. Not warranted. |

Cross-cutting hypotheses: missing/stale evidence; narrow prompt interpretation; poor method application/composition; selection pressure toward easy evidence; environment-specific feasibility; genuinely rational stopping. Only the recorded scope and evidence mismatch are presently established in #935. A cheap discriminating observation is whether the existing re-entry consumer presents the goal and stopping rationale at the same decision surface; native predecision evidence from a real later task is necessary to determine whether the agent corrects itself. Do not create a synthetic reproduction to claim autonomous cognition.

## 4. Convergent evaluation and scoped selection

**Select:** integrate concise, conditional safeguards into the existing controller, AMS policy, canonical full-autonomy task prompt and local guidance, together with the historical reconciliation. Existing semantics stay intact. This directly fulfills the owner's operational request and gives a fresh consumer a specific action trigger, not merely a new label. The more conservative choice (no guidance update) leaves the requested safety net absent; the more aggressive choice (new reviewer/controller/ontology) lacks evidence and conflicts with ADR 0038. Prefer reviewing actual decisions to every output.

**Construction direction:** inspect the existing receipt reader/first-use and method consumers for a concrete point-of-use barrier. If relevant authored closure context is suppressed, a small mechanical projection of existing fields can make the contrast visible without inferring ORD, ranking tasks or changing receipt semantics. Compare raw reads and no change before implementing. If already supported, use it instead. Preserve previously qualified behavior and check real historical receipts. Method recall/trials remain conditional, not required for routine work.

**Developmental choice:** the intended trajectory is externally prompted recognition -> method-supported diagnosis -> self-initiated correction -> conditional adaptation -> experience-informed prevention -> transferable improvement. These are prospective capability states, not ratified maturity levels or observed transitions. The governing product strategy is reaffirmed; no Level-3 reopening or Level-4 revision is warranted. Construction and use proceed together where justified.
