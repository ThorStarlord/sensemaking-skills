# Prospective synthesis capability (2026-11-23)

**Status:** normal-use capability construction. **Source baseline:** `1f8a21fb8b053fef607422027f15394f18351fa9`. **Capability:** an agent can turn a goal-family ecology into a reviewable new possibility with validated provenance, without automatic generation, ranking or selection. This is not independent later-agent use, comparative benefit or proof that the synthesized possibility is valuable.

## 1. Capability definition

**Beneficiary:** an agent doing prospective product reasoning.

**Consequential intellectual activity:** inspect an authored goal family, recognize a new recombination or gap, and retain it as a candidate future whose relationships, assumptions, contradictions and evidence are reconstructible.

**Present limitation before this work:** `product-outline` and `possibility-ecology` could retrieve and relate existing candidates, but an agent had no supported producer for a new synthesis. It would have to edit the reviewed possibility pack by hand, with no exact-revision validation of cited goals, candidates, capabilities or assumptions and no guarantee that the new claim remained hypothetical.

**Intended behavior:** an agent authors a synthesis episode; the producer validates every cited claim at the exact source revision and creates an unreviewed hypothetical `opportunity` with goal, `derived_from`, capability, assumption and contradiction links. After ordinary review, existing consumers expose it.

**Consumer:** `semantic possibility record`, then `knowledge-proposal-check`, `product-outline`, `possibility-ecology` and normal Git review.

**Completion condition:** a real synthesis is materialized, validated, reviewed and visible in the existing ecology with its authored provenance.

## 2. Why this rather than another local repair

The prior goal-family projection made eight candidates visible from the ratified top goal. It did not let an agent do anything with the family beyond reading it. The next capability gap was therefore not another field or query: it was the ability to **compose and retain a new candidate** from the family.

Alternatives considered:

- Another integrity repair: no current consequential defect was left; lower product value.
- A ranking/comparison engine: would replace agent judgment and had no demonstrated consumer need.
- A Campaign schema extension: existing Campaign/strategy handoff already preserves mission and responsibility; no schema change was demonstrated.
- A generated-opportunity engine: would manufacture candidates and undermine agent ownership.
- Agent-authored synthesis with provenance validation: selected.

## 3. Construction

New optional producer `semantic possibility record`:

- validates exact `source_revision`, tracked episode and evidence;
- requires existing `goal:` refs and existing `poss:` source candidates;
- optionally accepts `cap:`, assumption and contradiction refs;
- requires the four ProductPossibility dimensions and refuses a realization value other than `HYPOTHETICAL` or `NOT_ASSESSED`;
- creates a hypothetical `opportunity` claim with `candidate_opportunity_for`, `derived_from`, `requires_capability`, `assumes` and `contradicts` relations;
- creates an unreviewed proposal only; it does not edit existing claims, rank candidates, select a direction, commit or authorize work.

The real synthesis `poss:goal-family-construction-bridge` combines:

- `poss:assumption-aware-opportunity-ecology`;
- `poss:construction-time-possibility-reconciliation`;
- `poss:cross-episode-possibility-synthesis`;
- `poss:repository-local-prospective-decision-memory`;

with four existing capabilities, two assumptions (`hyp:cross-episode-synthesis-recurs`, `con:agent-owns-semantic-control`) and one explicit conflicting alternative (`poss:automatic-strategic-planner`). It proposes a compact construction bridge from a selected goal-family candidate to existing capabilities, Campaign/strategy evidence and the remaining realization conditions.

The synthesis is deliberately hypothetical. It is a new recombination, not an implemented bridge, selected direction or benefit claim.

## 4. End-to-end demonstration

1. **Initial task:** choose a valuable next capability after the goal-family discovery increment.
2. **Cognitive context:** the top goal family exposed eight authored candidates, ten shared links and realization states.
3. **New synthesis:** the agent composed `poss:goal-family-construction-bridge` from four candidates, four capabilities, two assumptions and one contradiction.
4. **Producer behavior:** `semantic possibility record` validated all refs at `1f8a21fb`, created the hypothetical claim and thirteen relations, and returned `UNREVIEWED_DRAFT` with `action_authorized: false`.
5. **Consumer:** after commit, `product-outline` and `possibility-ecology` expose the new candidate under its goals with `derived_from`, `requires_capability`, `assumes` and `contradicts` relationships.
6. **What was not previously possible:** the agent could read a family but had no supported, provenance-checked way to turn a synthesis into a reviewable candidate that existing consumers display.

## 6. Continuation

The next warranted responsibility is to use the synthesized bridge in one authentic construction trajectory or to observe a later agent doing so:

- select a candidate from the goal family;
- inspect the bridge's assumptions, source capabilities and conflicting alternative;
- choose a bounded increment and record the intended outcome;
- use existing Campaign/strategy/handoff mechanisms if they materially help;
- observe whether the bridge reduces reconstruction or changes the chosen increment.

If the bridge is redundant with existing continuity surfaces, that is a justified rejection. If it helps, implementation can follow the evidence. No automatic planner, ranker or generator is warranted now.
