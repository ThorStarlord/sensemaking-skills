## Bounded methodological candidate: adaptation-object check

This corrective review exposed a useful **claim-adjudication procedure** for future situations in which an apparently successful task is cited to close a methodological-development objective. It specializes existing AMS purpose-fit and SCL's distinction between product and methodological knowledge rather than adding a controller.

**When a consequential completion/learning claim depends on an alleged method adaptation:**

1. **Name the adaptation object.** Compare the earlier and later *reasoning procedure* (which question was asked, what evidence was sought, how counterevidence/options were compared, and what triggered a decision or stop) separately from the technical hypothesis, patch and task outcome.
2. **Demand the right evidence.** A successful patch, changed factual belief or adjusted one-off repair plan does not alone establish a change in a reusable intellectual procedure. If the procedure's before/after behavior is not observable, state `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, not that adaptation is impossible.
3. **Retain conditionally, if warranted.** Record a candidate method with the task class, source and revision, steps or decision trigger, conditions, exclusions, alternatives and contrary observations. Preserve the original incident unchanged. Its reviewable existence does not establish subsequent agent use.
4. **Reassess later.** A future *independently valuable* task, selected for its own purpose, may discover, source-check, apply, modify or reject that candidate. Record actual changed methodological choice and outcome separately from retrieval, retention and any claimed improvement.

**This is a candidate for later use, not a demonstrated learned skill.** Its source is the observed mismatch between #913's technical outcome and #930's closure interpretation plus the owner's explicit correction. The corrective review here uses the distinction as requested by the owner; that does not demonstrate independent, spontaneous, later reuse or improved reasoning competence.

**Counterinterpretations:** The #909 agent might have adapted an investigative method in private, but the accessible evidence does not establish it. Some task-local plans may embody generalizable methods; the absence of a trace should remain an evidence limit, not a confident negative. A future episode may show this four-part check is too strict, too costly or needs different boundaries.

**Revisit when:** A genuine later methodological-development claim depends on a technical repair, or a real task demonstrates a changed source-verified reasoning procedure and its applicability, or this distinction demonstrably causes a missed useful adaptation.
