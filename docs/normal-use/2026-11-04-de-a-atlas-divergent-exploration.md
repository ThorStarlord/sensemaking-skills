## 1. Source-grounded frame

Atlas's canonical [Product Thesis][T] promises **“hardcore depth without the hardcore stress”**: durable authored heroes, readable tactical responsibility, changeable builds, attachment to companions and familiar places, optional co-op, capable ordinary civilization and **Living Continuity**. A Guardian protects what people and institutions have already made valuable; neither character centrality nor combat power makes local people helpless. [Player Experience Identity][PX] adds calm, reversible experimentation, multiple legitimate cognitive intensities, a recoverable learning curve and protected historical biography.

**Three related authorities, not interchangeable:**

1. **Who the character remains.** The authored **Guardian / PRESERVE**, **Sigrid / INTERCEPT**, and **Eira / ANTICIPATE** carry stable values, habits, social responsibility and Hearth ties across compatible Jobs. Sigrid is a route steward; Eira is an archive reader/public educator. The Guardian's mature arc is to preserve without monopolizing others' decisions. [Character framework][CH], [Sigrid][SIGRID], [Eira][EIRA], [Guardian arc][GUARD].
2. **What the character can do in this practice.** Production buildcraft puts **character signature before compatible Job before build**. It imagines skill transformations/keystones with opportunity costs; Sigrid acts at credible enemy commitment, Eira from earlier legible preparation cues, Guardian establishes something worth preserving. Same Job does not transfer unique signatures. **This full transformation/signature system is selected future production design, NOT implemented.** The present hero runtime exercises a shared eight-Job corpus with bounded companion Job loadouts, discipline/mastery/Legacy proofs; solo direct control of every named roster character, per-character deep builds and full production compatibility are not established. [Build identity][BJ], [build-system identity][BD], [Transformative Buildcraft][TB], [detailed resolution][CI], [Project Status][STAT], [three-seat party model][PARTY].
3. **What the civilization contributes.** Hearths are places/institutions of People, Memory, Shelter, Craft and Connection—not generic XP meters or passive blessing dispensers. The accepted regional network derives **Safe Passage, Archive Relay, Relief Circuit** from the restored/connected Hearths, and allows one reversible active preparation: Route Support, Archive Intel or Relief Support. **This bounded two-Hearth preparation loop exists**: [regional decision][NET], [Package 15 receipt][IMPL15], [Hearth service implementation][HEARTHCODE], [`RegionalPreparationCatalog`][PREP]. Current effects are stamina recovery, stability/interrupt bonuses, or healing/barrier receipt, respectively. They do **not** yet model a universal logistics simulation, detailed hazard forecasts or an interactive society of build-aware NPCs. The separate [`RegionalNetworkCatalog`][NETCODE] also encodes emergency service support; do not confuse its active-support IDs with the three expedition preparations.

**Actual ordinary-life boundary:** [Larkspur's Package-16 implementation][LARK] and [morning circuit code][LARKCODE] expose a six-stop **noncombat**, civic/professional presentation with Sigrid/Eira reactions and derived regional markers. The renewal preference and station visits in that slice are session-local, not new durable world authority. These sources do **not** establish that an individual character's future keystone changes the civic encounter or creates a new persistent profession. [Hearth Model][HEARTH] and [Narrative Identity][NAR] distinguish desirable ordinary life from crisis and living restoration from a frozen return to before.

**Critical distinction:** selected canonical goals ≠ current runtime ≠ this inquiry's speculation. Where an idea uses the future signature or future itemization layer, the idea is a **hypothesis about what could be experienced**, not a report of something already playable.

## 2. Distinct possibility space — seven ways the same relationships could matter

The local labels `DE-A/01`–`DE-A/07` only identify candidates **inside this report**, preserving differences for another reader. They are **not** Atlas product IDs, curated `poss:` objects or construction tasks.

### DE-A/01 — Three ways of *noticing* the same danger

**Experience sought:** choosing an authored character alters **what a player learns to pay attention to**, not merely which attack produces the highest damage. The pleasure is *inhabiting another competent person's way of perceiving responsibility*.

**Source transformation:** [signature differences][TB] and the same Ranger vocabulary for [Sigrid][SIGRID] and [Eira][EIRA] meet the network's [Archive Intel and Route Support][NET]. Imagine one regional threat: Eira notices signs early and commits a preventer; Sigrid spots the point of dangerous commitment and intervenes; the Guardian preserves a meaningful place/ally/objective so it survives the pressure. A shared Job is thus **a shared language spoken with different timing and priorities**, not three cosmetic class skins.

**Prior-art boundary:** the **three signatures and Sigrid/Eira Ranger contrast are already explicitly designed**. The new **HYPOTHESIS** is the player-facing *regional literacy* that could emerge when distinct attention patterns meet real Hearth support and local danger. Archive Intel today is mainly concrete combat modifiers; it does **not** provide automatic Eira-specific prophecy, hidden RNG information, or authored regional predictive scenes.

**Distinct from other candidates:** this changes **perception during a crisis**, not civic authorship, moral decision authority, recovery pace or object attachment.

### DE-A/02 — The civilization becomes *legible as your ally*

**Experience sought:** a player feels **supported by a capable civilization**, not simply rewarded for saving passive NPCs. The player can see that their success depended on other people's preserved expertise, route labor and recovery work.

**Source transformation:** [Hearth social ontology][HEARTH] + [regional capacities with identifiable institutions][NET] + character-anchored responsibility create an imagined encounter where an expedition's recovery, intelligence or staging is visibly attributed to road crews, teachers or healers. The Guardian may heroically anchor a defense, but the Relief Circuit makes an otherwise costly mistake survivable; it is **civilian capability acting in the same narrative as the hero**.

**Prior-art boundary:** “civilization complements the hero,” Safe Passage/Archive Relay/Relief Circuit and visible Larkspur regional markers are **ALREADY ACCEPTED/partly implemented** [T][LARK][IMPL15]. Additional *character- and context-sensitive acknowledgment of mutual reliance* is a **REVISED experiential HYPOTHESIS**, not a new regional network level, fourth party seat, universal NPC AI or implemented story reaction.

**Distinctness:** the question is **who enables heroism**; DE-A/01 asks **what the hero notices**.

### DE-A/03 — Heroism through *trusting someone else to act*

**Experience sought:** the player can feel competent **without taking every available decision**, including moments when the mature heroic act is to protect a capable person's autonomy or permit an informed local risk.

**Source transformation:** the [Guardian's responsibility-to-handoff arc][GUARD], [Sigrid's route stewardship][SIGRID], [Eira's knowledge-sharing dilemma][EIRA] and [Hearth's local ownership][HEARTH] suggest an encounter where a powerful build gives the player the *ability* to intervene but not necessarily the best *authority* to decide how a community uses its capabilities. A trusted Roadwarden might choose a defensible evacuation route; the Guardian can make it survivable without taking over.

**Prior-art boundary:** trust, delegation, Sigrid's over-responsibility and the Guardian's mature self-limitation are **CANONICAL EXISTING STORY IDEAS** [GUARD][SIGRID]. Giving a player a consequential *felt decision* to step back—without inventing a political sim or “do nothing for a bonus” mechanic—is a **HYPOTHETICAL EXPERIENCE EXTENSION**, not a claimed new theme. Player choice must not turn informed local expertise into a subordinate stat.

**Distinctness:** **who has legitimate initiative/agency** differs from DE-A/02's acknowledgment that institutions provide tangible aid.

### DE-A/04 — A hero's competence makes sense *when no one is fighting*

**Experience sought:** an authored character's way of caring is recognizable in **beautiful ordinary life**, not just dialogue immediately after a boss or a combat stat displayed at a Hearth.

**Source transformation:** [Eira's public teaching and field records][EIRA], [Sigrid's route safety work][SIGRID], [the five Hearth domains][HEARTH] and the [six-stop Larkspur circuit][LARK] could make the same underlying competence legible in the morning. Eira might notice that an archive explanation omits the hazard a villager needs to understand; Sigrid might recognize an unsafe staging practice; Guardian might reinforce someone else's repair effort without claiming it. A build's emphasis could inform the **player's interpretation of a scene**, not become an automatic noncombat ability tied to DPS.

**Prior-art boundary:** Sigrid/Eira's existing ordinary professions and their reactions in Larkspur are **already articulated and partly presented** [SIGRID][EIRA][LARKCODE]. The **newly proposed question** is whether *player-chosen responsibility expression* can be felt across combat and peacetime while remaining consistent with the authored person. It is **NOT AN IMPLEMENTED BUILD-DEPENDENT SOCIAL SYSTEM**. Do not turn every peaceful place into chores, a daily job simulator or a new mastery currency.

**Distinctness:** DE-A/04 concerns **continuity between crisis and peace**; DE-A/01 concerns **moment-to-moment tactical perception**.

### DE-A/05 — One continuing person can inhabit different *legitimate practices*

**Experience sought:** a player can say, *“This is still my Guardian, but I have learned to preserve things differently,”* without discarding a character, respec-punishment or inventing an alternate-history personality.

**Source transformation:** [character-first, Job-second, build-third][BJ] plus [Hearth-bounded reversible specialization][TB] and [reversible regional preparations][NET] permit a future experience of expressing **different obligations on different expeditions**. A Guardian might prepare a resilient restorative response for a difficult rescue, then a more active suppressive practice for a different crisis. The enduring biography remains the same. Sigrid/Eira similarly have distinct compatible methods without exchanging signatures.

**Prior-art boundary:** **reversible builds, persistent authored character, low/high-activity viability and separate History are already explicit CANONICAL goals** [T][PX][BJ]. This is primarily **RESTATED as a coherent player experience**, with an **unproven cross-system extension**: making the chosen regional support/character practice feel like different *ways of remaining oneself* rather than an optimal-stat loadout. Full per-character deep build persistence is **not current runtime**. No duplicate timeline, permanent lost character, compulsory Job entitlement or mid-combat overhaul is implied.

**Distinctness:** DE-A/05 concerns **self-continuity across time and choices**, not the moment's danger or the community's credit.

### DE-A/06 — Missing support reveals what the relationship was worth

**Experience sought:** the player recognizes the difference between *being self-sufficient* and *being resilient with others*, particularly when a regional institution is strained and a familiar kind of support temporarily fails.

**Source transformation:** the [regional capability failure rule][NET] and implemented [strain suppression without deleting the chosen preparation][IMPL15] meet the future character-specific [signature and Job identity floors][TB]. Imagine a strained Archive Hearth making Archive Intel inactive; Eira can still read visible threats and the party remains viable, but the absence of regional support changes the quality of the expedition. Repair is meaningful because the community's help returns, **not** because a numerical modifier is replenished.

**Prior-art boundary:** breached-Hearth suppression and retained player choice already exist in the bounded network. Character-specific qualitative reactions and the player's *felt experience of missing civic capacity* remain **HYPOTHETICAL**. Eira's baseline competence must not secretly depend on unavailable intelligence, and no mandatory daily repair schedule or permanent history deletion is allowed.

**Distinctness:** DE-A/02 is **positive reciprocal support**; DE-A/06 explores **dependency, loss, adaptation and restoration**. They can coexist, but one is not simply a second support buff.

### DE-A/07 — A long-lived object can carry *another person's craft* into a hero's style

**Experience sought:** a cherished equipment piece feels like a living relationship between a **particular character's practice**, a **regional workshop's competence**, and a **continuing material history**, rather than a constantly replaced item-level number.

**Source transformation:** [production itemization's item attachment, character-scoped effects and workshop services][ITEM], [Hearth Craft and Memory][HEARTH] and [the Character/Job Identity Floors][TB] make it conceivable that a rebuilt regional workshop keeps an already-loved item relevant through a player-legible, character-compatible alteration. The pleasure is **recognizing the craftsmanship of a living place in the way the hero fights**. It is not transferring another character's immutable signature.

**Prior-art boundary:** long-lived gear, deterministic refocusing, item provenance where applicable, Foundation Reforging and future character-scoped transformative equipment are **ALREADY SELECTED DESIGN ELEMENTS** [T][ITEM][CRAFT]. Package 25 implements a **bounded hero-facing Evergreen Core**, not the full workshop/provenance/three-slot production system. A personally felt *craft-to-character-to-Hearth relationship* is a **REVISED INTERPRETIVE HYPOTHESIS**, not a newly invented crafting system or market.

**Distinctness:** this is **material continuity through artifacts and skilled labor**, not DE-A/05's continuity of a person's biography or DE-A/02's momentary institutional support.

## 3. Why these are materially different, rather than seven versions of one feature

| Local candidate | What experience changes? | Main time/agency locus | What would erase the difference? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `DE-A/01` | **How a person recognizes danger** | Encounter; character-specific attention | Three numerical loadouts with no difference in noticing/timing |
| `DE-A/02` | **Whose contribution makes victory possible** | Expedition; reciprocal institutional agency | Hearth support displayed only as an anonymous percent buff |
| `DE-A/03` | **Who legitimately chooses/acts** | Story/decision; trust and limits of power | Local experts exist only to applaud the Guardian |
| `DE-A/04` | **Where competence remains visible outside battle** | Ordinary life; embodied vocational continuity | Generic cozy interlude unconnected to what characters know |
| `DE-A/05` | **How the same self can evolve across practices** | Longitudinal biography and reversible choice | Disposable rerolls, identity-erasing Jobs or forced build regrets |
| `DE-A/06` | **How lost institutional support is felt and recovered** | Strain/restoration; resilience under limits | Random stat penalties or compulsory chores without social cause |
| `DE-A/07` | **How a durable tool embodies communal craft** | Material time; workshop/item/character relation | Loot treadmill or cosmetically labeled gear with no meaningful behavior |

**Relationships without premature convergence:** `01` can make `02` or `06` perceptually legible (one character understands support/strain differently). `03` and `04` both depend on real, competent civilians, but they answer **authority** and **peace-time embodiment** questions respectively. `05` and `07` preserve continuity in **person** versus **object**. These cross-links are not a feature proposal to implement all seven; no direction is selected or ranked.

### Deliberately excluded near-duplicates and boundary violations

- **“Choose more classes”** just repeats existing eight-Job and future specialization plans; coefficients do not create different experiences.
- **“Sigrid and Eira get distinct Ranger buttons”** repeats their already specified Intervention Window versus Forecast; the exploratory difference, if any, must be player *attention in regional circumstances*.
- **“Increase settlement XP/affection to empower builds”** conflicts with Hearth's concrete-domain authority and the [companion relationship's non-affection-score decision][MEM]; it is not an acceptable easy substitute for civic or interpersonal meaning.
- **“Equip Sigrid's signature on the Guardian / copy a full Job”** violates the accepted Character and Job Identity Floors and single bounded Legacy Technique.
- **“Give a different ending to every loadout”** conflicts with the economy of narrative/History authority and would mistake reversible practice for competing canonical timelines. Neither loadout selection nor Chronos licenses rewriting past events.
- **“Make every Hearth a mandatory activity hub”** conflicts with calm, non-FOMO, low-stress and non-chore promises [T][PX][HEARTH].
- **“Replace support networks with a worldwide political or logistics simulator”** exceeds the current regional model's explicit boundary [NET]; it is not needed to imagine institutional reciprocity.

These exclusions protect **meaningful conceptual difference**, not a claim that no creative work outside these limits is possible.

## 4. Originality, existing design, and unresolved evidence

**Clearly pre-existing:** authored personalities and three signature verbs, flexible responsibility/build hierarchy, Hearth as a capable community, the three regional network capabilities, the first Larkspur ordinary-life circuit, Guardian's delegation arc, reversible character practice, and durable item attachment. Presenting any of those as DE-A inventions would be false.

**Distinct relational hypotheses developed here:** *regional attentional literacy* (`01`); *civilization's contribution as emotionally legible to a particular character/build* (`02`); *player-felt responsibility without monopolized decision rights* (`03`); *chosen practice recognized in the peaceful life it protects* (`04`); *continuity of self across different civic contexts* (`05`); *support absence and recovery as social meaning rather than stat toggling* (`06`); *beloved gear expressing a particular place's craft alongside a character's form of care* (`07`).

These are **candidate experience formulations**, not validated novelty against all Atlas materials. Several deliberately **RESTATE or REVISE already imagined design**, and none supplies a source-backed newly desired **product goal**. Atlas's sources establish ingredients and constraints; they do not establish player demand, a finished implementation or automated knowledge-graph creative causation.

**Unresolved creative territory, without beginning a second inquiry:**

- **Player agency vs authored person:** When does experiencing Sigrid/Eira's authentic attention become educational/personal instead of just losing freedom to optimize? Their full solo direct-control and deep builds are not yet available.
- **Civic legibility without burden:** What minimum contextual feedback lets players recognize the road crew, archive and healers behind a support effect without making preparation an unwanted tutorial or an obligation to perform gratitude?
- **Real conflict among good values:** Could safeguarding a route, preserving dangerous knowledge and meeting relief needs produce honest tradeoffs without forcing faction locks, politically simplistic outcomes or a global civic simulation?
- **Region contrast:** Lantern Vale is a warm first region. The same characters might encounter other cultures with different practices of continuity; the requested source set does not authorize inventing those cultures.
- **Peaceful expression vs gameplay scope:** Does a build-specific ordinary-life response create real player meaning, or just branch-text bloat? The implemented morning circuit is not durable evidence of how players feel.
- **Stressed-support viability:** Could making support legible inadvertently punish low-attention builds or cause difficulty swings outside the intended equality of viable play?
- **Companions and authority:** A three-seat human/AI party and companion memory already exist, but controller identity must not replace authored character identity; deeper co-op social recognition is outside the proof examined here.
- **Actual novelty/benefit:** A full-source coverage comparison and players' experience would be needed to know which of these are genuinely unexplored, wanted or feasible. There was no gameplay run or human response in this inquiry.

## 5. Intellectual contribution and justified stopping

The principal intellectual contribution is a shift from “characters have different builds and Hearths grant preparations” to **three participants in heroic capability**:

> **An authored person supplies a distinctive way of taking responsibility; a legal build supplies an evolving practice; a capable Hearth supplies other people's continuing labor and expertise.**

The source-established constraints produce more interesting speculative questions than “how many builds?”: **What does this person notice? Whose work allows them to succeed? When should they refrain from taking control? What remains true outside combat? What is felt when the civic network strains?** These questions differ in attention, agency, context and time rather than equipment combinations.

**Why this bounded Divergent Exploration is complete:** the inquiry spans materially different *perceptual, institutional, ethical, peaceful, biographical, vulnerability and material* experience frames; each has identifiable sources, a player-experience claim, nearest prior ideas, limits and non-duplicate relationship. Further iterations of “another Job + another preparation + another village” would mostly vary surface mechanisms without changing the question. This is **local sufficiency** for this exact inquiry, **not** a Coverage Assessment of Atlas's entire possibility space or a claim of global diminishing returns.

**One next warranted inquiry, NOT EXECUTED:** a *bounded Coverage and Diminishing-Returns Assessment* of these seven experience hypotheses against **Atlas's already authored character/Hearth/Saga proposals**, especially where `01`, `03`, `05` and `07` may simply reframe existing directions. Its decision question would be: *Which genuinely different player-experience dimensions does this DE-A landscape add after preserving the existing source meanings, and which apparently new ideas collapse to established designs or leave important experience dimensions unexplored?* That would test distinctness before a Convergent Evaluation or Focused Depth. A new Divergent pass would be less valuable without a missing dimension; ranking/selection now would be premature. No subsequent inquiry is started here.
