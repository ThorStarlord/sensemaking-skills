## 3. Inquiry orientation

**Active operation:** DIVERGENT / PRODUCT EXPERIENCE, bounded to the author journey from provisional intention and creative deliberation through holistic working revision and Realization.

**Governing product commitments:** Author retains canonical authority; generated intentions and architectural proposals are not accepted story state; the graph should assist creative authorship without becoming a required manual data-entry procedure.

**Open question:** Is continuity merely the transfer of instructions and source identities, or could the existing connected narrative knowledge change what the author understands, desires or chooses creatively?

**Revisable premises:** That the same authorial emotional target should survive all scenes; that ambiguity is always a problem to resolve; that agents should always validate an articulated creative intention; that concrete scene work only executes a prior architecture.

**Premature work:** Selecting a winner, assigning implementation priority, treating a story hypothesis as user demand, claiming an existing feature is missing, inventing accepted story facts, or altering Auteur's product direction.

## 4. Product-level reframe and candidate expressions

The *local mechanism* is already strong enough for a documented experimental creative thread: keeping alternatives and intentions visible through revision/Realization. Repeating proposals for a better anchor, another selector or additional source IDs would mostly optimize that mechanism. The broader question is instead: **Could concrete storytelling make an author discover a different desirable narrative end?** The following candidates change distinct aspects of that question.

### C1 — The author discovers that the original desire was incomplete

**Proposed experience:** After examining a concrete scene realization, the author decides that their intended reader emotion or narrative purpose needs to change—not because the agent lost context, but because the developing story reveals a more meaningful desired experience.

**Source-grounded transformation:** The current intention status/lineage and author-directed revision owner ([continuity assessment](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/research/2026-11-06-creative-intention-continuity-assessment.md)) connect to emotional Realization anchors and the Identity/Structure/Realization distinction ([architecture](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/narrative-architecture.md)). Reinterpret those links from *preserving the old end* to *helping the author articulate a changed end*. A scene that is hypothesized to read as exposition rather than dread is a useful prompt to inquire, **not** a measured reader reaction.

**Difference:** P6 explores rehearsing an intended reader experience; P11 preserves creative purpose across time. C1 asks whether the *purpose itself* should be revised after creation. The authored intention is not inferred or changed automatically.

**Status:** PROPOSED different author outcome within this bounded inspection; actual novelty and usefulness **NOT_ASSESSED**.

### C2 — Creative ambiguity is something worth preserving

**Proposed experience:** An author knowingly carries two incompatible interpretations of a relationship or motivation forward because preserving that ambiguity generates interesting story meaning, not because they forgot to select a winner.

**Source-grounded transformation:** Source-supported inquiry alternatives and explicit adoption ([thread evaluation](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/research/2026-11-06-connected-creative-thread-evaluation.md)) intersect with the graph's first-class relationships ([design](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-unified-agent-interactive-creative-graph-design.md)) and the distinction between provisional and accepted meaning ([mission](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/MISSION.md)). Reframe alternatives from *inputs awaiting choice* to *productive, explicitly unresolved interpretations*.

**Difference:** Earlier P2 is question-centered navigation and P8 is comparing whole architectures. C2's desired experience is **deliberate sustained interpretive openness**, without inventing a new canonical state or requiring two complete story branches.

**Status:** REVISED neighboring possibility relative to P2/P8; not a distinct curated product identity.

### C3 — Source-grounded creative dissent without loss of authorship

**Proposed experience:** The author can ask an agent to articulate the strongest source-grounded *alternative reading* of a cherished creative intention, making a hidden tradeoff or surprising beneficiary visible, while remaining free to preserve the original design.

**Source-grounded transformation:** The existing Challenge operation and recorded distinction between evidence, alternative and accepted commitment ([thread evaluation](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/research/2026-11-06-connected-creative-thread-evaluation.md), [MISSION](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/MISSION.md)) support an inquiry from *is this mechanism coherent?* to *what other emotional or thematic end might a different interpretation serve?* A current challenge already exists; the different proposed end is informed author agency through productive dissent, not a new Challenge button.

**Difference:** C2 preserves multiple interpretations in the story; C3 asks the agent to expose a consequential alternative to the author's own framing. Neither implies that the agent can veto authorial intent.

**Status:** REVISED application of an existing creative operation; no verified new functionality or author preference.

### C4 — Realization can reframe Structure while the intended end stays stable

**Proposed experience:** A scene's concrete, source-attributed event/relationship evidence prompts reconsideration of the wider story mechanism, while the author's overarching intended reader experience remains unchanged.

**Source-grounded transformation:** The semantic layer distinctions ([architecture](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/narrative-architecture.md)) and implemented scene emotional/relational source anchors ([continuity assessment](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/research/2026-11-06-creative-intention-continuity-assessment.md)) support a hypothetical *bottom-up* learning path: scene insight -> revise the working architecture's explanation -> preserve the author's selected end unless they explicitly change it. This is the reverse of a pure architecture-to-scene delivery framing.

**Difference:** C1 asks whether the **end changes**; C4 keeps the end but reconsiders the **means/structural explanation**. This neighbors P9's manuscript/map continuity, but its hypothesis concerns reverse-direction conceptual learning rather than navigation.

**Status:** REVISED neighboring end/mechanism; not a separately established author need.

A relationship-over-scenes view is already explicit as P4 in the [earlier possibility landscape](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/blob/d0ead25f361a071274ea8456c5947b6da902cede/docs/research/auteur-ui-possibility-landscape.md). Repeating it with a renamed panel or more precise source IDs would not constitute a new possibility. This is a deliberate negative discrimination case.

## 5. Differences, uncertainty and reflective regulation

The five expressions are **not** five selected features. C1 changes the desired end; C2 preserves uncertainty instead of converging; C3 challenges an existing framing; C4 changes a structural explanation without changing the end; N1 is a restatement. The set is a bounded articulation of meanings, not an exhaustive landscape, novelty score or ranked portfolio.

**Local-optimum check:** Improving the Realization anchor transport would remain a reasonable engineering responsibility if requested, but it is no longer the unanswered creative question shown by the November 6 sources. This inquiry explicitly moved from local transport and retention to possible *author learning and reinterpretation* across narrative layers. It returned to source constraints before proposing any future.

**Strong counterargument:** The same author outcomes might already be served by asking a capable coding agent in ordinary language. Adding dedicated UI, schemas or process could turn productive creativity into administrative ceremony. The sources do not show human comprehension or demand. The next discriminating evidence, if a consequential development decision depends on it, is an actual author task showing whether one of these experiences changes a creative decision.

**Stop:** The bounded, source-backed result contains materially differentiated questions and an explicit restatement. More abstract candidate enumeration now is less valuable than an actual author observation. No coverage assessment, ranking, strategy selection, implementation or other repository mutation is warranted by this inquiry.
