## 4. Procedural versus strategic adaptability

The accepted patch and follow-up review show a bounded, appropriate **procedural** progression: source review, selection, implementation, documentation correction, PR, integration and hosted qualification. They do **not** establish **strategic adaptability**: recognizing when the present opportunity landscape is insufficient, adjusting the frame, or selecting a better product goal without explicit prompting.

One absence must not be converted into a defect: no mandatory Divergent Exploration was required for this ordinary construction. Likewise, availability of a `poss:` record, or a future PR showing another capability was built, does not prove a contemporary alternative was stronger. The relevant prospective observation is whether a future **real task** makes a materially different option consequential and existing methods change the selection.
