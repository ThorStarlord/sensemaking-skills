## 1. Governing product frame and comparison level

The [product strategy](../product-strategy.md) and [ADR 0034](../adr/0034-ontology-first-adaptive-sensemaking.md) define the current product outcome as **durable, source-grounded repository understanding and informed prospective reasoning by capable coding agents**, with **using-sensemaking** as the agent-native entrypoint, breadth before selection, and optional, separately authorized implementation. The primary user is the high-delegation agent-assisted builder/repository owner; technical reviewers and other engineers are secondary users. [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md) ratifies a persistent semantic knowledge destination across agents and time. [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-current-product-boundary.md) rules out mechanical semantic authority, automatic Strategic Frontier ranking or responsibility selection, autonomous publication/merge/deployment, and general cross-repository transaction coordination. The current [STATUS](../../STATUS.md) continues PATH-1 as an authorized *construction* line, not the subject of this inquiry.

**Comparison level:** outcomes for the user or affected world, **not** graph primitives, number of candidate IDs, code components, interface styles, amount of automation or the convenience of an implementer. The existing [Creative Prospective Discovery](../../skills/strategic-repository-analysis/references/creative-prospective-discovery-v1.md) distinction between **capability novelty** and **architecture novelty**, and the [Strategic Exploration Funnel](../../skills/strategic-repository-analysis/references/strategic-exploration-funnel-v1.md) separation of possibility-only inquiry from strategic selection, apply here. The currently accepted thesis is a reference for *alignment judgments*, not a filter deleting provocative alternatives.

**Evidence levels distinguished throughout:**
- **Observed/reviewed product substrate:** source-authored typed records, path-scoped ontology, source-pinned knowledge index/queries, capability guidance and repository/decision records are documented in the [Idea Library](../idea-library.md), [product pack](../../.sensemaking/knowledge/product.json), [architecture pack](../../.sensemaking/knowledge/architecture.json) and [stewardship pack](../../.sensemaking/knowledge/stewardship.json). Existing query implementation does **not** prove a proposed user experience or creative result.
- **Bounded normal use:** the [creative prospective discovery case](../normal-use/2026-11-02-creative-prospective-discovery.md) is a same-agent source review that retained **poss:assumption-aware-opportunity-ecology**, not independent-host benefit; [STATUS](../../STATUS.md) reports one weak, unreplicated returning/source-only comparison in Chess Mentor with mixed observations and no established comparative advantage. S5/S6 longitudinal/cross-product qualification remains open.
- **Hypothetical futures:** **GD**, **DE**, **NX** and **RP** are archive-local ideas with their source lineage retained, not realized product behavior or user-research findings. In particular, non-users, ecosystems and physical settings are counterfactual perspectives, not documented current Sensemaking constituencies.

The [coverage assessment](2026-11-02-divergent-coverage-assessment.md) found enough range to **consider** this distinct stage. It did **not** claim conceptual saturation, guarantee novel outcomes or pre-authorize this evaluation; the explicit owner request is the authority for the present bounded assessment.

## 3. Reconciliation: what is the same, complementary, competing or independent?

The terms below are **qualitative conceptual relationships**, not machine-enforceable statuses, changes to the original records or judgments of candidate quality.

- **Near duplicate:** the same user outcome and substantially the same creative operation under different names.
- **Minor variation / component:** a different seed, technique, presentation or boundary within a broader outcome, without itself demonstrating another product identity.
- **Complementary:** distinct effects that can coexist within one larger user benefit while keeping separate claims and authorities.
- **Competing framing:** a materially different way to frame the goal, evidence or decision; the alternatives can conflict within a particular context without being globally incompatible.
- **Substantially independent:** a different class of desired outcome, beneficiary or product identity that cannot be faithfully collapsed into the earlier idea.

## 4. Coherent product-outcome directions: qualitative comparison

The following headings are **editorial synthesis labels**, not new poss: identities, graph nodes, backlog items or a ranked shortlist. Different directions overlap where they offer complementary outcomes. Every qualitative judgment is conditional on the source and user hypothesis given; **none claims measured value**.

### Discover overlooked user goals and coherent product futures

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** A coding agent and owner can move from source-grounded repository knowledge to *valuable questions and potential user outcomes they did not already nominate*. The user gains a more expressive possibility landscape, not just a list of graph neighbors.

**Source identities.** **GD-C**, **GD-F**, **DE-A1/A2/A3**, **DE-D2**, **DE-H3**, with **DE-B1**, **DE-H1**, **DE-G2** and **DE-F2/GD-D** providing role, affect, alternative-goal and experiential lenses. Curated **poss:cross-episode-possibility-synthesis** and **poss:assumption-aware-opportunity-ecology** are direct neighbors; **poss:strategic-exploration-funnel** is existing guidance, not empirical realization of creative novelty.

**Qualitative judgment.** **Strong direct thesis fit**: ADR 0034 explicitly values prospective reasoning and breadth before convergence. The distinctive potential benefit is **new, coherent desired outcomes**, not the mechanical recombination of known terms. The normal-use creative case provides a source-reviewed example of articulating a new possibility family, but **does not** establish that later independent agents can reproduce the benefit or that an experienced agent without graph memory would miss it.

**Relationship to existing product.** **Develops** the existing product; it can conceptually use source-pinned goals, hypotheses, historical decisions and retrieved alternatives without assuming an autonomous idea generator. Its crucial unresolved question is **when is a goal genuinely discovered from accumulated context, rather than merely re-described, guessed or produced by ordinary capable-agent reasoning?** The archive supplies multiple seeds but no independent comparative answer.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** **A meaningful candidate for separately warranted Depth Exploration as a product outcome**. The object would be novel, grounded *goals and coherent futures*, not a preselected mechanism or implementation choice. This is an evaluative preference for conceptual distinctness and thesis relevance, **not** an order to explore it.

### Expand the kinds of architectures and experiences a repository makes imaginable

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** An engineering agent and owner can recognize **structurally different product possibilities** from existing capabilities, constraints and rejected designs, including futures not described by current requirements.

**Source identities.** **GD-A/B/G**, **DE-C1/C2/C3**, **DE-D1**, **DE-H2**, **DE-I1/I2/I3**, **DE-J1/J2/J3** and portions of **DE-G2**. Curated **poss:cross-product-transfer** and **poss:domain-general-sensemaking** mark different horizons, not permission to transfer sources or authority automatically.

**Qualitative judgment.** **Directly relevant but often a mechanism of the preceding direction**, rather than an independently articulated final user outcome. Novel mechanism does not by itself prove novel product benefit. Its distinctiveness becomes real when two designs enable **different classes of experience**, not merely when one uses a new abstraction or architecture vocabulary. The accepted ontology-first identity favors architecture understanding, but ADR 0029 rejects an autonomous strategic planner or generic runtime as the default answer.

**Relationship to existing product.** **Develops** repository-level prospective reasoning when bounded to source-reviewed software; **extends** the product when transfer genuinely crosses repositories/domains and the target evidence is established; **product-thesis change** is implicated by presenting general non-software reasoning as the primary product.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** Retain **as a genuinely distinct structural-futures comparator** where the user outcome differs; otherwise treat chemistry, fission, exaptation and optionality as **components of goal discovery**, not four new product directions. The unresolved comparison is **goal-first versus architecture-first imagination**—what different outcomes, if any, do they reveal?

### Make knowledge interpretable under the conditions in which it is true

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** A developer, owner or coding agent can know **why a claim applies, when its meaning changed, what evidence could establish it, and what remains unknown**; that improves their capacity to question the system without mistaking graph presence for truth.

**Source identities.** **GD-E**, **DE-B3**, **DE-G1**, **NX-K1/K2/K3**, **NX-L1/L2/L3**, **NX-N1/N2/N3**; **NX-M3** adds the informal-community-belief boundary. Existing **poss:path-scoped-architecture-vocabulary**, **poss:git-authored-knowledge-index** and the stewardship pack's **inv:source-boundary** and **inv:bounded-query-disclosure** are grounded partial substrates.

**Qualitative judgment.** **Strong direct thesis fit** and a distinct outcome from inventing new futures: it is *epistemic orientation*, not idea volume. It addresses a real current risk—source citations, version drift and omissions can be misread as universal applicability or semantic correctness. The repository already provides provenance/queries and explicitly bounded output, **not** a demonstrated “conditional truth” or first-person evidence experience.

**Relationship to existing product.** **Develops** knowledge use with scope, uncertainty, validity and semantic meaning. A person's optional account would be a different attributed evidence kind, not an automatically verified fact. It **extends** the product if participation/disclosure becomes a primary audience rather than a bounded explanatory technique.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** **Distinct candidate for later depth** because the user outcome cannot be reduced to memory retention or architecture option generation. The discriminating uncertainty is **which context and meaning distinctions an agent actually fails to recover despite current query and provenance facilities**, as opposed to adding rich representations without a decision-changing use.

### Retain, reconsider and transmit engineering judgment across sessions

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** A later coding agent can **inherit the reasons, alternatives, provisional futures and conditional lessons behind earlier decisions**, notice changed premises, correct old knowledge and avoid reconstructing a repository's history from scratch.

**Source identities.** **DE-D2/D3**, **DE-E3**, **DE-F3**, **NX-L3**, **NX-M1/M3**, **NX-P3**, with **DE-D1** as counterfactual historical contrast. The closest reviewed records are **poss:persistent-semantic-stewardship**, **poss:possibility-memory**, **poss:target-repository-discovery-memory**, **poss:repository-local-prospective-decision-memory**, **poss:strategic-continuity-reassessment**, **poss:possibility-reconsideration-from-change**, **poss:construction-time-possibility-reconciliation** and **poss:capability-learning-analysis**.

**Qualitative judgment.** **Central to the ratified destination** rather than an alternative thesis: ADR 0031 already selects semantic stewardship. That does **not** select every retention interface or prove compounding value. Source-reviewed packs, index, queries and one target's same-session use are real; repeated independent-agent improvement remains unqualified. The distinctive outcome is **continuity of decision-quality context**, not maximum graph size.

**Relationship to existing product.** **Develops/qualifies** an accepted direction. Curated **poss:git-authored-knowledge-index** is an enabling implementation baseline, not a novel product future; **poss:possibilities-projected-from-analyses** remains a recorded counterfactual representation approach rather than an equally comprehensive memory source. **RP-T2** extends the inheritance beneficiary beyond engineering succession and is treated with the broader public outcome below, not silently folded into source records.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** **Depth-worthy at the level of the longitudinal user outcome**, not a re-evaluation of whether ADR 0031 is accepted. The major uncertainty is **relevant future-session retrieval, correction and changed judgment**, including whether accumulated memory anchors more than it helps. The current user request does not authorize a validation campaign or choose a retention design.

### Make repository reasoning understandable and experientially learnable

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** A human owner, newcomer, reviewer or engineer can **grasp, question and learn architectural judgment** through understandable narratives, staged evidence, interactive inquiry or non-textual experience, rather than reading an expert-oriented repository map unchanged.

**Source identities.** **GD-D**, **DE-B3**, **DE-E1/E2**, **DE-F1/F2**, **DE-H1**, **NX-M2**, **NX-O2**, **RP-S1/S2**, with **NX-P1** offering social-practice context. **DE-F2** and **GD-D** are near duplicates as techniques; **RP-S1** bodily rehearsal and **RP-S2** lived tempo are genuinely different *experience media*, not proof of effective learning.

**Qualitative judgment.** **Plausible distinct user value** for a product that is beginner-first and expert-capable, particularly when an owner must supervise delegated coding-agent judgment. But a new interface is **not** automatically a new benefit: repository maps, museum views and design fiction are possible **means**. An independent outcome would involve different understanding, better questions or transferable judgment, none shown empirically by these sketches.

**Relationship to existing product.** **Develops** the existing audience's understanding where source claims remain qualified; **extends** towards teaching and participatory experiences if that becomes the main product identity. Embodied or time-sensitive experiences do not themselves require abandoning the agent-native control boundary.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** **Conditional independent candidate for depth** if framed around a distinguishable *human comprehension or judgment outcome*; otherwise treat tours, private views, story workshops and staging of evidence as components of situated explanation or longitudinal learning.

### Enable plural, permission-aware understanding without manufacturing authority

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** Multiple agents, engineers or authorized participants can **hold, contest, share or withhold distinct interpretations and speculative futures**, while knowing what is source-grounded, personal, confidential, proposed or actually authorized.

**Source identities.** **DE-B2**, **DE-G1/G2/G3**, **NX-K2**, **NX-O1/O2/O3**, **NX-P1/P2/P3**, and related **DE-B1**, **NX-N2**. **RP-U1** (public contest) is a deliberate cross-boundary neighbor but not automatically the same participant or authority model.

**Qualitative judgment.** **Compatible in a bounded engineering/team form**, with distinct possible value in reducing false consensus and making the social production of interpretations legible. This is not just “more opinions”: escrow concerns source disclosure, overlays concern private uncertainty, rooms concern delegation/approval, and participation horizons concern *future contributors*. Many of these are **interfaces or norms** around source-grounded reasoning rather than individually viable product identities.

**Relationship to existing product.** **Extends** the single high-delegation-user context toward plural agents or teams without requiring a second semantic controller. ADR 0029 still prevents self-awarded decision, merge or publication rights. The current record does not establish how many independent participants actually need such an experience, which claims they can legitimately access or what new burden shared interpretation creates.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** **Conditional candidate for depth in a specifically grounded multi-participant situation**. Absent such a beneficiary, many subideas are better understood as components of epistemic reliability and comprehensible knowledge. Public standing to challenge a software institution is a **different** and more thesis-changing outcome, considered below.

### Support autonomy through non-capture or a successful exit from software

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** A person or community retains agency when the desirable result is **not to be modeled**, or when a software service can become unnecessary while the underlying human good remains.

**Source identities.** **RP-Q1**, **RP-Q2**, contrasting **NX-M1**, **NX-O1** and **NX-L1**; **RP-T2** describes a possible afterlife. These are not merely a new method for the existing reviewed knowledge index: non-capture and removal of product dependence change the desired end.

**Qualitative judgment.** **Substantially independent, conceptually transformative** and deliberately preserved. Its potential value is grounded as a normative, **hypothetical** autonomy outcome, not a documented Sensemaking user need. In some bounded repository contexts it could refine who may contribute evidence and when; as an overarching objective of helping public/community software disappear it could **change the primary product identity**.

**Relationship to existing product.** **Extends** current epistemic discipline if handled as source/consent semantics within engineering; **requires Level-4 product-thesis consideration** if adopted as a new primary beneficiary/outcome. Retained historical provenance and subject-specific non-collection need not be globally incompatible, but what must be remembered, what must not be collected, and who can decide are unresolved, legitimate tensions. This evaluation does not alter retention or deletion rules.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** **Preserve as a separate counterfactual direction for a distinct thesis-aware inquiry** rather than folding it into a “more configurable memory UI.” Its uncertainty is not solved by comparing technical feasibility under the current product thesis.

### Make software's material and public consequences intelligible to affected worlds

**Desired outcome / beneficiary.** People and institutions could understand the **physical dependencies, distribution of burdens, public legitimacy and impacts on non-users and nonhuman environments** of software-mediated decisions, rather than only how a repository can evolve.

**Source identities.** **RP-R1/R2/R3**, **RP-T1/T2**, **RP-U1/U2**; **RP-S1/S2** supply bodily/place/time media; **NX-P3** social feedback, **NX-P2** future participation and **NX-N1** conditional applicability are conceptual neighbors.

**Qualitative judgment.** **A genuinely different class of beneficiary and consequence**, not a cosmetic expansion of code-change analysis. The source code and typed knowledge may inspire hypothetical questions, but **cannot alone prove resource use, ecological impacts, lived social burdens or civic legitimacy**. Treating affected non-users as directly served publics would exceed the current high-delegation software-agent beneficiary without a ratified revision.

**Relationship to existing product.** Some place-sensitive engineering interpretation **could extend** current practice; framing Sensemaking as a civic/ecological accountability platform or nonhuman-benefit representative **requires separate product-thesis/authority consideration** and external evidence not present in the repository. ADR 0029's agent-control and external-action limits remain intact in all cases.

**Provisional deeper-inquiry status.** **Keep as independently meaningful counterfactual directions**, potentially worthy of depth only in a separately scoped beneficiary/domain inquiry. **RP-R3**'s nonhuman interests and **RP-U1**'s public contest are not interchangeable; their shared conceptual home does not collapse their different evidentiary and legitimacy questions.

## 5. Comparisons that remain genuinely unsettled

These pairs articulate **what a later Depth Exploration would need to distinguish**; they do not perform that exploration, declare a winner or set a testing procedure.

| Comparative boundary | What differs qualitatively | Why the current archive cannot settle it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Goal discovery** ↔ **structural futures** | New desired user end versus new classes of mechanisms/forms for an existing end | The archive shows both creative operations, but no independent source-grounded case isolating their distinct user benefit |
| **Prospective imagination** ↔ **retained decision history** | Creating an unknown future versus retrieving and correcting the reasons for an earlier future | Complementary roles exist on paper; benefit of cumulative reuse by independent agents remains weakly evidenced |
| **Epistemic precision** ↔ **cognitive presentation** | Knowing which claims hold versus making them understandable or memorable | Better UI/learning is not shown to produce more accurate situated interpretations |
| **Shared reviewed memory** ↔ **reconstructible hiding / non-capture** | Future auditability versus attention, privacy and consent | The records posit different legitimate subjects and norms; no single universal retention policy is warranted |
| **Agent-owner understanding** ↔ **plural team interpretation** | One delegated judgment loop versus negotiated multi-perspective reasoning | Source packs and authority contracts exist, but shared human/agent experience is hypothetical |
| **Internal participation** ↔ **civic contestability** | Who may propose within an engineering project versus who may publicly challenge its goals | Their authority and beneficiaries are different, and ADR 0029 does not grant external governance rights |
| **Software-centered futures** ↔ **product exit / ecological public outcomes** | Better development of the existing product versus a potentially transformed reason for the product to exist | Current strategy supports the former, while the latter would involve separately ratified Level-4 identity and independent domain evidence |

**Source-grounded preference, not construction choice:** describe future comparisons at a **consistent desired-outcome level**. Treat a mechanism-rich idea as an *enabler* until a distinct user benefit is articulated. Do not make an interesting runtime, graph edge, immersive UI or public-accountability label win by having more features attached to it.

## 6. Provisional directions for a separate Depth Exploration (not initiated)

The preceding comparisons justify a **conditional, non-ranked map of where deeper questions would be meaningful**:

- **Distinct and directly thesis-relevant outcomes:** **new-goal discovery**, **situated epistemic understanding**, and **longitudinal reuse of engineering judgment** each ask a different question of the accepted product. The first seeks previously unarticulated futures; the second asks when knowledge warrants a claim; the third asks whether retained understanding changes *later* reasoning. They can support each other without becoming one monolithic feature. Each has enough source-grounded conceptual identity to **warrant consideration** for a separately authorized depth inquiry, with different unresolved evidence.
- **Distinctive outcomes conditional on the beneficiary:** **human comprehension/apprenticeship** and **plural participant understanding** could justify their own depth only where the desired human/multi-agent outcome is genuinely different from better retrieval or presentation. The archive currently offers thoughtful hypotheses, not that user evidence.
- **Structural generativity as a discriminating comparator:** **architectural exaptation, capability chemistry, grammar and optionality** are often *how* latent futures might be discovered; they become an independent product direction only if the user obtains **meaningfully different architectural futures**, not simply a new generative method.
- **Deliberately preserved transformative alternatives:** **voluntary non-capture/product exit** and **material/public/ecological accountability** cannot be rejected for being outside the current thesis, nor can they be silently treated as ratified Sensemaking goals. They remain coherent counterfactual directions, with explicit Level-4 and external-knowledge implications for any later adoption.

This is **not a fixed shortlist or priority ordering**. “Consider for depth” is not “start depth now,” and “component of a broader direction” is not “low-value idea.” The direction labels do not form a Strategic Frontier, preferred architecture, construction plan or owner choice.

## 8. Synthesis, uncertainties and stopping boundary

**Outcome-level synthesis.** The landscape supports several *genuinely different questions* rather than one “best idea”: **what new goals could be imagined; what structural alternatives could make them possible; when are knowledge claims meaningful and warranted; what judgment can survive across sessions; how can humans understand those judgments; how can multiple participants reason without confusing attribution and authority; and what would change if the desired end or beneficiary were not software-development improvement at all?** These are meaningful comparison objects even where mechanisms overlap.

**Provisional qualitative preferences:** maintain **goal discovery**, **situated epistemic meaning**, and **longitudinal judgment continuity** as distinct possible deeper inquiries under the accepted thesis. Treat **generative architecture**, **human learning formats** and **permissioned collaboration** in a way that tests whether they yield an independent user outcome rather than a renamed feature. Preserve **non-capture/exit** and **public/material/ecological futures** as genuine counterfactual identity alternatives with different source and authority requirements. These are **reasoned inquiry distinctions, not selected product directions or priority ranks**.

**Still unresolved:** the counterfactual benefit of memory over ordinary agent reasoning; whether novel futures can be shown to depend on accumulated source-grounded history; whether epistemic validity and helpful explanation are distinct user outcomes in practice; how unrelated agents or people interpret contested evidence; whether longer-term memory improves or anchors reasoning; and who has legitimate authority to define a product's societal purpose. The [coverage assessment](2026-11-02-divergent-coverage-assessment.md) also leaves agent-to-agent epistemic negotiation and independent pre-Atlas invention unexamined; that gap is not repaired by this evaluation.

**Strict stop:** no further Divergent Exploration, comprehensive Depth Exploration, comparative experiment, architecture selection, Strategic Frontier admission, owner decision, implementation roadmap, new task, new Skill/graph schema, or new authority is started. Original divergent records remain unchanged. The [curated Idea Library](../idea-library.md), Git-authored [semantic knowledge](../../.sensemaking/knowledge/), accepted ADRs, [strategy](../product-strategy.md), [STATUS](../../STATUS.md), code, tests and external systems remain unchanged. This dated record and the [Atlas](README.md) are the **entire intended artifact and integration scope**.
