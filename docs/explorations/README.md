## Evidence inquiry: qualifying creative goal discovery without executing a trial

The separately requested [Evidence and Qualification Inquiry](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md) asks what the **smallest decision-changing evidence** would be for an independent coding agent discovering a genuinely new, source-faithful, owner-relevant product end **because accumulated knowledge helped**, rather than recalling history, improving implementation reasoning or inventing architecture. It reconciles [existing qualified and unqualified findings](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md#3-existing-evidence-what-is-actually-established-and-contradicted), [competing explanations](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md#4-competing-explanations-and-distinguishable-observations) and [proportionate evidence options](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md#5-qualification-alternatives-under-experiment-economy).

**Bounded recommendation, not a launch:** the [smallest discriminating option](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md#6-smallest-credible-next-inquiry) is **one matched qualitative natural-use comparison only if a genuinely unfamiliar, open owner/product question and independent review are available**. A solo independent-agent session can support feasibility, but not comparative benefit. Even a positive pair would be *local* evidence of knowledge-assisted discovery, not proof of general advantage or that typed graph links were the necessary cause. No current target question, test, campaign, product intervention, schema or qualification result is created here.

## How to read candidate identities and statuses

- **GD-A through GD-G:** exploration-local names for provisional families in the generative-depth record.
- **DE-A1 through DE-J3:** exploration-local names for candidate capabilities in the first divergent record.
- **NX-K1 through NX-P3:** exploration-local names for the second divergent record's 18 hypothetical candidates. K–P are newly opened families; multi-family membership is explicitly allowed.
- **RP-Q1 through RP-U2:** exploration-local names for the third divergent record's 11 hypothetical candidates. Q–U are additional overlapping families, not new ontology entities; the third pass also documents near-duplicates *without* assigning them new IDs.
- They are *navigation labels only*, not claim IDs, `poss:` entries, `arch:` options, `conseq:` hypotheses, schema entities, or implementation tickets.
- Every candidate is **hypothetical** unless its source is separately identified as observed. A plausible use of existing code is not an implemented or verified capability.
- Newness is **provisional and bounded**: `RESTATED`, `REVISED`, `NEW`, or `NOT_ASSESSED` requires comparison with specific earlier ideas, not the number of ideas written. The records make differences explicit without declaring global novelty.
- `NOT_ASSESSED` coverage does not mean missing capability, rejected idea or violation of the current ontology.

## Separation of responsibilities

1. **Exploration record:** preserves the originating inquiry, its conceptual moves, source inspiration, ideas, overlap and gaps. Do not silently rewrite its historical claims after later work; add a later record or dated correction.
2. **This atlas:** links passes and groups related topics. It can be updated for navigation without treating a topic as strategically important.
3. **Coverage assessment:** reads across completed passes to distinguish coverage, restatement, novelty and unexplored lenses; it does not create candidates, judge their quality, authorize a transition or set policy.
4. **Convergent evaluation:** compares the different product outcomes and how candidate identities relate, while retaining their origins; provisional preferences are **not** decisions or depth findings.
5. **Depth inquiry prioritization:** provisionally compares the *remaining value of understanding* established outcome directions; it does not rank builds, select a product or automatically start another inquiry.
6. **Inquiry process reconciliation:** audits how the original inquiries served their own stated questions, clarifies adaptive inquiry modes and consequence-depth expectations, and recommends only targeted changes without retroactively overwriting history.
7. **Focused depth records:** investigate one already-identified outcome's mechanisms, competing explanations, risks, hypothetical cases and unqualified evidence without selecting a final product direction or architecture.
8. **Evidence and qualification inquiry:** review existing observations and competing explanations, recommend the least costly future evidence that could change a decision, and stop before any actual qualification, experiment or campaign absent separate authority.
9. **[Curated Idea Library](../idea-library.md):** selectively retains nonduplicative ProductPossibilities with evidence/provenance and disposition history, linked to the reviewed knowledge pack where warranted. No automatic promotion from an exploration ID.
10. **Decisions, strategic analyses and ADRs:** independently govern selection and authorization. An exploratory possibility is never a construction path, policy update or approval merely because it was documented.
11. **Method reflection:** preserves observations about reasoning *quality and format*, separately from the creative/product ideas.

## Continuation without a mandatory pipeline

A later agent may begin a new inquiry with a different angle: unrepresented user experiences, alternative assumptions, analogy failure, conceptual contradiction, historical revision, or a deliberately independent first pass before looking at this index. It should compare genuinely distinct additions to previous candidates, preserve original sources, and say which areas remain unexplored. This is an *optional reading convention*, not a new process phase or fixed number of passes.

A divergence-only user request ends with an expanded landscape. Moving toward Convergent Evaluation requires a separately warranted choice to change the inquiry mode; neither saturation, catalog growth, the coverage assessment nor documentation creates automatic selection authority. The subsequently owner-requested [Convergent Evaluation](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md) records that distinct inquiry only; it does not make Depth Exploration, final selection or construction automatic. The separately owner-requested [goal-discovery Depth Exploration](2026-11-03-depth-new-product-goals-from-knowledge.md) examines one direction without authorizing further modes or action. The separately requested [evidence inquiry](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md) recommends a *conditional* smallest evidence-producing comparison; it does not authorize, select a target for or run that comparison. The separate [inquiry prioritization](2026-11-03-depth-exploration-prioritization.md) ranks potential *further conceptual investigations*, not their implementation or automatic initiation. The later [Inquiry Process Reconciliation](2026-11-03-inquiry-process-reconciliation.md) clarifies how a specific premise, its traced consequences and its remaining intellectual unknowns govern *future* depth sufficiency; it does not re-run the completed phases. Qualitative signs of diminishing new conceptual diversity can inform that conversation, but they are not a scoring rule or automatic trigger.

## Evidence and authority ceiling

- Repository facts, historical engineering outcomes and cited code behavior should be checked against their original revision-bound sources.
- The first 2026-11-02 conversation explorations are conceptual syntheses; the second and third are separate source-reviewed agent-authored divergent passes. None demonstrates independent-host invention, cross-session creative reuse, graph completeness or comparative benefit.
- The divergent records document **possibilities**; the dated coverage assessment describes **breadth**; the convergent evaluation compares **potential product outcomes**; the focused depth report investigates **one outcome's plausible causal mechanisms and missing evidence**; and the evidence inquiry explains **what an independent comparative observation would need to establish**. The newer provisional prioritization allocates *possible future depth effort* without adding product, architectural or authority commitments. The separate reconciliation identifies a narrower **consequence-depth completeness** and **inquiry-target specificity** question without claiming any previously merged report was exhaustive or incorrect under its own original mandate. None executes a qualification, introduces a Skill/query/schema or review gate, promotes canonical `poss:` knowledge, automatically ranks, authorizes implementation or changes a Level-3/Level-4 commitment.
