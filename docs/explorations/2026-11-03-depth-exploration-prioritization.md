## 1. Governing frame, comparison level and method

**Priority Framing as inspected:** [product strategy](../product-strategy.md), [ADR 0034](../adr/0034-ontology-first-adaptive-sensemaking.md), [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md), [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-current-product-boundary.md), [CONTEXT](../../CONTEXT.md) and [STATUS](../../STATUS.md) are the actual governing sources. The earlier [coverage assessment](2026-11-02-divergent-coverage-assessment.md#1-source-priority-framing-and-authority) found no separate authoritative artifact formally titled "Priority Framing"; this inquiry does not invent such a policy. The selected product identity is ontology-grounded repository understanding and prospective reasoning by capable software-development agents, with persistent source-qualified knowledge across time and agents. Implementation is optional and separate. Source facts, owner commitments and agent hypotheses have distinct authority.

**Comparison set:** the **same eight outcome-level directions** named in the [existing Convergent Evaluation, section 4](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md#4-coherent-product-outcome-directions-qualitative-comparison). That evaluation's [identity crosswalk](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md#7-provenance-preserving-identity-crosswalk) remains the traceability authority for all seven generative-depth family labels, 59 DE/NX/RP candidate labels, and 19 curated poss: identities. This document neither reproduces that catalogue nor turns those labels into independent products. Two directions describe potentially **different product ends and beneficiaries** outside the current thesis; they are retained in an explicitly non-comparable transformative frontier rather than assigned a misleading low universal rank.

**Five qualitative lenses, applied together rather than scored:**

1. **Consequential potential:** could understanding conditional consequences change how an agent or owner frames the product, not merely elaborate a UI?
2. **Unresolved depth:** how much important consequence/interaction uncertainty remains after the *already completed* inquiries?
3. **Strategic relevance:** would understanding the matter inform accepted ontology-first, source-grounded, agent-owned reasoning, or require an explicit thesis change?
4. **Inquiry tractability:** is there enough authentic, source-reviewed material to distinguish meaningful hypothetical consequences *without fabricating user needs or claiming empirical proof*?
5. **Portfolio contribution:** would the inquiry teach something substantially different from completed goal-discovery depth and qualification reasoning, accepted stewardship design, or the other directions?

**Decision rule:** give precedence to **decision-changing, remaining conceptual learning** at the present source boundary, not to initial attractiveness, prior favorable discussion, raw uncertainty, candidate counts, implementation ease, or speculative market size. Uncertainty is useful when an inquiry could expose its consequences; uncertainty whose only resolution needs unavailable independent user/domain evidence is not a reason to write another speculative analysis. The numbered order below is therefore a **reversible inquiry agenda under the current thesis**, not a scalar comparison of product merit. Adjacent orders are arguable; major comparisons are made explicit in section 4.

**Important consequence of recent work:** [new-goal discovery already received focused Depth Exploration](2026-11-03-depth-new-product-goals-from-knowledge.md), followed by a separate [evidence-warrant inquiry](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md). These clarified causal hypotheses and found no established comparative creative benefit. Repeating the conceptual inquiry now has lower *marginal* learning value than opening a genuinely unexamined outcome, even though new-goal discovery is strongly aligned with the product purpose.

**Status of evidence:** existing Git-authored knowledge packs, versioned claims, retrieval/query guidance and decision history are reviewed product substrates, *not* proof that hypothetical experiences work. The Chess Mentor returning/source-only comparison showed one historical correction with a countervailing source-only finding; Atlas reported no new ProductPossibility and no cross-session reuse; Sensemaking source-link repair improved reachability, not observed creative invention. See [Evidence Inquiry, section 3](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md#3-existing-evidence-what-is-actually-established-and-contradicted). Nothing in this ranking changes those ceilings.

### Rank 1 — Make knowledge contextually trustworthy

**Trace and established base:** [Convergent Evaluation's epistemic outcome](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md#make-knowledge-interpretable-under-the-conditions-in-which-it-is-true) traces **GD-E, DE-B3, DE-G1, NX-K1/K2/K3, NX-L1/L2/L3, NX-N1/N2/N3**, with **NX-M3** as a neighbor. Existing **poss:path-scoped-architecture-vocabulary**, **poss:git-authored-knowledge-index**, and the reviewed stewardship source/omission invariants already ground conditional provenance and bounded queries. Their existence does **not** demonstrate an experience of situated validity.

**Unresolved consequential question:** if two records are each faithful to their evidence but apply at different revisions, environments, roles or semantic meanings, when is "contradiction" the wrong interpretation, and how could that distinction affect a later prospective judgment? A second tension is whether too much conditionality overwhelms the person/agent who needs a bounded decision. Depth could trace consequences of retaining, challenging or withholding applicability claims without proposing a new schema.

**Why first / tractability:** highly consequential to epistemic reliability *and* to every other direction that would use historical information creatively; already-grounded revision/authority/source examples permit meaningful conditional reasoning now. It precedes rank 2 because clarity about claim scope can make disagreements legible, whereas plural-agent participation introduces additional unobserved actors and consent premises. **Thesis:** direct development, not proof of a new graph capability.

**Change the priority if:** a source-grounded review demonstrates the existing contract already answers the exact semantic-applicability question, making another depth pass repetitive; or a new independent agent episode exposes a more consequential unmodeled knowledge-sharing failure than situated validity.

### Rank 2 — Enable plural, permission-aware reasoning

**Trace and established base:** **DE-B2, DE-G1/G2/G3, NX-K2, NX-O1/O2/O3, NX-P1/P2/P3**, with **DE-B1/NX-N2** as neighbors. [Divergent II](2026-11-02-divergent-repository-knowledge-second-pass.md) separates private interpretive overlays, conditional disclosure and delegation-shaped rooms. [Coverage Assessment, section 5](2026-11-02-divergent-coverage-assessment.md#5-remaining-territory-and-the-meaning-of-unexplored) names **agent-to-agent epistemic negotiation** as substantively underexplored. ADRs 0029/0031 preserve one active semantic controller and distinguish knowing, proposing and deciding.

**Unresolved consequential question:** if independent agents have different source snapshots and conflicting, well-attributed interpretations, what follows for shared understanding, disagreement, revisability and responsibility without turning a shared graph into an authority oracle? Relevant consent/disclosure questions are distinct from structural access permissions; no new permission system is assumed.

**Why second / tractability:** unusually high **novel portfolio contribution** beyond prior self/one-agent depth. Existing source, revision, attribution and authority boundaries give a credible bounded hypothetical two-agent situation, but no real multi-participant user demand is established. It sits above rank 3 because longitudinal memory has a ratified architecture and more prior analysis, while the consequences of **concurrent distinct interpreters** remain less developed. **Thesis:** possible engineering-agent extension, not an accepted collaborative product pivot.

**Change the priority if:** an authentic multiple-agent disagreement or shared-context incident shows a decision-changing failure, which could move it above rank 1; conversely if the product remains strictly single-agent in the relevant setting and no differentiated beneficiary can be stated, defer focused depth until that premise changes.

### Rank 3 — Preserve engineering judgment across sessions

**Trace and established base:** **DE-D2/D3, DE-E3, DE-F3, NX-L3, NX-M1/M3, NX-P3**, with curated **poss:persistent-semantic-stewardship**, **poss:possibility-memory**, **poss:strategic-continuity-reassessment**, **poss:target-repository-discovery-memory**, **poss:repository-local-prospective-decision-memory**, **poss:possibility-reconsideration-from-change**, **poss:construction-time-possibility-reconciliation** and **poss:capability-learning-analysis**. [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md) already ratifies the destination; [STATUS](../../STATUS.md) and the [evidence inquiry](2026-11-03-evidence-inquiry-creative-goal-discovery.md) describe limited, non-generalizing reuse observations.

**Unresolved consequential question:** when a later agent receives correct historical reasoning built on now-invalid premises, should the history act as a guardrail, a hypothesis to reopen, or a source of anchoring? What intellectual work does reconstruction across distinct sessions preserve that source-only reading might miss, and which consequences can be understood *without* claiming empirical superiority?

**Why third / tractability:** very high strategic fit and grounded historical cases. Yet [product strategy](../product-strategy.md), knowledge architecture, the [Idea Library](../idea-library.md) and normal-use reports have already treated continuity extensively. A **specific conditional temporal-consequence** question could add learning; another general essay that memory matters would not. It follows rank 2 for marginal novelty, but precedes rank 4 because the repository contains more attributable premises from which to trace distinctive consequences. **Thesis:** accepted destination under examination, not a reconsideration of its ratification.

**Change the priority if:** a later independent-agent episode shows memory materially changing or misdirecting a decision; reconsider the exact follow-up question before drafting another conceptual depth report. A simple memory-retrieval success alone does not automatically elevate the ranking.

### Rank 4 — Make reasoning understandable and learnable

**Trace and established base:** **GD-D, DE-B3, DE-E1/E2, DE-F1/F2, DE-H1, NX-M2, NX-O2, RP-S1/S2**, with **NX-P1** as context. The [Convergent Evaluation](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md#make-repository-reasoning-understandable-and-experientially-learnable) already distinguishes learning judgment from a display format; GD-D/DE-F2 partly restate the same hypothetical rehearsal method.

**Unresolved consequential question:** suppose an owner supervises a delegated architecture decision but cannot identify which assumptions are provisional. Could a layered explanation, adversarial learning encounter or participatory rehearsal create *a different capacity to question the reasoning*, rather than simply more readable documentation? What new omissions or false confidence could such an experience introduce?

**Why fourth / tractability:** the potential human outcome is genuinely different from graph retrieval and authority policy, making it a worthwhile addition to the portfolio. The engineering owner/reviewer beneficiary is in scope, but the archive provides hypothetical interactions rather than independent comprehension evidence. It follows rank 3 because source-backed temporal judgment is more directly investigable, and precedes rank 5 because **better human judgment** is a clearer separate end than most architectural creativity mechanisms. **Thesis:** develops existing owner understanding, may extend toward education if that becomes the main purpose.

**Change the priority if:** real owner/reviewer confusion becomes consequential despite correct citations, elevating depth; or the proposed outcome collapses on examination into "show the same information differently" without an independent judgment change, reducing the case for a separate inquiry.

### Rank 5 — Expand architectural possibilities

**Trace and established base:** **GD-A/B/G, DE-C1/C2/C3, DE-D1, DE-H2, DE-I1/I2/I3, DE-J1/J2/J3** and **DE-G2**, with curated **poss:cross-product-transfer** as an adjacent horizon. **Unresolved consequential question:** with an owner-desired outcome held constant, could architectural options yield qualitatively different classes of user experience and future freedom, rather than merely different implementations? What consequences follow from reusing declined patterns with their original rejection premises still in force?

**Why fifth / tractability:** there is sufficient existing architecture/alternative/decision source to examine this cleanly, and the distinction between outcome and method could meaningfully change the next conceptual question. But the prior archive has extensively explored similar structural moves and near-duplicates (e.g. **GD-G/DE-J3**). It follows rank 4 because its *independent outcome status* remains conditional; it precedes rank 6 only because its critical structure-first versus goal-first distinction has **not** received a dedicated focused Depth Exploration. **Thesis:** part of existing prospective reasoning when truly outcome-distinct; otherwise a supporting capability.

**Change the priority if:** a real, source-reviewed architectural comparison exposes a different user end or a previously unimagined class of feasible experience; decrease separate depth priority if every example is a known goal with a renamed design.

### Rank 6 — Discover overlooked product goals

**Trace and established base:** **GD-C/F, DE-A1/A2/A3, DE-D2, DE-H3**, aided by **DE-B1, DE-H1, DE-G2**, and curated **poss:cross-episode-possibility-synthesis** / **poss:assumption-aware-opportunity-ecology**. Their Chess Mentor, Atlas and Sensemaking examples distinguish retrieved goals, features, architectures and **new beneficiary-level ends**; they do **not** demonstrate independent comparative benefit.

**Unresolved consequential question:** the next materially open premise is primarily **empirical**: will accumulated memory materially change an independent agent's source-faithful, owner-relevant *new* goal compared with ordinary source-only reasoning? The evidence inquiry proposes a conditional matched natural-use pair only if an authentic open product question and independent review arise. This prioritization **does not authorize that pair**.

**Why sixth / tractability:** strong thesis fit, but another abstract Depth Exploration now risks reiterating the mechanisms and failure modes already documented. Earlier favorable attention does not entitle it to the first inquiry slot. Rank 6 is a *pause on more conceptual depth*, **not** a low product-value rating or a negative result. **Thesis:** direct development of ontology-first prospective reasoning.

**Change the priority if:** an authentic independent-agent observation exposes a *new conceptual consequence or mechanism* not handled by the completed depth report; that would justify a new, specific depth question. An unrun comparison or merely another invented vignette does **not**.

## 3. Transformative directions: parallel, non-comparable frontier

The following are **not discarded or ranked below** the six in-scope directions. Their counterfactual beneficiary/goal premises differ so much that a single linear ordinal comparison would smuggle a product-thesis preference into an inquiry ranking. They are coherent horizons **for separately scoped, thesis-aware Depth Exploration**, not automatic Level-4 proposals or new accepted audiences.

### T1 — Support non-capture and voluntary software exit

**Trace:** **RP-Q1/RP-Q2**, with **RP-T2, NX-M1, NX-O1, NX-L1** as related contrasts. [Divergent III](2026-11-02-divergent-remaining-possibility-space.md#family-q---successful-exit-and-the-right-to-remain-outside-knowledge) explicitly distinguishes *not collecting a claim* from hiding or conditionally sharing a stored one; *no longer needing a product* differs from preserving its promise in a new interface.

**Consequence and unresolved depth:** if not being modeled is a legitimate end, what follows for an evidence-grounded history system that also promises provenance and reconstruction? Could successful exit be intelligible without silently altering legitimate record retention, owner authority or consent? There is high conceptual potential, but no source-grounded evidence of an affected beneficiary requiring this experience **from Sensemaking**. An inquiry could map incompatible premises and possible coexistence, not choose a retention policy.

**Priority-changing condition / thesis:** rises to a live, separately considered Level-4 inquiry if an actual user/owner goal explicitly calls for non-capture or exit, or a real source-grounded consent conflict arises. In the current software-agent thesis it is not commensurable with improving agent claim validity. It is **NOT YET PRIORITIZED FOR AN IN-SCOPE DEPTH PASS**, not rejected.

### T2 — Address material, ecological and public consequences

**Trace:** **RP-R1/R2/R3, RP-T1/T2, RP-U1/U2**, with **RP-S1/S2, NX-P2/P3** as creative bridges. [Divergent III](2026-11-02-divergent-remaining-possibility-space.md#family-r---material-and-ecological-entanglement) and the [Convergent Evaluation](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md#make-softwares-material-and-public-consequences-intelligible-to-affected-worlds) treat affected non-users, resource dependencies and public contest as genuinely different possible ends, not a generic extra impact column.

**Consequence and unresolved depth:** if a software product is evaluated through physical conditions and affected people who never use it, what follows for the definition of a relevant product goal, legitimate evidence and whose interests the agent may speak for? Important consequences could transform the product identity, but repository-source inspection alone cannot establish material footprints, ecological effects, public standing or community desires. A legitimate depth question would need a specified beneficiary and external-evidence boundary, not manufactured numeric impacts.

**Priority-changing condition / thesis:** rises within a *separately scoped* product-identity inquiry if the owner advances such a constituency or independent source evidence reveals a consequential external burden; a normal code-impact finding does not suffice. T1 and T2 are **mutually incomparable as current inquiry priorities**: autonomy through absence of software and accountability to indirect publics pose different normative premises. This is **NOT YET PRIORITIZED**, not a finding of low value.

## 5. Independently executable Focused Depth Exploration questions

These are **questions to consider**, not authorizations, tasks, or prompts to be run automatically. Each states a concrete conditional premise and asks about consequences, while leaving solution architecture and empirical success open.

1. **Contextually trustworthy knowledge (rank 1).** *Suppose a later coding agent retrieves two source-faithful architecture claims whose applicability differs by Git revision, deployment condition and the historical meaning of a domain term. What consequences follow for identifying genuine contradictions, communicating uncertainty and making a prospective product recommendation without elevating a graph assertion into semantic truth?* Keep the unit of inquiry a **situated claim and its consequences**, not a new schema.

2. **Plural, permission-aware understanding (rank 2).** *Suppose two independent coding agents working on one software product inherit different, properly attributed source snapshots and share incompatible provisional interpretations, while neither may claim additional decision or publication authority from that exchange. What consequences follow for disagreement, re-entry, correction, attribution and selective sharing of their reasoning?* Keep the unit a **multi-interpreter knowledge relationship**, not access-control implementation.

3. **Longitudinal judgment continuity (rank 3).** *Suppose a fresh agent inherits a prior accepted architectural rationale and a declined alternative, but an original premise has materially changed since the prior session. What consequences follow for preserving useful judgment without treating historical decisions as permanent vetoes or prematurely reviving declined work?* Keep the unit **historical premise change and future judgment**, not a retrieval-score test.

4. **Human comprehension (rank 4; conditional additional inquiry).** *Suppose the repository owner must challenge an agent's high-consequence architectural conclusion using a bounded, source-grounded explanation rather than reading the entire semantic archive. What consequences follow for their ability to identify missing assumptions, distinguish evidence from opinion and exercise real supervision, compared with merely displaying more facts?* The proposed **different human understanding** remains hypothetical until reviewed.

Each question can be executed separately by a future agent against then-current sources. None prescribes a preferred option, UI, graph edge, experiment, campaign or build order.

## 6. Reassessment conditions and non-triggers

**Reconsider a comparative position when a real condition changes:**

- **Material new consequences:** a focused inquiry reveals an unexpected, source-faithful consequence that changes how two directions should be distinguished, or shows that a supposedly independent user outcome is only a mechanism. Reconcile *that pair*, not the entire portfolio automatically.
- **Novel possibility or changed beneficiary:** a genuinely different product end emerges that the eight outcome directions cannot fairly contain. Use bounded **new divergence/coverage reconciliation** if needed, rather than forcing a novel possibility into the existing order. Merely renaming a candidate does not qualify.
- **Authority or thesis changes:** owner-ratified product strategy/ADR changes the beneficiary, allowed authority or purpose. Reframe T1/T2 and relevant numbered directions using the new authority, not historical priority by inertia.
- **Premise/source currentness change:** relevant repository source or reviewed knowledge invalidates a load-bearing assumption (e.g. newly demonstrated applicability handling, independent multi-agent reconciliation, or a changed longitudinal failure). Check the source/revision first; a changed file alone is not semantic drift.
- **New authentic evidence:** a genuinely independent agent or user episode shows a consequential benefit, anchoring effect, confusion or failure in a direction that a *conceptual* Depth Exploration could now usefully explain. Keep qualification and conceptual exploration distinct; empirical evidence does not itself authorize depth.
- **Marginal learning exhausted:** a planned question turns out already answered in reviewed docs, or cannot progress without external participants/evidence. Reduce or suspend that particular depth question without demoting the whole product possibility.

**Do not change ranking merely because:** a new PR merged, CI passed, more ideas were counted, one candidate got a more vivid name, an existing ADR was quoted again, a graph relation became mechanically reachable, a hypothetical consequence sounds alarming, or a favored direction received more discussion. These can be evidence within their own scopes but do not establish *new depth learning*. A new source change matters only when its actual meaning affects a comparative premise.

**Review boundary:** the position is dated and revisable; a future context should read current strategy/ADR/source and the [Convergent Evaluation](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md) before relying on it. Do not automatically execute the top-ranked inquiry, reorder work, revise Level 3 or Level 4, update the [curated Idea Library](../idea-library.md), or change the semantic knowledge packs.
