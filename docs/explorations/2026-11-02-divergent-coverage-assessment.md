This assessment treats **sufficient coverage** as: a landscape containing materially different kinds of outcomes, audiences, experiences, creative operations and architectural readings, with conspicuous overlaps and missing territory documented well enough that a separate inquiry *could* examine choices without claiming the known catalogue is exhaustive. This is **not** a numeric threshold, idea ranking or claim of demonstrated creative benefit.

## 5. Remaining territory and the meaning of “unexplored”

**Substantively open even near the current product frame:**

- **Agent-to-agent epistemic negotiation:** how independent coding agents with different source snapshots, confidence, authority and histories might develop new understanding *through their disagreement*, rather than simply pass a handoff or share stored claims. This was explicitly left unexplored by [Divergent II](2026-11-02-divergent-repository-knowledge-second-pass.md#explicitly-unexamined-directions) and touches ADR 0031's multi-agent knowledge destination. It may overlap DE-G / NX-N, but those do not explicitly explore *different agents' evolving knowledge relations*.
- **Independent pre-recall imagination:** the [method reflection](2026-11-02-inquiry-method-reflection.md#4-optional-lenses-for-multiple-exploratory-passes) names a lens that starts without the Atlas and compares afterward. Its absence makes **catalogue anchoring** plausible, especially since later passes explicitly tried to differ from earlier family names. This is a blind spot in *the exploration method*, not proof of unrepresented product capabilities.
- **Source-grounded product encounters with unlike engineering contexts:** the archive's detailed examples touch Atlas and Chess Mentor, but an independent non-self-referential engineering domain could expose missing questions that the Sensemaking-specific vocabulary cannot currently name. Existing [normal-use cases](../normal-use/2026-11-02-creative-prospective-discovery.md) establish bounded source review, not broad cross-product creative validation.

**Open mainly outside the accepted primary domain or not yet independently grounded:**

- Cultural, linguistic, accessibility and care-centered experiences, including forms of authority and explanation that do not map neatly onto a technical repository owner.
- Non-textual preservation across generations, contested ownership of abandoned software, and practices beyond formal engineering organizations.
- Physical/environmental consequences, nonhuman affected systems and civic legitimacy in actual communities. RP-R/T/U **introduced** these dimensions but did not substantively research real manifestations.

The first group marks plausible additional *in-scope* lenses; the second marks broader speculative horizons. **“Not substantively explored” means the archive does not contain a grounded examination of that object, not that the system lacks it, that a candidate is defective, or that the missing territory blocks a separate evaluation.** Nor does a bounded search prove global absence.

## 6. Diminishing returns: what the sequence supports

**Observed qualitative pattern:** repeated familiar moves now encounter named predecessors. For example, recreating design-fiction, optionality, selective memory, stakeholder authorship or applicability descriptions often restates a GD/DE/NX idea. The third pass [records this explicitly](2026-11-02-divergent-remaining-possibility-space.md#candidate-like-variations-deliberately-not-counted-as-new). Within these well-traveled lenses, **conceptual additions have become harder to distinguish from variants**.

**Counter-pattern:** the same third pass still exposed *different kinds of ends and affected worlds*: success without software, principled non-capture, physical ecology, embodied time and unenrolled publics. Novelty therefore has **not** simply declined pass by pass. Changing the question, rather than continuing a familiar recombination operation, created new categories.

**Evidence limits:** the passes vary their framing, the same-day archive builds on itself, and no independent pre-recall comparison or controlled creative-output measure exists. We therefore cannot estimate a novelty rate, claim a global plateau, conclude an additional pass would be redundant, or treat later candidate counts as empirical returns. The [method reflection](2026-11-02-inquiry-method-reflection.md#5-what-diminishing-returns-can-and-cannot-mean) already warns that candidate totals and local reductions in novelty are neither a score nor a transition trigger.

**What can be said:** the archive exhibits **local diminishing conceptual returns under repeated established lenses**, alongside **continuing expansion when the frame changes**. This describes the *search process*, not the value, feasibility, priority or quality of any idea.

## 7. Could an alternative lens still uncover new territory?

**Yes, plausibly; this does not automatically make it a prerequisite.** Three materially different ways of looking are visible from the source gaps, without conducting another pass here:

- A **blind, pre-Atlas inquiry** by a fresh context followed by source-grounded comparison could test whether current vocabulary is anchoring the set of imaginable outcomes ([method reflection](2026-11-02-inquiry-method-reflection.md#4-optional-lenses-for-multiple-exploratory-passes)).
- A **cross-agent knowledge-relations lens** could ask about the creative consequences of agents encountering different, explicitly sourced understandings, not just about representation of contradictory statements ([ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md); [NX-N](2026-11-02-divergent-repository-knowledge-second-pass.md#family-n---situated-validity-and-epistemic-environments)).
- An **unfamiliar real-user or domain lens**, grounded in an independently observed repository/user setting, could expose goals that self-referential Sensemaking examples and hypothetical personas do not reveal ([product strategy](../product-strategy.md); [Divergent III's open territories](2026-11-02-divergent-remaining-possibility-space.md#further-territory-still-outside-this-pass)).

These name **underexplored perspectives**, not product proposals, ranking criteria, planned studies or requirements to perform the work. Their plausible productivity is compatible with sufficient *current* coverage to consider a different, separately authorized mode.

## 8. Provisional finding and explicit stop boundary

### Coverage appears sufficient to consider a separate Convergent Evaluation

**Reason for this finding:** within the accepted ontology-first, repository-knowledge and prospective-reasoning product frame, the archive now exposes qualitatively distinct **goals, intended and indirect users, sensory/social experiences, mechanisms, temporal/epistemic transformations and architecture-level possibilities**. It also documents substantial cross-pass overlap, clear restatements, bounded source origins and remaining uncertainty. A separate inquiry could therefore begin from a *meaningfully varied landscape*, instead of mistaking the most recent implementation task or the first attractive idea for the whole possibility space.

This finding **does not depend on proving diminishing returns globally**. Another perspective might reveal a major new category, and the newest divergent pass demonstrates why that remains possible. “Sufficient to consider” is a **reversible coverage judgment**, not “all possibilities exhausted,” “further divergence unjustified,” “all families independent,” or “proceed now.”

**No trigger follows.** No Convergent Evaluation, further Divergent Exploration, comprehensive consequence analysis, strategic reopening, owner decision, implementation, ranking, curation or architecture selection is initiated. If a later, separately authorized inquiry changes mode, it must respect the then-current strategy, authority and evidence rules; this record is not that authorization.

**Retention:** this assessment belongs only in the dated exploration archive and its navigable Atlas. The curated [Idea Library](../idea-library.md), `.sensemaking/knowledge/`, ADRs, strategy, product contracts, Skills, `STATUS.md` and code remain unchanged. Source-reviewed archive interpretation is established **only for the inspected revision**, not as validation of the ideas or the value of the exploration method.
