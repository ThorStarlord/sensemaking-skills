### Depth Exploration: explicit intellectual completeness test

Before concluding a **future consequence-driven depth** inquiry, inspect the *substantive* argument, not section count:

1. **Specify the conditional world:** a particular hypothetical capability/premise, beneficiary, context, source-revision baseline, and the closest existing idea/decision.
2. **Direct change:** what changes first if the premise holds? Is it a user outcome, architectural interpretation, evidence/authority relation or interaction?
3. **Propagation:** what else relies on the changed relation? Name *why* effects might follow through second-order consumers, roles, time or feedback; record a non-effect or break in the chain where appropriate.
4. **Systemic/higher-order surprises:** what new constraint, behavior, failure mode, competing goal or opportunity emerges when consequences interact? Do not generate an elaborate chain when no material consequence passes scrutiny.
5. **Benefit and harm:** include plausible upside, downside and tensions among beneficiaries; do not assume one group's benefit is universal.
6. **Counterfactual forks:** name at least one important changed premise or alternate agent/owner behavior that would substantially alter the predicted effects, **where material**; avoid artificial branch quotas.
7. **Epistemic integrity:** mark **logical consequences** (follow from explicitly stipulated assumptions), **plausible conditionals** (would require additional evidence) and **creative possibilities** (interesting but underdetermined). Distinguish *source-grounded starting facts* from inferred user desire, causal efficacy or external world claims. "A may produce B and B may enable C" never proves "A produces C".
8. **Consequence-to-opportunity return:** identify any truly distinct new question/candidate exposed by the chain; retain it as a non-promoted hypothesis with origin, without automatically restarting broad Divergent Exploration.
9. **Stop reason:** state which important downstream consequences were investigated, which remain unknowable or irrelevant, and why more reasoning now would or would not change intellectual understanding.

A focused inquiry can legitimately end with **no demonstrated cascade** or no new opportunity. Missing empirical evidence may cap a consequence claim; it does **not** automatically authorize qualification. This complements, rather than supersedes, [Consequence Depth v0](../research/consequence-depth-and-systems-reasoning-v0.md) and the [prospective reasoning ontology's conditional-chain rule](../semantic-architecture/prospective-reasoning-ontology.md#4-domain-specializations).

## 3. Three different comparative/priority claims

| Decision object | Question, legitimate basis and output | Does *not* imply |

| **Strategic Attractiveness** | *Which alternative desired product future might be preferable?* After enough consequence exploration, compare beneficiary value, assumptions, costs/tradeoffs, strategic consequences, counterevidence and owner fit; preserve uncertainty and competing perspectives. A provisional strategic judgment belongs to an appropriately authorized strategy process. | Owner ratification, accepted product identity, Level-3 construction path or implementation authority |

**Non-identity:** a top-ranked exploratory question can concern a product future that is ultimately rejected, or an accepted product goal whose benefit is not yet qualified. The three orderings need not—and should not—be numerically correlated.

### Important distinction: ranking bias versus a proven misranking

Current rank 1 (contextual validity) has an attributable source base and high cross-direction consequence potential; that is a defensible *conditional intellectual preference*. But the [ranking's own language](2026-11-03-depth-exploration-prioritization.md#4-direct-comparisons-that-drive-the-ordering) sometimes uses **existing source richness** to justify preferring one topic to another. Source richness is relevant for tractability, but it is **not** by itself a measure of how transformative or intellectually consequential another question could be.

The correct response is to ask which **specific unexamined consequence** might overturn the comparison, not artificially boost an unfamiliar topic or demote a familiar one. A transformative direction that needs a new beneficiary or authority frame can have a **separate, separately authorized inquiry priority within that frame** without losing its identity in the original portfolio. This report neither changes the six-place order nor invents a winner between T1 and T2.
