# Evidence and Qualification Inquiry: does accumulated knowledge improve creative goal discovery?

**Date:** 2026-11-03.
**Status:** **NON-AUTHORITATIVE EVIDENCE / INQUIRY-WARRANT ASSESSMENT**; not an executed qualification, normal-use session, experiment, product decision, implementation plan, Strategic Frontier admission, Skill, or amended success criterion.
**Inspected baseline:** `ThorStarlord/sensemaking-skills` `main@5adda5eac1f9dc7d03f7c8c5400dc133382a138f`. Source and evidence claims are revision-bounded.
**Narrow question:** What is the smallest credible evidence that would distinguish an independent coding agent's **new, coherent, potentially valuable product-goal discovery attributable in part to accumulated knowledge** from what an ordinary capable agent would discover without that retained knowledge?
**Provisional inquiry judgment:** **INQUIRY_REQUIRED for the *comparative creative-benefit claim*, but no experiment or other qualification is authorized or begun by this record.** A **single, authentic, tightly bounded matched qualitative comparison**, *only when a genuinely open repository/product question and independent execution/review are available*, is the smallest plausible next evidence-producing action that could differentiate **knowledge-assisted discovery from ordinary source-only reasoning in one context**. One pair **cannot** establish general superiority, isolate graph representation from the historical prose and prompting that accompany it, or certify S5/S6. If no such real question or reviewer exists, **do not invent a synthetic opportunity or start a campaign**; the appropriate immediate result is this documented evidence gap and a stop.

## 1. Decision to inform, warrant and scope

The product strategy states that Sensemaking exists to make a software repository understandable and its possible future intelligently reasoned about across agents and time. [ADR 0034](../adr/0034-ontology-first-adaptive-sensemaking.md) accepts ontology-grounded understanding and prospective reasoning as legitimate standalone results. [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md) accepts persistent, source-qualified, typed knowledge as a long-term destination but expressly treats knowledge **usefulness**, not graph volume, as the goal. [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-current-product-boundary.md) keeps semantic judgment with the active agent and rejects automatic strategic selection and self-granted authority. The current [product strategy](../product-strategy.md), [STATUS](../../STATUS.md), and accepted ADRs remain unchanged by this inquiry.

**Actual claim needing evidence:** *In at least one genuine independent-agent situation, accumulated, attributable repository knowledge materially contributes to an owner-recognized, source-faithful and genuinely **new desired user/product outcome** that a comparably capable agent with ordinary repository access does not find or does not support as well.*

This is **not** the stronger unqualified claim that the knowledge graph always makes agents more creative, that graph edges autonomously infer human needs, or that a numeric novelty measure proves value. It is also **not** established by any previous normal-use record.

**Decision such evidence could change:** whether further *targeted inquiry or investment in goal-discovery-specific mechanisms and retrieval/guidance* would be justified, as distinct from continuing the accepted baseline stewardship capability without a new creative-benefit claim. A positive observation could warrant *consideration of a later, separately authorized investigation*, not automatic architecture or implementation selection. A negative observation could justify withholding a special “creative advantage” claim and avoiding new machinery; it would **not** overturn ADR 0031 or 0034. There is no current decision to select or build a goal-invention subsystem.

The governing inquiry/experiment discipline is [Inquiry Policy v0](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/inquiry-policy-v0.md) and [Experiment Economy & Proportional Rigor v1](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/experiment-economy-v1.md): an unresolved question is **not** an experiment warrant; identify decision-changing evidence and prefer the lowest total-cost source capable of supporting the intended claim. This document compares inquiry options but executes none.

## 2. The outcome must be product-goal discovery, not a weaker adjacent success

The [Focused Depth Exploration](2026-11-03-depth-new-product-goals-from-knowledge.md#1-the-selected-inquiry-object-not-a-selected-product) defines a candidate end as **beneficiary + circumstance/unmet tension + desired change in the user's life or work + why it could matter**, separately from implementation. The [Creative Prospective Discovery reference](../../skills/strategic-repository-analysis/references/creative-prospective-discovery-v1.md) already distinguishes **capability novelty** from **architecture novelty** and uses source-reviewed `RESTATED`, `REVISED`, `NEW`, or `NOT_ASSESSED` judgments relative to named predecessors. These are semantic judgments, not graph scores.

| Output in an independent session | What it would support | What it would **not** support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correctly recalls an old `poss:`, rejected option or accepted goal | Historical memory/retrieval and source fidelity | Novel-goal discovery |
| Corrects a false implementation assumption or changes a preferred architecture | Better bounded engineering reasoning | A newly articulated **desired user end** |
| Combines graph records or names an unrepresented connection | Mechanism/relationship exploration | New beneficiary-level goal without an independent outcome |
| Offers an old requirement in compelling new language | Potentially useful explanation | Novelty relative to that requirement |
| Presents a new feature or UI for an accepted outcome | Implementation/product-design possibility | A changed *purpose* or user accomplishment |
| Proposes a previously unarticulated user/end hypothesis, supported by source tensions and distinct from reviewed prior goals | **Candidate goal discovery**, contingent on reviewer checks | User value, graph causality, authority or comparative benefit until independently examined |
| Owner finds a distinct end worth considering, whether or not adopted | Potential **owner relevance** | That graph retrieval rather than reasoning or prompt framing caused it |

**Novelty has a comparison scope:** novel to an agent, to the immediate owner question, to the repository's accepted goals, or to the full archive are different conclusions. An agent can be surprised by a *remembered* goal. “NEW relative to inspected documented product goals” is a falsifiable repository-local claim; “globally original” is not a credible standard here.

For every proposed goal, consider separately (a) **source-faithful fact use**, (b) **difference from existing goals**, (c) **coherence with the domain and constraints**, (d) **plausible owner/user importance**, and (e) **retained-knowledge contribution**. Success in (a)–(d) does **not** entail (e). The owner may appreciate a surprising, nonselected hypothesis. An implementation task is not a prerequisite to discovering a valuable question.

## 4. Competing explanations and distinguishable observations

A credible inquiry must not simply ask “did the graph agent produce something good?” It must ask **what information or reasoning opportunity changed**.

| Explanation | What could produce an apparent improvement | Distinguishing evidence or reason for restraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ordinary capable-agent reasoning** | An independently competent agent infers an overlooked need from source code, domain expertise or good questions without persistent records. | The source-only comparator articulates essentially the same **beneficiary and end**, perhaps with different wording. No marginal knowledge benefit is supported in that case. |
| **Historical source prose** | An old ADR, issue, normal-use report or design narrative already contains the decisive tension; agent reasoning from the prose suffices. | The relevant detail is present in source documents reachable by both agents. A match to graph-record content establishes **historical information** helped; only an actual difference in access/relevance supports a retrieval claim. |
| **Relevant historical retrieval / navigation** | Typed/indexed memory makes a previously overlooked, source-backed fact reachable at the moment the agent needs it. | Inspect the transcript: did the returning agent **actually retrieve** a revision-correct old premise that the comparator did not surface, and transform it into a materially different end? This would support a **local access/memory advantage**, not a proof about graph topology. |
| **Typed graph relationships** | Explicit `references`, `decided_by`, `learned_from`, `contradicts` or scoped goal/constraint links expose a relation not salient in isolated prose. | Identify the **specific** relationship traversed and source-check both endpoints; compare to what reading the same linked sources achieves. Edge presence, degree or adjacency alone is not causal evidence. |
| **Creative prompting / Skill guidance** | The [Creative Prospective Discovery reference](../../skills/strategic-repository-analysis/references/creative-prospective-discovery-v1.md) instructs an agent to distinguish ends from structures, invert assumptions and seek negative space. | Hold instructions as equivalent as possible before attributing a different answer to memory. Otherwise the observed effect may be of **framing**, which can be valuable but is not a demonstrated graph advantage. |
| **Anchoring / irrelevant retrieval** | The archive surfaces attractive previously formulated goals and rejected architectures, suppressing fresh user-perspective questions or filling the context with low-relevance facts. | The source-only agent may discover a **more distinct and useful** end or notice evidence the returning agent missed. Treat that as counterevidence, not as an anomaly to discard. |
| **Uncontrolled differences / evaluator preference** | Different model capability, repository SHA, effort, tool access, prompts, hidden hints or reviewers' familiarity with the prior ideas drive the apparent difference. | A minimal matched comparison controls only differences able to overturn the **local** inference; blind evaluation of outputs where practical, and preserve baseline/method provenance. |

**Claim hierarchy:**

```text
reviewed source-backed records exist
  -> accessible when queried
  -> actually read by an independent agent
  -> materially used in a goal-level reasoning move
  -> proposed a source-faithful, non-restated end
  -> owner finds that end worth considering
  -> source-only agent does not achieve the same outcome in the same situation
  -> local evidence of incremental creative benefit from available accumulated knowledge
     != general advantage across tasks
     != necessity of graph representation
     != authorization to implement
```

Each arrow is an **unproven causal or evaluative step** until direct evidence supports it. Merely including a graph citation in the final answer does not establish which earlier inference depended on it.

**Why not mandate controlled experimentation?** If the decision were only “is a particular owner finding independent goal-discovery outputs useful enough to keep discussing?”, a normal-use independent agent plus review could suffice. But the question here explicitly asks whether accumulated memory **materially improves over ordinary capable-agent reasoning**. To warrant that *comparative* statement, some credible **comparison** is needed. One matched natural-use pair can supply a **local comparative signal** with far less ceremony than a campaign; it does not provide research-grade causal attribution.

**No immediate experiment warrant:** the inspected sources contain no newly offered, genuinely open and prevalidated owner question *for this inquiry* and no ready independent reviewer for it. Replaying the Chess Mentor prompt, Atlas's completed architectural problem, or the hypothetical goals from the Depth Exploration would disclose the answer space and test recall/contamination instead of spontaneous discovery. **Do not choose or invent a target merely to run a trial.** This assessment's only recommended next evidence-producing move is **conditional on a real question arising**, not an instruction to launch anything now.

## 6. Smallest credible next inquiry

**Warrant target:** one **local, decision-relevant observation** of whether the retained knowledge surface changes an independent coding agent's *goal hypotheses* in a real product/repository decision, not proof of general creative superiority or a new graph architecture.

**Bounded action if its preconditions are met:** on an independently arising repository/product question that admits goal reconsideration, conduct **one matched qualitative natural-use comparison** with two independent capable-agent contexts. The two receive the same genuine question, the same current repository/source revision and comparable model/tool/effort access. **Both may inspect ordinary source, current requirements, historical prose and decisions.** The returning context can additionally use the existing Git-reviewed `.sensemaking/` authored memory and normal selective retrieval; the source-only context does not receive the curated semantic surface, but is **not** deliberately prevented from finding equivalent information in the canonical repository sources. No hidden example goal, recalled candidate ID or desired creative answer is placed in either prompt.

The claim being tested is *incremental availability/usefulness of accumulated curated knowledge under the product's realistic operating mode*. **This two-condition distinction does not isolate graph structure from better recall or relevance selection**; do not describe it as graph-edge causality.

**A source-and-authority integrity check is indispensable:** pin each condition's actual repository SHA and material difference; avoid comparing different product implementations. Verify that the retained records are source-current, and that the source-only run's absence of the curated memory does not also strip governing instructions, accepted ADRs, user goals or ordinary historical documents. Record both agents' actual access paths and query/report omissions rather than assuming the intended environment was used. Use existing CLI, connector and document surfaces — no new evaluator framework, schema, Skill, dataset or automatic recorder.

**The question should be genuinely open:** its owner is considering *what the product should enable*, rather than asking for a particular feature or a yes/no implementation of a goal already ratified. The investigators must not be seeded with the Depth Exploration's Chess Mentor, Atlas or Sensemaking scenarios as target answers. A domain reviewer can confirm the question is real and decision-relevant **without** prescribing a wanted outcome.

**Lightweight adjudication:** preserve both complete outputs and enough inspectable interaction/source-retrieval evidence to trace their reasoning; a domain/owner reviewer first judges the proposed **ends** without seeing which condition produced them where feasible, and only then a source reviewer examines predecessor goals, citations, truth claims and agent access to retained material. If the owner is also the primary domain reviewer, document that reviewer overlap as a limitation; do not invent an independent panel.

**Stop at one answerable difference**: if the two outputs converge on the same end, if neither reaches a credible novel end, if both are useful but in different ways, or if a graph-surfaced old fact explains an incremental difference, record the precise finding and stop. Do **not** automatically repeat until a favorable result, enhance the memory graph, or add a third condition. Any stronger attribution or generalization requires a *new, separately warranted* inquiry.

## 7. Reviewer judgments: keep novelty, value and contribution independent

A reviewer should encounter **the goal hypothesis as an end**, not a polished product feature or a graph-derived numerical score. Helpful qualitative questions are:

1. **Beneficiary and desired change:** Does the candidate explain who would be able to do, understand or experience **what** that they presently cannot, and in which relevant circumstances?
2. **Prior-art/goal boundary:** Is that end already in accepted product strategy, earlier owner decisions, documented requirements, a previous `poss:`, or an explicit user question? It may be **RESTATED**, **REVISED**, plausibly **NEW** or **NOT_ASSESSED** relative to the reviewed corpus; this is **not** a priority rank.
3. **Source faithfulness:** Which concrete observed facts are quoted/linked; which causal or user-value steps are hypothetical; are previous rejected alternatives, policy decisions and temporal scope respected? A likely wrong fact can disqualify source fidelity independently of creative attractiveness.
4. **Conceptual coherence:** Are beneficiary, desired state, motivating tension and constraints jointly intelligible? Does the idea explain a real tension or merely concatenate nouns from unrelated graph nodes?
5. **Potential owner/user usefulness:** Does the end change what futures the owner would seriously consider, reveal a blind spot, or clarify why no change is warranted? The owner may **decline** a coherent possibility; usefulness is not “implemented” or “selected.”
6. **Comparison without source attribution leakage:** Do the two outputs express materially different **ends**, or are they stylistic variants, different features for the same end, or different levels of detail?
7. **Retained-knowledge contribution — assessed later, separately:** Did the agent actually retrieve relevant historical material, source-check it and use it in the **transformative step**? Did the source-only agent already find the same premise elsewhere? Could guidance alone explain the difference?

**Separating review order matters:** an evaluator who knows which output used the graph may favor it even when it merely cites more claims. Conversely, the reviewer need not be “blind” to *all repository facts*: understanding the domain and past promises is essential to detecting restatements. Source reviewers should inspect the original records at their declared revision rather than trust either agent's novelty label.

**Qualitative judgment categories (descriptive, not scores):** source-faithful/contested/unsupported; goal-level/restated/feature-level/unclear; coherent/inconsistent/unassessed; owner-relevant/unhelpful/undetermined; retrieved-and-used/retrieved-only/not-seen; comparative advantage/approximate parity/source-only stronger/inconclusive. Do not combine them into a numeric “creative quality” metric.

## 8. Negative results and what each would change

| Plausible observation | Legitimate interpretation | What becomes unwarranted or remains open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source-only reaches the same new and owner-valued end** | Ordinary agent reasoning/source prose sufficed **in this case**; knowledge availability had no demonstrated marginal goal benefit | Do not attribute novelty to graph; retain possible value for recall, speed, accuracy or other tasks separately |
| **Source-only finds a better-grounded or more useful end** | Retained history may have anchored or distracted the returning agent, or a different stochastic reasoning path helped source-only | Investigate anchoring **only if** that difference matters to a real future decision; do not hide or “fix” the case post hoc |
| **Returning agent mostly retrieves `poss:` and restates them** | Recall/relevance benefit (if source-correct), **not** new-goal invention | No goal-discovery superiority; the original possibility may still be useful |
| **Returning agent produces a technically novel architecture for an existing goal** | Structural creativity or better design, **not** a changed user end | Keep product-goal claim unresolved |
| **Both produce no coherent newly articulated goals** | The question may offer no real latent goal, or both may be too anchored/incomplete | One case does not refute all possible creative benefit; **stop the case**, do not manufacture novelty |
| **Returning agent discovers a distinct goal but only via incorrect source or invented user demand** | Creative story unsupported by the repository's evidence | Do not promote to source-grounded success; owner may still consider as an openly speculative question |
| **Both find different plausible goals, with no clear owner-relevant difference** | Multiple ordinary creative paths; no demonstrated superiority | Maintain uncertainty; do not force a winner or score |
| **Returning agent finds distinct, source-backed, owner-relevant end not found by source-only, traceably enabled by retrieved history** | **Promising one-context evidence** of incremental benefit from accessible accumulated knowledge | Still not proof of long-term, general or specifically graph-structural superiority; decide any further qualification separately |
| **Goal appears only because graph condition received richer creative instructions or a pre-named candidate** | Prompt/hint confound, not isolatable memory benefit | **INCONCLUSIVE** for comparative claim; do not retrofit outcome into proof |
| **No accessible historical records, truncated query or stale graph** | The intended qualification target was not exposed; or wrong source scope | **NOT_ASSESSED** rather than evidence of absent user need or creative incapacity |
| **Agent breaches Git/PR authority** | Product-control defect, independent of whether a coherent goal was phrased | Preserve it as an adverse normal-use finding; do not let goal creativity excuse unauthorized action |

These outcomes can inform **whether to pursue any subsequent evidence** without pre-authorizing construction. The owner may decide the existing knowledge system is worth keeping for long-term stewardship even if no special creative advantage emerges from this one question.

## 9. Why one pair is the *minimum discriminating* step, and what it cannot prove

**More than a solo anecdote:** a solo independent-agent discovery plus owner recognition would establish *feasibility of source-grounded hypothesis generation*. It cannot distinguish cumulative memory from ordinary reasoning, which is explicitly the decision-changing uncertainty in this inquiry. Comparing two agents on a genuinely open question supplies at least one contemporaneous counterfactual.

**Less than a controlled research campaign:** the [CME pre-registration](../normal-use/operational-qualification-001-preregistration.md) already supplies a narrowly documented matched-source comparison precedent. Its completed [qualification](../normal-use/operational-qualification-001.md) shows why exact source, identical prompt, retained-memory access, actual transcripts, reviewer awareness and authority failures matter. For the next *creative* question, sufficient rigor must shift from “did the agent recall/correct?” to “did it articulate an owner-relevant **new end**?” There is no evidentiary reason yet to build a scoring harness, large dataset or additional model.

**What one result can warrant:** at most a *case-bounded* statement such as “for this exact goal question, compared with this comparable source-only run, the agent with retained knowledge surfaced and used a specified historical tension that contributed to an independently judged, non-restated and owner-interesting goal.” It might motivate a later choice to investigate reachability or novelty across contexts. It cannot prove repeatability, reliability across repositories, semantic causal edges, graph-specific contribution over equivalent prose, or accepted owner intent.

**Disambiguation remains conditional:** if the local difference matters and the owner later needs to know whether **typed relationships**, rather than easier historical access or creative prompts, caused it, *then* an additional historical-prose/structured comparison may be justified. Do not demand that more expensive attribution in advance when the current decision is whether meaningful **incremental creative value exists at all**. Normal capable-agent competence and source inspection are the product's natural environment, not contamination. What matters is avoiding **direct answer leakage** and different consequential inputs.

**Evidence stop/warrant:** if a real question does not emerge, the cheapest correct action is to **stop**, leaving the comparative goal-discovery claim explicitly unproven. If a real question emerges but a matched comparison cannot be arranged, record a **solo normal-use** encounter under its weaker claim ceiling. No number of repeated curated records or synthetically generated ideas can substitute for this missing evidence.

## 10. Synthesis, decisions left untouched and stop

**Current observation:** typed, source-grounded memory and creative-discovery guidance exist; one completed CME pair showed one useful retained correction and an independent source-only counterfinding; the Atlas case showed careful engineering reasoning but no novel goal; local Sensemaking history links improved retrievability, not observed creative invention. **There is currently no demonstrated comparative new-product-goal discovery.**

**Interpretive conclusion:** an independent agent might transform a hard-to-find historical tension into an unasked, coherent desired user outcome. But ordinary source reading, prose history, model reasoning and creative guidance are plausible sufficient causes, while retained candidates can also anchor the agent. A credible benefit claim needs a meaningful **different user end and owner recognition**, not just a higher volume of citations, an interesting architecture or a successful lookup.

**Recommended *future* evidence move, conditional:** **one authentic, matched, qualitative independent-agent comparison** when a genuinely open product-goal question and independent reviewer naturally exist. Do not execute it now. This is the smallest plausible **discriminating** inquiry for the question as phrased; a single normal-use episode is cheaper but cannot show comparative improvement, and a third condition or research campaign would presently add costs for a stronger claim not needed to inform the next decision.

**What remains reserved:** whether to arrange the comparison, authorize agent execution, interpret owner demand, adopt or reject any newly stated goal, expand qualification scope, amend strategy/ADRs, select architecture, commit external work, or promote any `poss:`.
