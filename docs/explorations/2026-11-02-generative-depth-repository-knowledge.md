## Source-grounded foundations and evidence ceiling

- [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md) selects persistent, typed, provenance-aware semantic knowledge, separating knowledge and agent-led reasoning planes.
- [ADR 0034](../adr/0034-ontology-first-adaptive-sensemaking.md) makes understanding and prospective reasoning first-class outcomes, with optional implementation and breadth before convergence.
- [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-current-product-boundary.md) keeps semantic selection with the active agent and excludes automatic strategic planning and implied authority.
- The [prospective reasoning ontology](../semantic-architecture/prospective-reasoning-ontology.md) distinguishes ProductPossibilities, ArchitectureOptions, ConsequenceHypotheses, preferences and authorized decisions.
- The [curated Idea Library](../idea-library.md) already has possibility memory, cross-episode synthesis, target discovery memory and assumption-aware opportunity ecology. These are **related sources**, not proof of a new product mechanism.
- The [Atlas Midgard first application](../normal-use/prospective-reasoning-atlas-001.md) reports same-session architectural reasoning that revised the design to avoid Chronos anachronism; [the ontology-first follow-up](../normal-use/ontology-first-entry-atlas-001.md) identified other cross-system questions. Neither provides independent evidence of general creative intelligence.
- [Longitudinal historical-link repair](../normal-use/2026-11-02-cross-episode-source-reentry-learning.md) documents source-review and mechanical retrieval improvements, not causal cross-session benefit.

## Central generative idea

The repository does not merely hold answers to engineering questions. It also holds rejected alternatives, prevented failures, unresolved distinctions, constraints and partial descriptions of user outcomes. Under speculative reinterpretation these could help an agent discover **new goals and experiences**, not only improved means of implementing known goals.

The causal chains below are **conditional thought experiments**. They do not establish that a query engine generated these ideas, that an external coding agent would do so independently, or that a target product should adopt them.

## Mechanism A - Constraints as creative material

**Tension:** correct historical behavior vs imaginative product experience. **Source seed:** Atlas's source-reviewed Chronos anachronism consequence, derived world presentation and canonical History boundary.

- Existing engineering fact (at the recorded Atlas revision): restoration markers must not imply that future restoration was already present during historical reactivation.
- Hypothetical inversion: keep historical truth intact but explore an explicitly noncanonical visualization, such as a *Temporal Echo* representing a possible future.
- New outcome: a player can anticipate what their restoration might make possible before it happens, and later recognize the earlier glimpse.
- Other forms: design-time visual prototypes, hypothetical outcome previews, or authored narrative foreshadowing; none require durable alternate state.

**Distinctive transformation:** a prohibited presentation becomes an ingredient in an intentionally differentiated experience. This does not claim the Atlas feature exists.

## Mechanism B - Architectural exaptation

**Tension:** rejecting an architecture for one purpose vs reusing one of its mechanisms for another. **Source seed:** Atlas's compared scene wiring, derived consequence catalog, durable world-state authority and restored variants.

- The original rejection of an additional durable authority can remain valid.
- A *fragment* of a declined design, such as plural visual projections, might be useful without adopting the rejected responsibility.
- New outcome: canonical events with several clearly identified interpretations (memory, reality, dreams, projected futures, historical storytelling).
- Other forms: interpretive overlays; contextual reconstructions; noncanonical simulations.

**Distinctive transformation:** unlike reviving a rejected possibility, this keeps the original decision in force and uses discarded design material for a different outcome.

## Mechanism C - New perspectives on one repository

**Tension:** file-centric retrieval vs experience- and goal-centric inquiry. **Source seed:** source-to-context anchor repairs in [PR history synthesis](../normal-use/2026-11-02-cross-episode-source-reentry-learning.md) and the ontology's distinction between SoftwareSystem, Product, Knowledge and Intent.

- Existing fact: `context-for` follows reviewed ownership and relations for known source paths, and some historical missing-path links were repaired. This is not evidence that the entire agent reasoning process is file-first only.
- Hypothetical new interaction: developers navigate several source-grounded conceptual maps: component topology, product meaning, historical interpretation, alternative futures.
- New outcome: a designer begins with an experience such as “help a person understand and reshape the system” instead of a file path.
- Other forms: user-goal lens, architecture atlas, architectural interpretation conversation, capability-to-experience map.

**Distinctive transformation:** multiple interpretive organizations of one factual substrate rather than multiple conflicting authorities.

## Mechanism D - Latent product goals

**Tension:** implemented mechanisms vs unarticulated reasons users might value them. **Source seed:** Atlas restoration, companionship, ordinary life and world-history systems in the [ontology-first assessment](../normal-use/ontology-first-entry-atlas-001.md).

- Existing facts establish separately designed game systems, not a ratified new combined user goal.
- Hypothetical interpretive question: what if the common value is that *the world develops a living social memory of the player's actions*?
- New experience: different people remember, interpret and retell events; heroic actions acquire social and emotional meaning.
- Other forms: local legends, narrative echoes in routines, community recollections, competing historical interpretations.

**Distinctive transformation:** the agent discovers a *candidate product aspiration*, not just an architecture for a previously declared feature.

## Mechanism E - Knowledge versus independent imagination

**Tension:** increasing factual context can expand knowledge but also anchor exploration to existing named opportunities. **Source seed:** curated possibility records and [Creative Prospective Discovery](../../skills/strategic-repository-analysis/references/creative-prospective-discovery-v1.md).

- Knowledge-led inquiry explores reviewed capabilities, decisions, constraints and existing options.
- Outcome-led inquiry imagines desired experiences before treating the known Idea Library as the boundary.
- Hypothetical new capability: *creative differential reasoning*, comparing independent product-experience proposals with historical, source-checked ideas to find both overlaps and surprising absences.
- Other forms: independent fresh lens, outsider-perspective exploration, historical-dissent reflection.

**Distinctive transformation:** the system reasons about what accumulated knowledge makes difficult to imagine, not merely what it helps to remember.

## Mechanism F - Authority enabling speculation

**Tension:** strict decision/authority boundaries vs radical counterfactual exploration. **Source seed:** ADRs 0029, 0031 and 0034.

- A rejected automatic planner is not authorized and does not become current strategy through exploration.
- Hypothetically, the aspirations behind such a planner (orientation, reduced mental burden, continuity) can inspire user-controlled decision landscapes, or other experiences without automatic semantic selection.
- New outcome: users can examine product-thesis-incompatible counterfactuals safely as **ideas**, then find different ways of achieving the desirable parts.

**Distinctive transformation:** prohibitions clarify which *ends* remain attractive even when the originally imagined *means* are excluded. Exploring a thesis-conflicting possibility does not ratify it.

## Hypothetical engineering episodes (illustrations only)

### Episode 1: Atlas Chronos -> Temporal Echoes

**Problem:** future restoration must not appear as historical fact. **Knowledge:** canonical History, derived restoration presentation and the Chronos anachronism finding. **Creative move:** distinguish truth-preserving historical display from a deliberate, explicitly hypothetical visual projection. **Possibility:** fleeting glimpses of restorations that players may later realize. **Difference from ordinary inspection:** the *reason a presentation is forbidden* generates a distinct experiential mechanic. No engine run or user feedback establishes it.

### Episode 2: Sensemaking source context -> intent-first design atelier

**Problem:** historical source knowledge is sometimes inaccessible from file-first entries until explicitly anchored. **Knowledge:** `context-for` ownership rules, existing ontology and prospective synthesis. **Creative move:** ask how exploratory questions without a natural file owner could be experienced through multiple source-grounded interpretations. **Possibility:** an exploratory design atelier organized by desired user experiences and competing architectural interpretations. **Difference from ordinary inspection:** it explores product meanings rather than retrieving only implementing files. Existing agent-native reasoning may already support parts of this; independent novelty is unassessed.

### Episode 3: Chess-Mentor-Engine cold start -> participatory learning discovery

**Problem:** a proposed coach-oriented experience lacks an active learner hypothesis for a new student. **Knowledge:** the [limited operational qualification case](../normal-use/operational-qualification-001.md) and its separate consent/evidence concerns. **Creative move:** treat cold-start absence as an opportunity for a learner-and-coach discovery experience rather than only a missing dashboard prerequisite. **Possibility:** student-controlled sharing and distinctly attributed coach/system interpretations. **Difference from ordinary inspection:** the gap suggests a new user journey. No evidence shows students want this design or that it improves learning.

## Unexpected conceptual consequences to explore later

- Preserving a historical *question* may be more generative than preserving the selected answer.
- Additional knowledge may expose **more meaningful uncertainty** rather than always reducing it.
- One factual repository state may support several coherent analytical architectures without several sources of authority.
- A rejected option may be valuable precisely while its original rejection remains correct.
- An architecture may expand the classes of worthwhile future experiences it makes imaginable.
- Better creative reasoning may require no new database, query, Skill or runtime at all.

These are provocations for later inquiry, not evaluated conclusions.

## Open inquiry dimensions

New user goals vs new architectures; local constraints vs creative affordances; alternate-history imagination vs empirical history; participant perspectives; teaching through architectural decisions; creative optionality vs over-generalized platforms; preserving unresolved questions; independence from the first recalled idea.
