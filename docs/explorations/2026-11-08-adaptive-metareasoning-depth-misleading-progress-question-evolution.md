### The two questions must keep different identities

**Q0 — legitimate descriptive question:** "What authorization/continuation and ownership behavior does the *present* DSH contract support for an ended Ticket's human follow-up after Policy changes and multiple subscriptions?"

**Q1 — provisional normative/product-contract question, quoted from the archived answer:** "When an operator changes authorization policy, what should happen to already-authorized responsibilities and their future follow-ups—particularly where multiple Executors might act on the same request?"

Q1 changes the **type of answer** from *what the present design implements* to *what user-facing guarantee should be chosen*. It arises naturally from the combined original source relations: retained Mandate + source listening and revalidation + multi-owner hand-back + absence of a unique side-effect guarantee. The same sources **do not** decide Q1's normative answer or show owner adoption. Q1 is a plausible `opensQuestion` from a successful Q0; it is **not** evidence that Q0 needed `revisesQuestion`.

## 5. Temporal recognition: when a reconsideration becomes worth its cost

The target case has **no continuous, independent progress trace**. Thus the following are **hypothetical event-sensitive opportunities**, not events observed or a recommended schedule:

1. **First source-defined ownership/authorization separation:** a source states that subscriptions deliver context but confer no new authority ([authorization §Subscriptions](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/dsh-superagent-prototype/blob/6337fc823ce20251c8de8085948165c81aaa701e/docs/002-authorization.md#L18-L26)). If the agent is about to infer authorization from fan-out, a bounded source correction becomes useful immediately; there is no reason to question Q0 itself.
2. **A changed-policy fact alters the scope of a near-complete answer:** `revalidate` has basis-specific outcomes ([implementation](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/dsh-superagent-prototype/blob/6337fc823ce20251c8de8085948165c81aaa701e/src/policy.ts#L213-L239)). If the agent is close to concluding "all ended Tickets resume" or "all lose authority," review the unsupported universal claim while still doing otherwise sound work.
3. **A second owner appears in the same Thread:** `followupOwners` and hand-back require all owners to release before Eval ([model](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/dsh-superagent-prototype/blob/6337fc823ce20251c8de8085948165c81aaa701e/src/core/model.ts#L68-L87)). That material relationship should expand *answer coverage within Q0*; it need not create Q1 or invalidate source reconciliation.
4. **A justified technical conclusion exposes an unchosen normative consequence:** after Q0 is answered, the source combination may make the operator-expectation question Q1 worth **mentioning**. This is a distinct inquiry possibility, not automatic attention allocation or escalation.
5. **An apparent closure would overclaim:** a conclusion about one source path cannot establish the entire requested guarantee. Compare original Q0 and actual examined surfaces once closure depends on that gap; the [controller's scope-at-closure backstop](../../skills/using-sensemaking/SKILL.md#inquiry-orientation-and-global-local-scope-integrity) already instructs that comparison.

**What distinguishes quiet drift from legitimate depth?** A repeated subtask is concerning when additional technical precision no longer improves Q0 **and** a consequential requested dimension is unaddressed. If the subtask remains decision-changing (e.g., checking an admission basis), repetition is warranted. **What distinguishes a valuable signal from a reflection trigger?** The signal must plausibly alter the claim, inquiry scope, next action, or authority decision at cost proportionate to the consequence. There is no required timer, periodic check or novelty count. [Metareasoning Policy's qualitative effort law](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/metareasoning-policy-v0.md#5-qualitative-selection-law) and [Exploration Policy's search allocation](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/exploration-policy-v0.md#6-qualitative-allocation-law) already express the economy boundary.

These consequences are conditional on reliance and do not establish real production incidents.

**The minimum material account for this case** (a representation *example*, not new schema fields) is:

- **Q0 purpose and scope:** reconstruct user-facing behavior at `dsh-superagent-prototype@6337fc823ce20251c8de8085948165c81aaa701e` after Policy changes and among multiple Tickets.

- **Q1 expression:** a potential product-contract decision about which historical authorizations and follow-up expectations **should** persist. Meaningfully different answer type and decision owner.
- **Relation:** **`opensQuestion(Q0 findings, Q1)`, not `revisesQuestion(Q1, Q0)`** under the evidence inspected. The latter would require evidence that Q0 was badly framed, superseded or required correction.

### Governing principles

1. **It is not correcting every apparently unproductive inquiry; it is recognizing when the present question or method no longer serves its purpose.**
2. **It is not preserving every old question and not pursuing every new question; it is preserving established understanding while making only warranted intellectual changes.**
3. **Metareasoning maturity is recognizing when to continue, when to correct, and when a successful answer opens another consequential question without inventing authority.**
