## 1. Scope, authority and method

### Governing frame

The product strategy and ADR 0034 define Sensemaking as ontology-grounded repository understanding and prospective reasoning for capable coding agents and their owners. ADR 0031 selects persistent semantic stewardship across agents and sessions as the long-term destination. ADR 0029 keeps semantic judgment with the active agent and reserves strategic commitments and protected actions to their appropriate authority. [STRATEGY] [ADR34] [ADR31] [ADR29]

This assessment treats “Priority Framing” as the combination of those governing sources and the user's current inquiry objective. The inspected sources do not identify an additional authoritative document formally bearing that title. The repository's construction priorities in STATUS address a different decision from the exploratory ranking here. [S711] [S715] [STATUS]

The source set includes all 18 files in the exploration directory, the governing strategy and ADRs, the curated Idea Library, the authored possibility/architecture/stewardship/prospective-reasoning packs, and the relevant normal-use evidence. Primary evidence was inspected at the pinned revision. Existing reports about Atlas and Chess Mentor are assessed as evidence in Sensemaking; this report does not independently re-audit those other repositories' present state.

### Three decisions with different evidentiary demands

| Decision | Question answered | Appropriate present conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploration priority | Which specific unresolved inquiry could most improve understanding now? | A provisional ordering with explicit questions, rationale and reversal conditions |
| Strategic attractiveness | Which product direction offers the most compelling beneficiary value, consequences and tradeoffs? | Qualitative judgments, with unresolved comparisons where depth or evidence is insufficient |
| Implementation priority | Which authorized change should be built next, given dependencies and current conditions? | No new construction ordering follows from this assessment |

The distinction is already explicit in the inquiry-process reconciliation. An accepted strategic destination may still warrant an inquiry into a major weakness; an attractive speculative outcome may have little remaining value from another conceptual essay. [S716]

### Comparison method

The ranking considers current-thesis relevance, the consequence of resolving the question, the intellectual uncertainty remaining after previous inquiries, availability of authentic source material, overlap with other inquiries, and whether conceptual analysis can actually produce the needed answer. These are qualitative judgments rather than numerical scores.

The unit of comparison is a **specific remaining question within an outcome direction**. Unknown user preferences, independent-agent effectiveness and graph-specific causal advantage cannot be settled simply by expanding a conceptual explanation.

## 3. Diminishing returns and alternative lenses

### What the record supports

There is credible **local diminishing conceptual return** when an inquiry reuses a familiar source relationship, beneficiary and transformation:

- Design-fiction rehearsal and design-fiction workshop overlap strongly.
- Optionality foresight and optionality landscapes overlap strongly.
- Repeated planner-to-landscape transformations encounter ideas already in the archive.
- Additional illustrations of the same applicability or reciprocal-question premise would repeat consequences already analyzed. [S711] [S717] [S718] [S719] [S721]

There is also evidence that changing the source relationship can still produce a different articulation. The latest Atlas case changes the creative seed from rejected planning authority to the asymmetry of host and guest histories, interpreted through an established hospitality theme. Its strongest result is a bounded player-level hypothesis; current gameplay, actual player desirability and incremental graph contribution remain unestablished. [S722]

The records do **not** support a global saturation claim, a measured decline from 30 to 18 to 11 ideas, or a creativity success rate. The passes used different frames and overlapping sources, with no standardized independent comparison. The candidate counts are inventory facts, not productivity measurements. [S711]

### When another lens would be worthwhile

| Lens | What it could add | Condition for meaningful use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Atlas independent question formation | Reveal dependence on the archive's existing categories and omissions | Preserve genuine independence of the initial framing, then compare sources and predecessors |
| Longitudinal question inheritance | Distinguish preserving useful inquiry from preserving obsolete goals | Use an actual source-grounded question trajectory and one consequential changed premise |
| Human judgment and contestability | Examine whether explanation helps the owner challenge the supplied frame | Focus on a specific understanding/decision outcome rather than a new presentation format |
| A materially different architectural relationship | Discover an experience that the familiar source seed does not make imaginable | State what relationship, beneficiary or consequence is different before beginning |
| A different real domain or beneficiary | Reveal conditions and needs absent from self-referential repository examples | Establish target scope, authoritative sources and access to the relevant evidence |

These are alternatives for a future bounded inquiry. Another generic “find more possibilities” pass is not a prerequisite to using the current portfolio.

### A practical stopping rule

For a repeated conceptual inquiry, ask whether the next increment introduces a consequential new premise, changes a beneficiary or desired end, exposes a previously unexamined causal consequence, overturns a comparison, or establishes a genuine gap in the catalogue. If it only renames a method or adds another vignette with the same consequences, retain the result as a variant and stop expanding that example.

This is an analyst's recommendation derived from the archive's local stopping judgments, not a new mandatory runtime stage or numerical novelty test.

### Transformative frontier: preserve outside this common ordering

**Exit / non-capture (RP-Q1/Q2):** potentially valuable where independence from software or absence of captured knowledge is itself the desired end. Its primary beneficiaries and retention commitments need an explicit frame.

An entry question is: **Under what circumstances would successful Sensemaking leave a person less dependent on the software, and which aspects of their experience should remain outside its captured knowledge?** This preserves the difference between helping someone leave and declining to model them.

**Public / material / ecological consequences (RP-R1/R2/R3, RP-T1/T2, RP-U1/U2):** potentially valuable where affected communities, physical dependencies or post-repository inheritors become the relevant subjects.

An entry question is: **Who or what is materially affected by a software decision while absent from its declared users, and what evidence and standing would support an account of those consequences?** Material effects, normative standing and representation of affected interests remain separate questions.

These directions are retained without ranks 7 and 8. A low position under today's owner-and-agent frame would mostly measure a mismatch of evaluation frame, not weak value. They may be explored hypothetically without adopting a new thesis; adopting them as primary product ends would require a separate strategic decision and appropriate domain evidence. [S710] [S712]

## 7. Strategic attractiveness

### The strongest current-thesis proposition

The strategic synthesis in this assessment is **source-grounded prospective reasoning whose judgment improves across sessions**. This combines the distinctive promise of Futures with the accepted destination of Continuity. Truth supports reliable interpretation; Structure supplies ways to discover different experiences; Experience supports the owner's capacity to understand and redirect the work; Plurality can broaden reasoning when real differences in interpretation matter.

This is an interpretation of how the existing directions could cooperate, rather than selection of a new architecture or proof that the combination works. The strategy already values both prospective understanding and persistent stewardship. [STRATEGY] [ADR31] [ADR34]

| Plurality | A useful extension when multiple agents or participants genuinely contribute different understanding | Correlated perspectives, interpretation burden and artificial consensus can eliminate the benefit |
| Exit and Public | Potentially compelling under different beneficiary and success frames | Current repository evidence cannot fairly adjudicate their broader value or required authority |

The first three have particularly strong current-thesis alignment, but alignment is not demonstrated effectiveness. This archive does not support a defensible universal 1–8 strategic-value ranking. Depth is uneven, beneficiary value is mostly hypothetical, and several directions are complementary rather than substitutable.

**The difference from inquiry ranking is intentional:** Experience can deserve the next conceptual investigation while Futures or Continuity remains more central to the overall strategic proposition. Likewise, Truth can be essential while one narrow applicability question has reached local conceptual completion.

## 8. Recommendation and reassessment

### Recommended next intellectual investigation

**Meaningful owner agency in an agent-authored possibility landscape** is this assessment's narrowly preferred next Focused Depth Exploration.

The specific question is:

> When Sensemaking presents a source-grounded landscape of possible product futures, what would let the existing high-delegation owner recognize an omitted end, challenge the assumed beneficiary or revise the question itself, while retaining the benefit of delegating the analysis?

The baseline is already established conceptually: formal authority over selection does not remove the agent's influence over which questions and alternatives become visible. The new work should distinguish the consequences of genuine understanding, mere persuasiveness, ability to transfer judgment to another case, and excessive supervision burden. It should stay with the current owner persona and avoid assuming a new interface or participatory product.

The output would be worthwhile if it changes how the portfolio's outcomes should be understood or reveals a concrete failure of the current framing. Another version of the contributor encounter in #721 would not be sufficient. Actual human comprehension or learning would remain an observation question.

### Conditions that would alter this recommendation

- **Changed owner purpose or a live inheritance failure:** move the longitudinal question inquiry to first.
- **A decisive unobserved condition:** move the observability inquiry upward.
- **An authentic independent creative-use opportunity:** make the incremental-benefit question the first evidence inquiry.
- **A materially different source relationship that exposes a new experience class:** reconsider architectural generativity above the current first two.
- **A newly selected beneficiary or product purpose:** reframe the comparison, including the Exit/Public frontier.
- **Only another synonym, another diagram or another variant of a completed example:** preserve it as a variant; do not reopen the whole portfolio.

This is a dated, revisable ordering. The assessment is complete when the landscape, distinctness decisions, residual questions and comparative rationale are clear. It does not require constructing a mandatory stage pipeline or automatically executing the first-ranked inquiry.
