## 1. Question, method and source boundary

**Question:** Does the source-bounded M01–M18 landscape represent the materially different ways an agent might recognize, diagnose, retain, proportionately revise, or productively evolve a reasoning method or intellectual question, while preserving purpose, evidence and authority? **Purpose of sufficiency:** whether that landscape is broad enough for a *separately warranted* Convergent Evaluation, **not** whether every concept is new, implemented or effective.

Assessment uses the [Possibility Assessment and Prioritization Ontology](../semantic-architecture/possibility-assessment-ontology.md#31-coveragefinding--several-different-kinds-of-covered): corpus coverage, conceptual breadth, depth, empirical support and perspective coverage are separate. Candidate counts establish only the identity of this declared corpus. This review inspected **all eighteen original expressions** and their contrasts; it does not claim complete conceptual coverage of metareasoning in general.

## 4. Comparability, overlap, and what is genuinely distinct

Coverage is not a winner-take-all comparison. Comparing an observability discipline (M16) to an authority constraint (M15) as alternative metareasoning engines would be a category error. Source identities and attributed differences survive every navigational grouping.

| Contrast | Coverage-level relationship |
| --- | --- |
| **M01 / M02** | Related but distinct: **answer relevance** versus **question-origin/provenance**. Neither subsumes the other. |
| **M03 / M18** | **Genuinely different:** competing/replacing an inadequate frame versus a valid answered question yielding an additional consequential question. M04 may supply relational material; M12 preserves the history. |
| **M05 / M06 / M08** | Complementary: causal-diagnostic rivals versus alternative-method audition versus distinguishing an unavailable/misdelivered method from method inadequacy. No symptom proves its cause. |
| **M07 / M17** | Distinct granularity: adjust one method versus alter composition/sequence among legitimate operations. Both reuse existing controller semantics; neither warrants a second controller. |
| **M09 / M10 / M11** | Complementary monitoring, justified stability and economy. More variants or more reflection do not automatically improve fitness. M10 and M11 substantially restate current policy safeguards. |
| **M12 / M13** | Distinct temporal units: retain *one inquiry's* evidence/revision vs retain a *contextual methodological lesson* that might help in another episode. Both rely on existing provenance. |
| **M14 / M15** | Reviewer/critic is a possible perspective source; authority safeguards constrain what that perspective may change. They are not comparable alternatives. |
| **M16 / all reasoning candidates** | M16 evaluates observable work and evidence ceilings; it does not perform M05, M06 or M18. Retrospective explanation cannot prove its own cognitive cause. |

There is **real overlap**, especially M07/M10/M11/M12/M14/M15 with already explicit guidance. The overlap is useful for checking concept boundaries, **not** evidence that these are six missing abstractions. M05, M09 and M18 are comparatively distinctive **application questions** within the already expressible framework, not newly ratified mechanisms.

## 5. Consequential territory remaining unexamined or shallow

1. **Metareasoning during apparently productive work — shallow, not absent.** M01, M02 and M09 ask whether progress answers the governing question; M11 allows reflection at material moments. What would *reliably* expose misorientation before a failure or negative result is unknown. This is better investigated as a discriminating behavioral/depth question than a generic fresh divergent family.
2. **Question value versus question interest — shallow, not absent.** M01, M03, M04, M15 and M18 allow competing/new questions. The new question's incremental decision relevance, opportunity cost and governing-purpose fit remain to be assessed; M18 does not promote it by being intriguing.
3. **Method fitness changing mid-inquiry — represented but not worked through.** M07, M09, M12, M17 and M18 distinguish a method becoming inadequate from earlier evidence becoming invalid. A focused conditional example could expose difficult temporal boundaries without changing the concept space.
4. **Misleading performance indicators — represented but evidentially unqualified.** M09 and M16 distinguish a fluent artifact, source citation and actual progress; there is no validated proxy for intellectual quality and no evidence authorizing a score.
5. **Gradual drift without discrete failure — shallow, not missing.** M02 (question provenance), M09 (purpose-relative progress), M11 (economical activation) and S4 (global–local return) already describe its conceptual region. A later inquiry could study weak signals, false alarms and reflection cost.
6. **Multiple legitimate frames without indecision — represented but shallow.** M03/M10/M11/M12 cover conditional coexistence, continuity and exit. Competing frames must not automatically require a permanent parallel search structure.
7. **Portability and actual effect — outside this conceptual coverage claim.** The available normal-use records and fresh-agent partial qualification do not establish loaded specialist guidance, sustained application, superiority, or reliable read-only compliance. These uncertainties could affect a later *adoption* decision but do not currently demonstrate a missing conceptual family.

**No material uncovered mechanism requires M19+ in this stage.** A neglected empirical perspective or a richer conditional case can be worth pursuing, but that differs from an unrepresented kind of metareasoning. Do not manufacture candidates to fill cells.

## 6. Diminishing returns: comparative judgment without ranking candidates

- **Retrospective contribution:** M01–M18 already cover diagnostic failures, incorrect methods, erroneous questions, joint mismatch, valid-question evolution, appropriate non-revision, learning, composition, authority and observability. Many surface restatements of S6/S7/S9; the useful distinctiveness of M18 and M17 does not depend on novelty of a runtime object.
- **Prospective contribution of another generic divergent pass:** likely *low* for deciding what kind of metareasoning concepts the landscape contains. Additional labels may repeat reframing, diagnostic attribution, proportional revision or economy without altering the coverage disposition.
- **What could change that judgment:** a specifically articulated *new conceptual mechanism* that cannot be described as question/frame revision, competing explanation, method choice/composition, continuity, learning, observability or authority; or an authentic case where those meanings **cannot be preserved** using current documentary/knowledge representations. No such discriminator was evidenced.
- **What could be more useful than generic divergence:** a **separately warranted Convergent Evaluation** that compares the promising *reasoning mechanisms within comparable roles* and treats M15/M16/constraints as cross-cutting requirements; or later focused depth on productive-looking misorientation versus valid question evolution. A future authentic-use inquiry would have a different empirical purpose and need its own warrant.

**Conclusion:** conceptual breadth is sufficient for the named purpose; it is **not exhaustive coverage**, mechanistic depth sufficiency, proof of agent competence, or approval for Stage 3.

## 7. Disposition, handoff, and stopping rule

**Disposition: SUFFICIENT FOR CONVERGENT EVALUATION.** The represented corpus offers enough substantively different mechanisms and safeguards to compare purpose-relative intellectual approaches without a further broad divergent pass. The consequential open territory is within the mechanisms (especially M05, M09, M17, M18), not a demonstrated missing ontological dimension.

Stop now after documenting the coverage judgment. A later Stage 3 or depth inquiry requires a separately warranted task.

### Governing principles

1. **It is not the number of mechanisms that proves coverage; it is the materially different intellectual responsibilities they represent.**
2. **It is not a missing document and it is not an unimplemented controller that proves a semantic gap; it is a consequential meaning the existing model cannot preserve.**
3. **A successful inquiry that discovers another question is actually productive intellectual evolution, not necessarily correction of a failed method.**
