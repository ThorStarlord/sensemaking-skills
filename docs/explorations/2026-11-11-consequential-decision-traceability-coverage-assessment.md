## 1. Question, assessment frame and method

**Governing question:** Is the existing landscape broad and differentiated enough to compare plausible ways for Sensemaking to make consequential autonomous engineering decisions reconstructible, challengeable and reusable **while also addressing the separate problem of whether better responsibilities were discovered and chosen**?

The original motivating diagnostic is sharper: **Why did a coding agent select a local repair over a possibly more valuable repository-level responsibility?** A case in which the repair was the better warranted choice remains a live rival explanation. No original historical error or general Local Completion Bias is assumed.

The governed product is ontology-grounded repository understanding and prospective reasoning: [product strategy](../product-strategy.md), [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-current-product-boundary.md), [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md), [ADR 0034](../adr/0034-ontology-first-adaptive-sensemaking.md). Preserve four-level authority, agent-owned selection, source identity and the current PATH-1 strategy. This inquiry does not reopen them.

**Assessment operands:** original source-qualified **CandidateExpressions** C01–C19, plus their declared roles and relationships, **not** 19 independent ratified product possibilities or implementation tickets. The inspected corpus includes the original report and receipt, current ontology, possibility-assessment and selection guidance, available runtime consumers and PR #812's historical source. The original report's source and assessment revision are separately recorded.

**Method and criteria:** Follow [Possibility Assessment v1](../../skills/strategic-repository-analysis/references/possibility-assessment-v1.md) and its [ontology](../semantic-architecture/possibility-assessment-ontology.md). Judge (1) declared corpus coverage; (2) conceptual breadth of **ends, beneficiaries, mechanisms and frames**; (3) consequence and rival-explanation depth; (4) empirical and operational support; and (5) perspective/source-access coverage. Compare source identities without silently consolidating them. A sufficient landscape must expose materially different possible ways of solving the *named* problem, their important conflicts, and questions that could change a later choice. It need not be exhaustive or proven useful. Neither item counts nor a numeric coverage score decide sufficiency.

**Classification in this document:** “Adequately represented” refers to conceptual coverage for the next comparison, **not** implementation. “Conceptually shallow” means the mechanism/end exists but its consequences or discriminators need depth. “Operationally unqualified” means actual producer, consumer, host or benefit evidence is absent/partial. “Materially missing” would require a new outcome/mechanism whose omission could change the eventual choice. “Outside scope” identifies a tempting but unjustified expansion. A row can have adequate concepts and unqualified real execution.

## 5. Overlaps, complementarity, dependencies and incompatibilities

1. **C01/C02/C03/C04 — shared accountable choice, different operational investments.** C01 is low-ceremony composition, C02 requires original decision-time authorship, C03 grants a documentary reference identity, C04 adds conditional machine querying. C01/C03 partly overlap in retained fields, but pre-action timing and machine consumers are independent requirements. C04 has no demonstrated missing consumer that forces schema expansion. They compete on complexity when intended to solve the same reader problem; they can also compose.
2. **C05/C06/C07/C08 — chronology, interpretation and observability are not aliases.** C05 observes repository history; C06 interprets expected vs observed consequences using actual original expectations; C07 references host events *when supplied*; C08 favors sparse, explicit noncapture. C05 does not require C07 on hosts without telemetry. Even all four together cannot show hidden model cognition.
3. **C09/C10/C11 — three failure diagnoses.** A better alternative absent from the recorded set (C09), inadequate downstream responsibility scope (C10), and unacceptable opportunity cost (C11) can be independent. A good local repair could pass C10 yet have substantial C11 opportunity cost; a bad selection with adequate options is not a C09 failure. A retrospective invented alternative is not an original observed candidate.
4. **C12/C13/C14/C15 — history, retrieval and actual application differ.** C12 preserves dated deferral/reselection; C13 follows executed choice into assumption/lesson update; C14 retrieves relevant claims before commitment; C15 makes the claimed inferential role explicit. A chain from C12 to C14 to C15 is a **possible composition**, not proof the later agent learned. A reviewed record can remain unused.
5. **C16/C17 — conditional same-agent deliberation vs independently supplied perspective.** Both could improve option discovery, but neither is just an audit log. They may be substitutes under a tight cost budget, complements when stakes/novelty warrant external challenge, or inappropriate when a bounded repair is already well evidenced. A critic never becomes a second decision authority automatically.
6. **C18/C19 and C05/C07/C08 — contestability need not mean log maximization.** A reviewer may need a few challengeable source-linked claims rather than a large transcript. With confidential hosts, deliberate nonretention may make a complete trace impossible. Honest source-coverage disclosures are a product property, not a mechanically repairable missing event.
7. **Whole-system tension:** accountability surfaces can yield persuasive post-hoc text without changing prospective decisions. Conversely, genuine new opportunity discovery can produce a better selection without sufficient evidence for external review. This is a comparison among different **ends**, not a conflict that mandates a unified product architecture.

These are an attributed, **purpose-relative ConsolidatedView** under the existing assessment ontology. Shared infrastructure, possible dependency and conditional competition are reported as such; no identity or schema equivalence is asserted.

## 6. Does the landscape investigate the original local-versus-global choice?

A useful diagnosis must separate **evidence unavailable at decision time**, **knowledge not retrieved**, **knowledge misinterpreted or not applied**, **option-set failure**, **comparison-criteria failure**, **local-completion pressure**, **justified local work**, and **unassessable past choice**. A detailed rationale alone cannot discriminate all of these.

| Rival explanation / outcome | Candidate mechanisms bearing on it | Decision-relevant evidence needed | What cannot be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Governing objective never retrieved | C14, C07–C08; C01–C02 | Original source-access record and a known-complete host/query event boundary, plus the governing source at the historical SHA. | Available strategy text alone proves neither retrieval nor nonretrieval. |
| Objective retrieved but misinterpreted | C15, C16–C18 | Pre-choice source-grounded paraphrase/assumptions and independent source review against the original objective. | A later fluent interpretation cannot establish the original cognition. |
| Objective understood but not applied | C15, C16; C11 | Time-grounded original selection warrant showing which objective affected the options, with contrasting observed action and source constraints. | Cited goals are authored testimony; causal influence is not directly observable. |
| Option discovery too narrow | C09, C16–C17; C14 | Original recorded choice set and a source-pinned challenger finding a credible omitted option available then. | Later discovery cannot prove the option was unconsidered internally. |
| Adequate options, weak comparison | C03, C11, C15–C16 | Original candidate warrants, expected consequences, criteria, defeaters, nonselected alternatives and authority. | Adequacy and quality require purpose-relative judgment, not a numeric regret index. |
| Preference for easy verification | C11, C16–C17; C06 | Multiple authentic selections with alternative values/costs and observed selection behavior, holding the evidence ceiling explicit. | One PR does not diagnose a systematic preference or policy effect. |
| Appropriate local safety repair | C10–C11, C15; C05–C06 | Real defect/source risk, affected consumers, acceptable downside/opportunity cost and no decisive contradictory evidence. | Correct local value does not prove global optimality or exclude higher-value opportunities. |
| Choice assessable but original rationale thin | C01–C05, C08, C18 | Actually retained pre-action notes and source/chronology links where present; disclose unrecorded dimensions. | Post-action PR prose does not travel back in time. |
| Evidence insufficient to discriminate causes | C05, C08–C09, C18–C19 | Explicit NOT_ASSESSED/host-coverage report and narrower questions that genuinely can be answered. | Unknown is a valid outcome, not an inferred Local Completion Bias diagnosis. |

**Sufficiency judgment:** the landscape covers the alternative *classes of failure* and offers plausible investigative responsibilities, including a legitimate **no error** conclusion. C09 and C17 could challenge a narrow set, but cannot independently prove that the original agent did not think of a missing candidate. C15 could inspect an authored knowledge-to-choice account, but does not turn declared relevance into observed causal influence. C16 could change the agent's prospective decision but has no demonstrated comparative benefit. The **same observed local repair** can remain compatible with several rival explanations because the historical evidence is incomplete. That is an epistemic limit, not a novel ontology concept or a reason to invent a compulsory reasoning diary.

**Shallow, but not missing:** an explicit diagnostic comparison between **misinterpretation** and **correct interpretation but nonapplication** (and between **narrow discovery** and **weak comparison**) would need more source-anchored episode depth. The conceptual carriers already exist in C14/C15/C16/C17 and Convergent/Warrant guidance; this can be a distinct *depth/comparison question* without a tenth candidate family or any claim that a model's private interpretation is observable.
