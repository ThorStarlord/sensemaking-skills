## 7. Discovery failure vs comparison failure: the decision-changing fork

**Case A — inadequate discovery but adequate comparison:** The original selector gives an appropriate conditional ranking to candidates it actually considered. A **material alternative** that already had a source-backed identity, feasible authority and a credible benefit over the selected set was not in the **recorded** candidate set. A later source-pinned critic may establish a *retrospective candidate-set challenge*; absent a complete original event boundary, it cannot prove the agent privately failed to think of that option. A prospective conditional source discovery mechanism could change this by placing the missing option in the actual **next** choice frame.

**Case B — adequate discovery but inadequate comparison:** The original candidate set is credibly sufficient and recorded, but the agent applies a stale product premise, a wrong system invariant, unwarranted certainty, ignored dependencies or misleading ease-of-verification criterion, and chooses badly. Here **more discovery may be wasteful**; source-specific comparative warrant, assumption checking or an independent reviewer on the original criteria is the relevant method. [Warrant/Choice](../../skills/using-sensemaking/references/warrant-choice-policy-v0.md) already supplies this vocabulary, though correct application must be established in use.

**Necessary discriminator:** a bounded original `ConsideredChoiceSet` **as-authored at decision time**, with each candidate's source identity, decisive warrant, authority/feasibility, claimed tradeoff and expressed omitted scope, plus a time-qualified independent reviewer who can distinguish real missing alternatives from post-hoc inventions. A complete internal thought inventory is neither available nor required for a **recorded-set adequacy** judgment. For true agent-cognition diagnosis, the conclusion can still be `NOT_ASSESSED`.

**What counts as a consequential alternative?** It must (a) serve the governing outcome under same contemporary authority/time frame; (b) be discoverable from contemporaneous source without later facts; (c) offer a nontrivial different beneficiary, result or scope; (d) be sufficiently supported to survive an initial warrant check; and (e) have plausible difference in expected product contribution/omission cost. An aesthetically attractive new feature, a large rewrite without a consumer, or an out-of-authority owner thesis is not by itself a credible omitted candidate.

**Stop rule for exploration:** investigate a plausible alternative only while it could materially alter comparative selection, its missing premise could be cheaply/source-groundedly resolved, and the expected benefit of more insight justifies attention cost. When the original local action is source-supported, low downside and no currently credible broader option is evident, **stop and build**. This follows existing conditional scope guidance rather than adding a fixed quota, global enumeration score, mandatory strategy reanalysis or a universal counterfactual.

**False negative cost:** wrong narrow selection can defer an important capability, conceal a consumer/invariant and make downstream work repeat. **False positive cost:** an ever-present speculative alternative can absorb attention, delay safe useful fixes, raise cost and substitute abstract strategy for engineering value. These costs are qualitatively real but unmeasured in #812.

## 9. Consequence-depth analysis: causal bridges and meaningful forks

### Chain I — Relevant source retrieval can change a selection, but might not

**Hypothetical mechanism:** a consequential choice approaches commitment → source-sensitive RECALL retrieves an accepted product constraint/previously deferred opportunity **at the original source revision** → the active agent correctly verifies its scope and applicability → it discovers another warranted responsibility or defeats the current one → its comparison and choice **change** → implemented work contributes better to the product.

**First grounded links:** current queries/Knowledge/Steward can expose tracked reviewed claims and sources; Warrant/Choice supports defeaters, comparison and deferral; agent judgment is explicitly the consumer. **Unproven links:** query actually invoked before choice; correct interpretation; an alternate candidate newly found; change of the original warrant; alternate engineering outcome; downstream superior value.

**Counterfactual forks:**
- Retrieved source was already known or redundant with Git/PR; **no incremental learning**.
- Retrieved source was accurate but irrelevant to this scope; the local safety fix rightly remains selected.
- Agent interprets a true claim outside its conditions; a citation makes a **worse** choice more persuasive.
- Retrieved source exposes a credible higher-value responsibility, but it is **owner-reserved**, too risky or unsupported; appropriate disposition is defer/escalate.
- Source might change the agent's reasoning while the original evidence record remains thin; a better decision would still be hard to audit.

**Consequence boundary:** increasing retrieval hits or writing a query receipt alone does not establish better selection. The useful outcome is a **source-qualified material change (or justified nonchange)** to a live warrant.

### Chain II — Bounded candidate discovery might reveal missing product value or displace good construction

**Conditional mechanism:** a local defect is identified → a source-grounded question about governing outcome and downstream consumers raises one plausible alternative scope → agent checks whether that alternative is credible, source-current, within authority and decision-changing → compares it with local fix and no-change → commits to a warranted scope.

**Positive branch:** a previously unseen cross-component invariant or product user outcome is discovered; selecting that broader responsibility avoids repeat local patches and produces retained value. **Negative branch:** a speculative strategic avenue consumes context and delays a concrete security-relevant fix with no better evidence. **Null branch:** a credible broader alternative is considered and correctly rejected—the discovery mechanism may still improve **justification**, but not necessarily the selected action.

**Required bridging premises:** relevant source/consumer was accessible, agent was allowed to explore it, it survives Warrant/Choice, and its incremental value exceeds the local action's expected contribution plus search cost. PR #812 does not qualify these premises for the actual historical choice.

### Chain III — Better original records may improve reviewer decisions without improving the original choice

**Conditional mechanism:** author records an attributed original source and option/warrant statement **before code** → Git retains its relative time → PR/CI later document actual work → future reviewer can distinguish original claims, observed events and subsequent interpretation → reviewer challenges unsupported assumptions or revises **future** policy/engineering decisions.

**Positive branch:** an independently useful owner/reviewer can expose a false premise, wrong source applicability or missed downstream contract and the next selection improves. **Negative branch:** longer records become polished rationalization; critical omitted options remain missing, and time cost rises. **Privacy branch:** source/host logs are unavailable or sensitive; explicit limited evidence gives less detail but higher integrity. **No behavior branch:** the reviewer understands more, yet nothing demonstrates changed agent selection.

**Required premises:** genuine prior authorship; readable source identities; consumer use; factual review; effect on later choice. Git commit ancestry only supports the timing and code segments, not all later links. Full telemetry and a machine schema are **not** prerequisites.

### Chain IV — Cross-session memory produces only potential, not established compounding returns

**Conditional mechanism:** earlier action creates a reviewed `decision`/`alternative`/`lesson` record → later fresh agent retrieves and verifies it at updated source → prior deferral/outcome becomes relevant → current warrant or opportunity set differs from Git/PR-only reasoning → improved bounded work.

**Existing counterevidence to an easy claim:** [Frontier Challenge 04](../normal-use/2026-11-10-frontier-04-semantic-continuity.md) established independent-context engineering continuity through Git/PR, but **record-derived decision benefit was NOT DEMONSTRATED** and superiority over source-only re-entry was **NOT TESTED**. Therefore the chain's critical *distinct contribution of the reviewed record* is missing, not proved by successful session re-entry.

**Consequence stop:** additional fifth/sixth-order speculative benefits—greater organizational intelligence, automatic optimality or a new knowledge-runtime layer—would not change today's evidence or authorization. No new chain depth is warranted without actual independent adoption/consumer evidence.
