## 2. Equal-level directions and six qualitative lenses

**B: Longitudinal Engineering Judgment (historical rank 3).** Intended outcome: a later coding agent or owner retains **why** an earlier decision was made, notices its assumptions changed, reconstructs alternatives, and can inherit a valuable *question* rather than canonizing an old answer. Origins: **DE-D2/D3, DE-E3, DE-F3, NX-L3, NX-M1/M3, NX-P3**; near curated records include `poss:persistent-semantic-stewardship`, `poss:possibility-memory`, `poss:strategic-continuity-reassessment`, `poss:repository-local-prospective-decision-memory` and `poss:possibility-reconsideration-from-change`. Selected semantic stewardship does not prove longitudinal user benefit.

**A: Architectural Possibility Generation (historical rank 5).** Intended outcome: reinterpret **source-qualified capabilities, constraints, rejected alternatives and conditional consequences** as material for imagining **different classes of experiences and product ends**, not just better means for an accepted goal. Origins: **GD-A/B/G, DE-C1/C2/C3, DE-D1, DE-H2, DE-I1/I2/I3, DE-J1/J2/J3**, plus related **DE-G2**. The [original Convergent Evaluation](2026-11-03-convergent-accumulated-creative-possibilities.md#expand-the-kinds-of-architectures-and-experiences-a-repository-makes-imaginable) already cautioned that many creative operations are *means*, not independently valuable products. This **locally revises which of these two inquiries to consider first**, without declaring A the better eventual product.

## 3. A specific architecture relationship to investigate later

**Source seed — a rejected *proposal*, not a reusable implementation:** [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-current-product-boundary.md#outside-current-product-scope) forbids a deterministic `StrategicPlanner` or `OuterLoopEngine`, automatic strategy ranking and task selection. The [curated Idea Library](../idea-library.md) retains `poss:automatic-strategic-planner` and `poss:ranked-possibility-backlog` as **REJECTED_FOR_NOW** due to strategic incompatibility. This does **not** imply executable planner code exists, that the owner wants it, or that the rejection has expired.

**Source seed — legitimate complementary reasoning:** The [Prospective Reasoning Ontology](../semantic-architecture/prospective-reasoning-ontology.md#6-relationships) describes **conceptual, conditional** relations such as `ArchitectureOption → mayProduce → ConsequenceHypothesis → mayEnable → ProductPossibility`, with preferences distinct from decisions and actual implementation. [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-persistent-semantic-stewardship.md) and [ADR 0034](../adr/0034-ontology-first-adaptive-sensemaking.md) support remembering alternatives and creatively interpreting them under active-agent/owner authority; **no automatic generator or tested graph-causal inference is established**.

**Creative lineage, not novel discovery:** **GD-B** (exaptation of declined architectures), **GD-G** (generative optionality), **DE-I2** (pattern fission), **DE-C2** (contract inversion), **DE-H2** (conceptual metamorphosis), **DE-J1/J3** (new experiences from structural affordances and optionality). The [Generative Depth companion](2026-11-02-generative-depth-repository-knowledge.md) *already* imagined using the desire for orientation/continuity behind a rejected planner as material for owner-directed experiences. The [Atlas Midgard normal-use record](../normal-use/prospective-reasoning-atlas-001.md) is a counterexample: source-grounded architecture options and consequential design improvements did **not** discover a genuinely new product possibility.

**One bounded hypothetical relationship to explore:** separate the **organizing/comparative purpose** behind the *rejected* planner/ranked backlog from the **prohibited automatic authority** it would assume. Ask what *different owner or coding-agent experiences* could arise if architectural alternatives and consequences become **creative material** for questioning desired futures, not instructions about which task to execute. The source relationship is **rejected automatic selection ↔ accepted attributable architecture–consequence–possibility reasoning with owner authority**. It is conceptual *fission*, not a proposal for a new ranking engine, UI, database, architecture design or autonomous planner.

**Beneficiary:** high-delegation repository owner, supported by an active coding agent. The research would need to distinguish four possible claims instead of jumping from one to the last: **(1)** new implementation of a known goal, **(2)** materially different architecture/experience, **(3)** new valuable capability, **(4)** previously unarticulated desired product end. #4 requires beneficiary, changed desirable experience and source-faithful novelty relative to `poss:assumption-aware-opportunity-ecology`, `poss:cross-episode-possibility-synthesis` and existing agent-native architectural synthesis; it is **not established** here.

**What a later Depth Exploration could newly reveal:** direct changes in the *questions the owner can ask*; second-order changes in what experiences or new product goals become thinkable; systemic effects on optionality, anchoring, owner burden, false feasibility and authority drift. Unexpected harms could include silently rebuilding the rejected planner under a more appealing name or mistaking a conceptually traversable link for evidence of user desire. Beneficial and harmful consequences both matter; the proposed relationship is neither technically validated nor strategically selected.

### Exactly one independently executable future Depth Exploration question — NOT RUN

> **Suppose a coding agent creatively repurposes the organizing/comparative aspiration behind Sensemaking's rejected automatic strategic planner and ranked possibility backlog, while preserving the explicit prohibition on automatic selection. When it interprets retained source-grounded architecture alternatives, constraints and hypothetical consequences as material for imagining futures, what qualitatively different owner/user experiences or desired product ends could follow?** Trace direct, second-order, systemic and unexpected beneficial/harmful consequences of this premise, including changes to creative optionality, owner judgment and authority; test conditional forks where a proposed experience is only a renamed Idea Library view, or where an equally capable source-only agent could generate it; distinguish mechanisms, architectures, capabilities and genuinely new goals. **Do not build or select anything.**

The question can produce an honest **RESTATED / REVISED / NOT_ASSESSED** result. Such a negative intellectual result would be useful: it would show the apparent architectural generativity is a known goal in a different costume, not warrant automatic further invention.
