# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Offensive or insulting language
- Attacks on personal characteristics
- Unwelcome attention
- Any other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported to project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly.

Retaliation for reporting violations is not tolerated.
