## 3. Reassessed frontier and construction choice

**Strategic Decision to Support:** How can a user delegate sustained valuable product development such that a fresh agent enters the correct cognitive environment, uses and enriches adequate semantic understanding, re-warrants directions, adapts its intellectual methods and retains consequential experience economically?

Credible construction paths: (A) position the product differently without altering the operating experience; low implementation cost but leaves integration gap. (B) integrate the current entry, stewardship, strategic continuation and learning responsibilities into a concrete sustained-development lifecycle and then repair the most consequential actual consumer barrier; high direct relevance without new machinery. (C) introduce a new dedicated strategic runtime/Skill suite; greater design freedom but duplicates controller and is not warranted by current evidence. (D) focus only on additional narrow retrieval/validation mechanisms; potentially useful locally, but insufficient to realize the complete experience.

**Selected path:** B. The first bounded responsibility is the accepted thesis, current documentation/entrypoint and knowledge reconciliation in this change. The next independently warranted responsibility is Episode 10C: reliable cognitive entry, integrated semantic stewardship and task-relevant continuity, driven by actual beneficiary barriers. Determine existing support before adding runtime code. Prefer a real entry/re-entry consumer path with verified identity and accessible existing receipt/knowledge tooling; establish a useful end-to-end experience rather than an administrative platform. A later authorized beneficiary session should perform worthwhile work with explicit knowledge-edit authority, recover prior knowledge, reconcile consequential changes, and report actual contributions/limits. Do not alter beneficiary repositories through this framework mission.

**Evidence ceiling:** this change integrates an owner decision, source-reviewed operating guidance and a reviewed goal projection. It does not execute a beneficiary session, validate a receipt mechanically, show improved cognitive performance, or qualify a release.
