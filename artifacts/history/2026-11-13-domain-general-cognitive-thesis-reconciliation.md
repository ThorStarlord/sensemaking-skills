## 4. Reassessed strategic frontier

**Strategic Decision to Support:** Which authentic agent-facing cognitive capability or qualification would improve later consequential judgment beyond ordinary retained facts and separate Skills?

Material open boundaries include: (1) actual decision-time use of retained experience, (2) better future cognitive-method selection and correction, (3) cost-adjusted contribution of functional integration, and (4) careful reuse across different agents/domains without inherited authority.

**Selected Level-3 continuation:** preserve PATH-1 as a worthwhile *engineering specialization* when genuine work needs it, with explicit distinction between knowledge coverage and improved cognition. A first credible qualification direction is a small independent, matched multi-episode comparison of experience-informed method adaptation with fixed methods, later extending to component isolation and other domains only when warranted. This is a research direction, not a mandated experiment, predetermined backlog, or automatic change to source code.

**Current bounded responsibility:** integrate the revised Level-4 product thesis, maintain exact historical source authority, reconcile stale current product statements and record this Level-3 effect. This documentation responsibility is terminal when source/diff review and integration complete.

**Strategic effect:** REVISE_STRATEGY (new product purpose, beneficiary and success measures); CONTINUE existing warranted software-domain work under new purpose; no invented Level-3 full exploration funnel or new product runtime.
